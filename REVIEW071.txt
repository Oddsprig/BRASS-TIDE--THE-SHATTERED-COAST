No new framework. No economy, save, AI or performance work in this seat.

Pass only when every item below holds. A still is not motion. A codec check is not a heard mix.

1. Routing
- A reported fire, hit, miss, engine failure or sinking keeps that kind. A miss or a muzzle report is not turned into a hit. An unknown kind adds no sound layer.
- MG, cannon, rocket and torpedo fires use distinct recorded banks: gun, cannon, rocket, splash. Hits, misses, engine trouble and sinking use only gun, cannon, rocket, splash, wood or metal. Engine trouble uses metal. Sinking uses splash plus a second layer.
- CombatArt.effect draws a fresh reported effect and draws nothing for an empty or expired one.

2. Budget
- Step, event-ring, effect and wreck caps drop overflow. They do not grow without a bound.
- One Sound.consume admits at most EVENT_VOICE_LIMIT layers, accents included. Accents do not get a second cap. The voice pool stays VOICE_LIMIT. Events past audible radius are dropped. With audio disabled, sequence still advances and nothing is scheduled.
- A wake draw stays within MAX_PRIMITIVES.

3. Sequence
- id, seq and sequence advance together. pop_events drains the queue once.
- Sound ignores a seq at or below the last admitted seq, including a lower seq that arrives later in the same batch.
- Voices may play in priority order. Accent layers are scheduled after that event's primary and share the same admission cap.

4. Mounts
- emit_fire keeps the muzzle it was given, plus that mount's id and index. The mount dictionary is not rewritten. The per-mount shot time is the battle time.
- The drawn muzzle or impact sits on that reported point, nearer to it than to a distant decoy.

5. Speed and stop
- A stern path that actually travelled paints a bounded wash. A stopped stern and samples older than wake life paint nothing. Drawing does not change the samples.
- The wake producer appends only when the hull center travels. Zero speed, turning in place and a sunk hull do not append and do not refresh an existing sample time. Old samples expire where they were left.

6. Worker-safe
- Pose preparation interpolates x, y, angle and turretAngle on a copy. The published ship, including nested mounts, stays byte-identical. Editing the copy's scalars does not write the live ship.
- v0.070 uses Dictionary.duplicate() without deep copy. Nested mounts, spec and condition objects are still the published instances. Draw and audio must not write those objects.
- Effect drawing, wake drawing and sound admission do not mutate the dictionaries they read.

7. Outside this fixture
- No polygon golden masters, roster crawl, battle loop, economy, save or performance pass.
- This script does not prove on-screen motion or a heard mix. Root reviews rendered motion and offline sound separately.
- No new production symbols. Callers stay on the existing CombatEvents, CombatArt.effect, Sound.event_layers / consume / attenuation_db, wake draw, Chart wake update and Chart pose preparation. Publish a one-line delta before changing those names, caps or the six bank ids.
