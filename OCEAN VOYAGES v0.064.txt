BRASS TIDE v0.064 — OCEAN VOYAGES

Shatter Coast now connects to Glasswind Keys through an open-ocean chart.
Glasswind Keys is one remote group with three islands. Exploration rewards,
distant jobs and additional island groups are planned in later versions.

Open Ocean approaches from the chart, or use the approach controls offshore.
Chart a course to a north or south approach and sail there. Depart into open
ocean, chart the other group's approach, then enter its coastal chart.
Use the same steps to return. WASD pans; Home centers; scroll zooms.

Travel uses your actual fuel, provisions, wages and campaign time. Your fleet,
crew, cargo, equipment and coins stay with you. Saving retains the active chart
and route. Shatter Coast ports and coastal work remain in Shatter Coast.
The home economy and independent home fleets continue while you sail elsewhere.

Resolve immediate threats and bring back shore parties and deployed gear before
departing. Loaded coastal passenger commitments must be completed first.
No new ocean combat or weather system is introduced in this release.
