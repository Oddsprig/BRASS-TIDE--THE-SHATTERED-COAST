# Brass Tide v0.007 — Engines and battle damage

- Physical stern machinery: transom-mounted outboards on small craft and marine drives on larger hulls, with cowlings, brackets, lower legs, vents, exhaust and propeller housings. Equipment remains separate from interchangeable weapon sockets.
- Local impact marks follow the boat's heading. Open seams, engine smoke and layered deck fires communicate damage without animated crew or water simulation. Repaired hulls hide old scars; old saves remain supported.
- Stronger material impacts, muzzle flashes, explosive bursts, water plumes and staged sinking. Flooding and destruction use different feedback.
- The final wreck and explosion finish after victory. Ship movement, ammunition, repairs, battle time, randomness and payouts stay frozen while the effects finish. Pause and Escape still stop the animation.
- Short background animation captures and focused feedback tests support future art work. Prior versions, the finite coin economy, fitting rules and owner music remain intact.

Source and portable verification are recorded in docs/TEST RESULTS v0.007.md and the exported RELEASE.json. Visual previews use captured game drawing calls; they are controlled fixtures, not a GPU frame-rate measurement.
