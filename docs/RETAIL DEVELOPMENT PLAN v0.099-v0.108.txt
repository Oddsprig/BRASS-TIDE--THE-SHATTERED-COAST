CURRENT POSITION
v0.098 is the latest installed, scoped Windows build. It contains substantial
fleet, campaign and economic systems, but it is not yet accepted as a sellable
finished game. The next ten work packages are in docs/roadmap/SELLABLE RELEASE
MAP v0.099-v0.108.txt. This task prepared that plan, a local store page and
retained screenshot references. It did not implement v0.099 or change the
installed executable, desktop launcher, saves, settings or owner music.

Product: a single-player motorboat fleet game for Windows PC, with local offline
play, keyboard/mouse controls, 36 existing hull designs, interchangeable physical
weapon mounts, crew damage control, finite bronze coins and goods, local island
charts and the Open Seas connecting generated island groups. A 300-point battle
deployment cap is separate from fleet ownership. No Android or multiplayer
promise. Price is fixed at $9.99 USD. Early Access versus a full-release label
has not been selected; the present listing is an in-development draft.

PERFORMANCE AND THREADING CONTRACT
The game already runs authoritative combat at 30 Hz on detached worker data,
with staggered role/target decisions. Godot's nominal 60 Hz callback is not a
requirement to recalculate fleet goals or scan every target 60 times per second.
Keep commands and revisions authoritative; interpolate visible movement apart
from decision cadence. Main owns SceneTree/UI/resources/draw/audio submission.
Worker tasks own copied data/RNG and publish after completion; retire/join them
before reconciling original boat/cargo owners. Do not move Godot resources into
arbitrary workers or solve one backlog by increasing contention.

Lock a representative populated campaign and light/mixed 300-point battles
before optimizing. Record CPU/GPU/driver, RAM/VRAM, Windows, resolution, settings,
speed, active entities, frames and actual simulation progress. Compare matched
workloads and relevant source dependencies. Unmatched old results establish a
gap, not an attributable before/after gain. Profile simulation, targeting,
planning, copies, application, draw calls and caches separately.

Proposed release acceptance to pin before implementation:
  - Combat worker P95 <=33.333 ms; maximum simulation lag <=100 ms.
  - Main publication P95 <=6 ms; no recurring catch-up/deadlock.
  - >=95% of active rendered frames <=20 ms on the declared tested configuration.
  - Aim for a steady 60 FPS; report raw FPS and recurring >50 ms hitches openly.
    Historical strict >=60 results stay historical; do not round or relabel them.
  - Command/ordinary control to visible acknowledgement P95 <=200 ms.
  - Populated travel, daily publication and normal harbor use remain responsive.
These are acceptance targets, not achieved results or published minimum specs.
If a measured case fails, fix its demonstrated cause or retain HOLD. No hidden
smaller fleets, slower world, missing weapons or free transactions to pass.
Safe rendering mode 1 remains requested. Separate mode previously had native
resource failures and is not a release shortcut. Cosmetic quality options may
reduce measured visual expense without removing important damage information;
they cannot be presented as resolving authoritative simulation lag.

EARNED GAMEPLAY AND ECONOMIC PROOF
Six roles are reached through the common ordinary start and paid acquisition;
six new starting classes are not required. Each reference records discoverable
work, starting capital/boat, payer/buyer, trip time, gross receipts, wages,
food/fuel/ammunition, replenishment, repairs, fees, losses and return reserve.
Keep 100 coins per sailor-day as the base and show any actual specialist/crew
differences. Separate capital expenditure and escrow from operating profit.

Explorer/merchant use 102 and the completed 106 expedition. Local fisher and
passenger operator use 103; fuel transport is an existing merchant application.
Escort/privateer use 104. At the final reconciliation, each repeatable role
needs a successful paid return and an affordable second departure; where
repeatability is the issue, retain two settled outings. Risky careers must offer
recoverable choices and truthful losses, not a guaranteed profit on every fight.
Known alternative opportunities must be discoverable on declared seeds without
selecting a favorable seed after a failure. No global statistical balance study.

Reuse real earned checkpoint saves to avoid replaying settled history. Preserve
their source/state hashes and origin. Do not inject position, cargo, cash,
reputation or a target and call it ordinary play. A controlled negative/failure
case stays labeled controlled. No target, no buyer or unfunded demand is a GAP.
No live omniscient prices or enemy cargo finder. Opportunity and reserve advice
use information the captain actually knows, including its age.

The opening design should let a new captain prepare in roughly the first ten
minutes, do useful work in the next twenty and return/reinvest within an hour.
Those are design goals, not current measurements or authorization for a long
test. Automated receipts cannot establish enjoyment or a stranger's understanding.
Record attributed player feedback when available and use the owner's normal
play feedback without taking control of their PC. No hour-long autoplay/soak.
