BRASS TIDE v0.055 - COASTAL FLEETS AND LOCAL INTELLIGENCE

Open Coastal traffic from Voyage jobs & contacts to read your lookout reports.
A current nearby living fleet can be hailed. Old intelligence keeps its observed
purpose and destination; it does not track a distant captain. Reacquire it nearby
before hailing. Witnessed outcomes are dated; unseen fights are not revealed.

Pirates seek locally observed loaded carriers. Patrols intercept hostile raiders
or screen friendly loaded convoys. A brief lost contact is pursued at its last
known position; expired intelligence ends that pursuit. Damaged or poorly
provisioned fleets return for paid recovery instead of pursuing indefinitely.
NPC decisions are staggered; distant fights use bounded salvos and real stores.

Prizes move actual cargo between surviving holds. Patrols can recover stolen
goods from a raider. Repair consumes kits, materials, labor and coins; replacement
uses actual stock or a funded yard commission and available trained recruits.
Lost hull equipment and recoverable cargo remain with the sea wreck before any
replacement relaunches. Crew remain numerical, water remains inexpensive.

This version improves the existing campaign systems. Full-game art, balance,
performance and play quality remain later roadmap work. Exact scoped checks and
actual exported-view evidence are recorded in tests/ and the current build notes.
