BRASS TIDE v0.036 - WORKING CIVILIAN VOYAGES

Open Voyage jobs & contacts with K. The Work board and Fishing tabs use the
boats, people, stores and purses already in your campaign.

FISHING
Buy or commission one of the three fishing hulls. Hire sailors, carry fuel
and food, and leave room for the catch. Sail clear of the harbor, stop in
safe open water, then choose Fishing, the boat, working days and sailors.
Begin fishing returns to the chart. Unpaused campaign time advances work;
waiting at the dock earns no catch. Changing course, leaving the work site
or losing the assigned boat stops its fishing assignment. Recall is allowed.
Catch comes from a shared, bounded water-cell resource quota. Crew time,
fuel and rations are consumed; wages remain part of normal campaign payroll.
Sell landed preserved fish through the existing harbor market. Buyers need
actual coins and hold space remains finite. No sale or profit is guaranteed.

FREIGHT AND FUEL
Existing sealed freight jobs remain alongside the story contract, up to six
compatible commitments. Source stock and passage fees must exist first.
Fuel delivery offers name a real needy fleet and receiving harbor. Choose
an active transport or tanker with free hold. Sealed fuel belongs to that
carrier until a real receipt, loss or harbor unloading; it is not extra
player tank fuel. The named recipient must be present and still need it.
The sponsoring treasury funds materials and the passage fee. Delivery moves
the same fuel into the recipient's custody and pays the reserved fee once.

PASSENGERS
Funded charters use a real transport or yacht. Existing crew, captives and
other passengers occupy berths. The source supplies actual passengers and
provisions; their carried savings stay separately accounted for in escrow.
Passengers eat during elapsed campaign time. Reach the named harbor and
settle the job there. A harbor needs reception room for safe discharge.
Abandonment and loss use the existing refund, discharge and loss records.

COASTAL TRAFFIC AND CONTACTS
The existing coastal fleet owners now include appropriate civilian hulls.
Their cargo, purchases, wages, repairs and replacements use their actual
economies. Existing escort and interception battles protect or raid loaded
carriers. Captain contacts remember encounters you witnessed and actual
completed jobs. Remote events do not become eyewitness knowledge later.

SCOPE
36 boat designs. Coins; 100 coins per sailor-day. Ownership remains separate
from the 300-point battle limit. This version connects civilian work; the
separate v0.038 pass addresses career guidance and demonstrated balance gaps.
