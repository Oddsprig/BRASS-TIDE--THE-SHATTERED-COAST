REQUIRED ROOT ADAPTERS
Bind once before gameplay:
1. Boarding.harbor_rules(core, port) -> Dictionary containing exact booleans:
   here: genuinely at this actual harbor
   service: actual Politics.permit(core, "service") result normalized to bool
   friendly: actual Friendly authority
   Missing fields/binding deny access. Do not use fixture state flags in production.
2. Boarding.unit_price(core, port, good) -> positive exact integer bronze-coin
   unit price from the actual Economy.price interface.
3. Boarding.fleet_capacity(core) -> actual campaign roster limit, 1..256.
4. Boarding.household_key -> canonical EXISTING port.purses household account.
   No household balance is generated when the key is missing.

Economy.price and politics result schemas were not supplied. These adapters avoid
inventing their signatures. Public-store purchases transfer their entire material
value to governmentPurse; construction labor then transfers to the existing
household purse. Private stocks are never taken as public property.

CAMPAIGN HOOKS
Call ensure_state(core) during migration. It adds only missing zero counts,
an empty boardingPrizes array and a zero boardingSerial; malformed values survive
for validation rather than being silently reset.

Training must provide genuine training.qualified.boarder within sailor total.
hire_party consumes existing general workforce/recruits and qualification counts,
adds actual crew/boarders within physical berths, and transfers the normal ten
sailor-day advance to crewPurse. No party purse exists.

Anonymous cross-qualifications cannot identify which gunner/mechanic trainees
were hired. Hiring conservatively reduces both entitlements by the hired count,
bounded at zero. General defense recruits exclude all specialty counts
conservatively. No people or qualifications are generated.

BATTLE HOOKS
Pass actual ship references and the battle RNG.
launch(), abort(), tick(battle, dt) operate entirely on those real people.
Tick uses a globally ordered two-second exchange clock; at least four seconds
precede capture. Maximum 32 jobs, 128 people per party, 30 exchanges per job.
Tick accepts finite dt up to 3600 seconds.

Job records contain JSON-safe IDs/counts only. initial, killed, returned, lost,
prize and captives are historical accounting fields; alive is the temporary
physical cohort. Do not count historical returned/prize/captives again.

Abort returns survivors only to a living source in actual contact, limited by
remaining berths. Otherwise lost/partial recovery is explicit. No automatic
third-party rescue exists.

Captured hulls remain the SAME dictionaries in battle.ships and battle.captured.
Crew becomes the surviving party; old surviving defenders become captives.
Hull damage, loadout, fuel, ammo and supplies remain unchanged.
Wounded defenders remain included in the captive count; captive injury detail
is not separately modeled.

Immediately honor captureSecured, supportOnly and needsHarborRepair:
exclude captures from working weapons, targeting as combatants, deployment
points and ordinary salvage. Root owns rendering, towing, outcomes and politics.

SETTLEMENT
Call settle before discarding combat state. Actual source references already
contain their survivors; settlement never re-adds their original crew.
Active jobs are first recovered or explicitly lost.

Captured hulls enter fleet once when capacity permits. Otherwise the SAME hull
is held in saved state.boardingPrizes with its real prize crew. Repeated settle
can promote held prizes without duplication. Only missing/colliding UIDs change;
boardingSerial supplies stable prize-N UIDs. Held captives transfer once to
state.captives and leave the hull's captive count.

A failed settlement requires retaining unresolved battle references. Do not
discard them. Pending prizes cap at 64; captive counts cap at 1,000,000.
Root must include boardingPrizes in save/load and tow/harbor handling.
After real harbor repair, root can clear needsHarborRepair/supportOnly.
No coins, resources, weapons or deployment points are granted by settlement.

DEFENSES
One kind per harbor, maximum four units. Captain-funded construction only.
Requires actual public materials, trained general recruits, available labor and
available specialists. Construction is an immediate paid work bill; it does not
implement a persistent timed construction job or individual expert identities.
Battery labor: 12 laborer-days + 4 gunsmith-days + 2 foreman-days = 2150.
Garrison labor: 8 laborer-days + 1 foreman-day = 975.
Available specialists provide the numerical oversight capacity.

Defense crew leaves workforce, availableWorkers, recruits and sailor eligibility.
port.defenses.crew is the canonical separate cohort.
guns/ammo become held defense inventory; structural materials are consumed.
New or expanded defenses remain inactive until subsequent daily provisioning.

Call daily_defenses at each economic day before using operational strength.
Existing defenses must have current daily accounting before expansion.
Payroll is governmentPurse -> household at Goods.SAILOR_WAGE per crew-day.
Arrears accumulate; all arrears and current wages must be paid to operate.
Paid standby crews may still be inactive for missing food/support/ammunition.
Food and supplies are consumed from real publicStores; ammunition refill moves
existing public ammo into defense ammo. Repeated calls on one day do nothing.

Use defenses.active/strength only after daily accounting.
take_defense_shot(port) spends one held ammunition and returns a bounded
damage/range/speed/cooldown spec. Root must enforce that cooldown and use its
actual projectile/impact system and harbor position. There are no invisible
shots, automatic fleet kills, territorial conquest or infantry nodes.

VALIDATION
validate(state, world) -> empty string on success, otherwise a reason.
world must expose ports as an Array of id-bearing records or Dictionary values.
Checks finite numerical boarding fields, exact whole boarder/defense counts,
berths, unique UIDs, saved prize flags, defense cohorts/accounts and world port
references. Existing global economy/save validation remains required.
Battle boarding jobs are not saved.
