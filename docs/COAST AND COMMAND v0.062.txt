BRASS TIDE v0.062 - COASTAL DETAIL AND CLEARER DECISIONS

Use the Desktop shortcut named Brass Tide for the latest completed build.
WASD moves the camera, the mouse wheel changes zoom, and flagship autopilot
remains the default. The36boat roster and physical equipment are unchanged.

The eight islands and ten ports now show shaded slopes, individual trees,
building walls and pitched roofs at practical chart scales. Drying racks,
timber stacks, granaries, foundry chimneys, assembly slips, tank farms and gun
courts distinguish their industries. Artwork uses the existing cached meshes;
water and navigation keep their existing inexpensive systems.

Your place on the coast puts selected progress, current shortages and watch
choices first. Open the labeled sections for dated sightings, visited market
quotes, sponsor trade estimates, full terms, reserves and history. Unseen ports
stay unseen; quotes and completed achievements do not guarantee future safety.

Home harbors puts your existing business books ahead of new charters. Purse,
daily pay, unpaid wages and actual paid working days are prominent. Cargo and
capital controls remain visible. Expand Staff, materials & stored goods for the
roster, exact inputs and inventory, or New business charters & setup costs for
paid construction terms. Capital is separate from revenue; labor/materials
and unpaid wages remain real costs. Review and close pay nothing; an explicit
action publishes pending accounts before checking access and the transaction.

still need work. The full-fleet measurement retains its failed performance
criteria; no performance improvement or Starsector parity is claimed. Exact
scope tests and measurements are in tests/ and docs/TEST RESULTS v0.062.md.
