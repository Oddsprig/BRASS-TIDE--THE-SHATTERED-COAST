BRASS TIDE v0.053 - MEANINGFUL LTV TRADE

Open a harbor Market, then Trade opportunities to compare recorded visits.
Visit another harbor and open its Market to record prices, stocks, buyer funds
and useful local input needs. Unvisited markets have no remote price quote.

Each estimate separates the gross sale, nominal round-trip net after purchase,
wages, fuel and food, and a conservative price sensitivity allowance. That
allowance uses the existing age uncertainty of6% per day, capped at48%; it is
not a probability, guaranteed minimum or insurance against piracy. A same-day
quote has zero current age allowance. Prices and buyers may change en route.
Cargo already aboard excludes its historical purchase cost from displayed net.

Open Costs, price uncertainty & quantity limits for funding, food, working fuel,
quantity constraints and the two-day contingency allowance. A loaded tank is
individual to each boat. Loose fuel is still cargo until loaded. Owned stored
or uncrewed hulls cannot provide an active shared tank or hold. Purchase limits
are estimates; actual stock reserves, legality and payment are checked again.

Select a destination & review voyage opens preparation without buying or
setting sail. Buy through the existing Market quantity controls, load stores
through Hold, explicitly Chart course in preparation, then Sell at destination.
Keep the return payroll, food and fuel protected before committing your purse.

Local input needs & supply consequences explains dated stock gaps and an
actual recipe using those inputs. Selling adds physical stock for paid workers;
other materials and wages remain necessary. A stock gap is not a purchase order.
