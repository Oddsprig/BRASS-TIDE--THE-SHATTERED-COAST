BRASS TIDE v0.074 - TACTICAL COUNTERPLAY

Source reading of delivered v0.073, commit 21fe05b, on 30 October 2026.
This note is not a battle result. Nothing here was run in the engine.

WHAT YOU SHOULD SEE
Give a boat attack, escort, hold, move, withdraw or board, and it should
answer that order without spinning its target or stalling the group. Light
gun boats already try to reach an open side, fire a short pass and peel.
A torpedo or rocket boat already lines up one fixed shot and turns away.
An escort already stays on a leash around the boat you named. A heavy gun
boat already tries to sit in a useful firing band. Support stays behind an
armed friend.

v0.074 makes those existing habits meet. Two light boats may share one
target when each can actually reach a different side. An escort steps onto
the beam, inside its leash, when a flanker is working there even if that
flanker has not yet pushed into the leash. A heavy boat turns its station
so the arcs it already has face that flanker, then holds. The flanker is
punished if it stays in those arcs, and it leaves after its burst or its
one shot. A faster boat still gets away. The same rules apply to both sides.

WHAT THE SOURCE ALREADY DOES
Roles come from tactical_role_plans.gd:infer_role. An escort order, or a
named protected ally, is a screen before hull size is considered. A mission
ally, a boat with no working mount, or a support, civilian, cargo, tanker,
yacht or fishing spec is support. Otherwise the first working mount decides.
A first working mount that is not a fixed tube, on a hull larger than
radius 24 or on a gunboat or cruiser, is heavy.
A fixed tube (rocket or torpedo whose arc is under 135 degrees) is a striker.
Anything else with radius at or under 24 and speed of at least 95 is a
skirmisher. The remainder is heavy.

On the current roster that means a Tinwake Skiff (size 14, speed 118, gun)
and a Galeblade Interceptor (size 18, speed 148, gun) infer skirmisher. A
Silt Runner Lugger (size 17, speed 104, gun) does too. A Fen Stinger (size
17, speed 136, torpedo) infers striker. A Bastion Armored Cruiser (size 31,
speed 84, cruiser, cannon) infers heavy. An Ashwake Escort Corvette (size
21, speed 102, cannon) infers skirmisher unless it has an escort order or a
named ally, in which case it is a screen. The order makes the escort. The
hull class does not.

choose_target scores at most sixteen enemies. The shortlist is distance and
closing position only. Arcs, fittings and shots are not read until a hull
is inside those sixteen. An explicit attack or board target uses one of the
sixteen slots and does not erase a nearer threat. Screen weights protection
six times. Everyone else weights it three times. A same-target penalty of
0.9 per friendly reservation applies on engage, including when the second
boat could use the opposite side. The only fit bonus is hull radius: a
striker prefers a larger hull and a heavy prefers a smaller one. It is not
a role match. The previous target is kept unless a new score wins by more
than 0.5, or by a fifth of the old score, and a skirmisher's 1.2 second
commit is only extended by a real shot during a fire pass.

Stations offer at most eight approaches. Sectors start from formation index.
An occupied friendly sector on the same target is pushed down. Nothing adds
a score for a complementary bearing. A skirmisher pass sits near 0.78 of
range, fires inside 0.9 once it is near its anchor, and peels when the
burst record is met or when its speed is no longer ahead of the target's
actual velocity and the target is still outside range. A striker stages,
aligns, and leaves after one observed fixed shot. Spending that shot is
tied to the pass identity. A new route request does not refund it. A new
attack order does clear it.

A screen anchor goes to the threat only when formation is already "screen"
or the contact is incoming. Incoming means protection_threat is above zero:
the enemy's working arc already covers the ally, or the enemy is closing
into the leash. On the default line formation, a flanker that sits outside
those arcs and is not closing leaves the escort on its formation offset.
The escort's exit runs around the protected hull to the rally. It does not
hold the leader's chair.

A heavy anchor holds when the boat is already inside 0.55 to 0.88 of reach
and otherwise steps along the bow line, shifted one sector sideways. The
exit is that same point, a hold, not a chase to a rally. The role planner
does not read combat_balance044.gd. That file clamps a cannon on mount0,
when the socket size is 3 or more, to a 135 degree bow arc, and its base
reload note is 1.8 seconds. The planner only sees the arc and reload
already stored on the mount. It does not yaw the heavy station so that
stored arc faces a beam flanker.

Survival already breaks engage, or an order marked for conditional
withdrawal, at hull 0.60 / 0.45 / 0.30 and the matching flood for cautious,
steady and aggressive doctrine, and also for fire, imminent sinking, dry
guns, no fuel, or weapon crew at or below 0.01. Move, withdraw and board
hold fire. Hold may shoot only as a defend request. A lost escort or a lost
protected ally holds. Manual helm drops the retained plan. Attack with no
remaining target holds.

Shots are not granted by a role, a reservation or a predicted path.
tactical_routes.gd still has to certify the geometry. tactical_fire_control.gd
still has to pass gate, solution and actual_lane: ammunition, crew, a live
mount, reload, a held-fire choice, land, allies, and for a tube the same
unspent pass. Those files stay authoritative.

The battle clock stays. The data worker steps at 1/30 second. Helm and fire
are still every tick. A hull without a worker intent is eligible at 0.1
second; once it has an intent the gap is 0.15 second. With the worker, the
observation gap is 0.15 second. Each side gets one 0.15 second batch and
one 2900-test, 128-proof, four-route allowance. Completing the batch does
not open another. Safety rechecks at 0.1 second unless hull, crew, engine
or equipment actually jumps. The lease renewed before 0.2 second is the
existing certification lease. None of that moves onto the main thread.

Clocks that already survive an ordinary resample, and must keep surviving:
pass shots and their timestamps, tube salvo spending, pursuit age (the 18
second abandon), the 6 second window after a real range opportunity with no
useful shot, the 3 second stall, the 4 second failed-target exclusion, and
recovery travel. A real order change, a real target change, a new role, a
danger break or a stall may reset the clocks the source already resets. A
fresh sample by itself must not.

WHAT v0.074 CHANGES
Only tactical_role_plans.gd and tactical_stations.gd. No new role, no new
order, no new boat, no new weapon, and no new clock.

choose_target and _decide may allow a second attacker to keep the same
target when a separated approach is actually open. They favor a closing
flanker that the screen or the heavy can already see inside the sixteen.
The general same-target penalty remains for a second boat that cannot reach
a different side.

candidates ranks those separated bearings from the friendly reservations,
travel, the target's motion and the working arcs already on the shortlist.
Each offer is still one finite pass with an exit. If the complementary
station cannot be certified, the boat takes another legal approach or
regroups. It does not wait for a partner and it does not have to complete
a ring.

For the escort gap specifically: when the chosen contact is a skirmisher or
striker on the beam, the screen station moves to that beam bearing and stays
inside the leash, including on the default line formation when the flanker
is outside the protected arcs and is not yet closing. The heavy station yaws
toward that same flank and still ends in a hold. Turning the arcs the boat
already has, an escort intercept, or a real speed advantage must still beat
the squeeze. Both sides use the same observation, movement and weapon limits.

THREE WITNESSES
Root runs these later. A source reading is not a pass. Each witness uses
boats already in game/data/world.json, the existing orders, and the existing
route and fire certificates. No new scenario harness.

1. Screening a beam harasser.
   Bastion Armored Cruiser, escorted by one Ashwake Escort Corvette on the
   escort order and the default line formation. One Galeblade Interceptor
   starts abeam, outside the Bastion's working arcs, not closing into the
   leash, then begins a gun pass.
   Today the corvette is a screen because of the order, and the source
   leaves it on the formation offset until the Galeblade enters an arc or
   closes into the leash. After the change the corvette should take the
   beam station inside the leash and meet that pass. The Galeblade peels
   when its burst record is met. The corvette does not orbit and does not
   cross the Bastion. If the Bastion is removed, the corvette holds.
   Fail if the escort chases past the leash, switches target on every
   sample, or a shot appears without gate, solution and actual_lane.

2. A fitting changes the flank, and the heavy answers it.
   One Bastion against a Galeblade that reaches the flank inside nine
   tenths of its gun range. Repeat with a Fen Stinger instead of the
   Galeblade.
   Today both attackers are smaller than the Bastion, so the heavy fit
   bonus treats them alike, and the Bastion re-anchors on its bow line
   instead of yawing the mount arc onto the beam. The Galeblade is a
   skirmisher and peels after the burst. The Stinger is a striker and
   turns away after one observed fixed shot. After the change the Bastion
   still holds rather than chases, and its station yaws so the arcs already
   on its mounts face the flanker. A Galeblade that stays in those arcs is
   in the gun station. A Stinger still gets one certified shot and leaves.
   The two fittings must not share a phase. The planner must still not
   import combat_balance044.gd. Sampling the decision again must not refund
   the Stinger's salvo or zero the Galeblade's burst record.
   Fail if the Bastion's exit becomes a pursuit, either attacker fires
   without the existing certificates, or the Stinger launches a second
   tube shot on the same pass.

3. Shared pressure, a faster boat, and the orders.
   Two Tinwake Skiffs on engage against one Bastion. They are faster than
   the Bastion, so the existing "target pulling away" regroup does not
   apply while they can still reach. Today the second skiff is penalized
   for the first skiff's reservation even when the far side is open, and
   sectors only avoid a duplicate. After the change the second skiff may
   keep the Bastion when its opposite bearing is reachable, for one finite
   pass with its own exit. If that station is blocked it takes another
   legal approach or regroups. It does not wait, and the pair does not have
   to surround the Bastion.
   Repeat the chase with two Silt Runner Luggers against one Galeblade
   while the Galeblade's actual velocity stays ahead of the luggers and
   the range is still open. That chase already ends in regroup. The change
   must leave that ending in place.
   On these same boats, attack stays on the named hull, withdraw and move
   hold fire, hold shoots only as defend, board does not shoot, a lost
   escort holds, and manual helm suspends the plan. A new attack order may
   clear a spent tube pass. A resample alone must not clear pass shots,
   pursuit age, recovery travel or salvo spending, and neither side may
   score a hull that never entered the sixteen.

NOT IN THIS PASS
v0.073 art stays a separate delivery. This note does not recertify hulls,
weapons, charts or motion, and it does not call those assets newly accepted
or newly unchanged. The v0.073 limit still stands: pixel and physical asset
coverage, not every angle, damage state, fitting, sinking, realtime frame
rate, sound or play. Light-fleet frame time and the v0.071 listening review
remain open and are outside this scope.

No officer track, roster, economy, render mode, weapon framework or
performance harness is added. tactical_fire_control.gd, tactical_runtime.gd,
tactical_routes.gd and combat_balance044.gd are not part of the edit.
