BRASS TIDE v0.074 - TACTICAL COUNTERPLAY

Light boats can share a target while approaching from different bearings.
They seek a useful firing pass, turn away after their burst, recover room,
and try again. They do not wait for a complete ring. Crowded approaches,
long travel, enemy gun arcs, reefs and friendly boats can spoil a pass.

Escorts respond to observed flank pressure near the protected boat and stay
within their leash. Use an escort to screen a transport or damaged ally;
chasing a raider across the map leaves the cargo exposed.

Heavies seek a firing station and bring their fitted working arcs to bear.
A heavy may turn with fire withheld before requesting a shot. Different
mount angles change that response. Flankers still exploit slow turns,
reloads and gaps in coverage; a speed advantage can make a pass fail.

Attack follows the named target. Escort protects the named ally. Hold
allows defensive fire; move, boarding and withdrawal retain their existing
fire restrictions. Default flagship autopilot and WASD camera remain.
A fresh AI decision does not reset a spent burst, salvo or recovery clock.
Ammunition, fuel, damage, crew and equipment keep their actual constraints.

TRY THE EXISTING PRACTICE BATTLES
In the fleet laboratory choose 'Practice: flank a heavy' for two light
boats against a Furnace heavy. 'Practice: escort against raiders' places
a loaded carrier and escort against raiders. 'Practice: fitted strike pass'

an attack, hold or withdrawal order. Laboratory practice gives no career
reward. Space pauses; Tab opens the tactical view; WASD moves the camera.

VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
The native witness uses small declared test fleets through the actual
battle, movement and firing systems. It checks complementary approaches,
finite paid passes and recovery, escort interception, commands and two
working gun arcs. A separate exported-game movie uses the canonical
three-boat practice setup and the normal worker. Root records its actual
result and sampled visual review in tests/ and ACTUAL074 CHECKS.json.
A short movie prefix does not prove every fitting, terrain or full battle.
Earlier light-fleet performance, sound listening and overall quality limits
remain open. v0.073 artwork is retained unchanged with its prior evidence.
