BRASS TIDE v0.075 - FACTION STRATEGY

What you can watch for on one funded run between two generated island
groups. Source note only. Pending in play. No chart has been exported
or witnessed.

Forty-eight groups and thirty-six hulls stay. Islands inside a group
can belong to different factions. The open sea is the long passage.
A meeting counts on the local chart you are using. Harbor access and
who rules an island stay as they are. Changing control is v0.076.
This version is presence, trade, and a paid return home.

INTENTION
A trader in port, with coins and room aboard, chooses goods that
harbor can spare and a destination it can afford to reach. A pirate
prefers cargo it has already seen and judged worth the risk. A patrol
answers a threat it knows, escorts a friend, or moves to cut a hostile
supply run. Short fuel, food, crew, hulls, or wages send the boats to
their own yard instead. Sightings are dated. A hold you have not seen
stays unknown.

DELIVERY
Cargo that reaches the other harbor sells for coins that harbor has,
and only up to the stock it still wants. Shelves and purses change by
that sale. Goods left unpaid stay aboard. Cargo, coins, crew, and
boats remain the ones already paid for.

LOSS
A taken or sunk carrier does not land its goods. The lost hull is a
wreck, and a share of the hold goes with it. Survivors sail home.
You see the fight on that chart when the sighting is fresh. Far away,
the same event arrives as a scheduled report. Unseen cargo stays
unseen.

PAID RECOVERY
Home is an existing yard. Repair, rations, and sailors are invoiced.
Surviving boats must physically return before replacement collection.
A fleet recorded entirely lost can fund a new hull through its existing
home business. A replacement comes from harbor stock or a slip, one hull
at a time, when the purse covers the hull, duty, workers and crew.
Wages stay one hundred coins per sailor-day. A new hull appears at
the yard, after it is paid for.

LIMITS
Source note only. The corridor is pending in code. Root later queues
one private chart witness on the chart you are already using, after
the native pass. Distant fleets stay on that schedule. Whole-game
quality and listening stay open.
