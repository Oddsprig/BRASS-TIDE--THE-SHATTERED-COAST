CIVILIAN CAREERS v0.086 — PLAYER & VERIFICATION NOTES

Overview
========
v0.086 ("Civilian Careers Worth Repeating") improves existing fishing, sealed
freight, tanker fuel delivery, and passenger charter work and player guidance
so each role presents concrete route, capacity, risk, and paid reinvestment
alternatives using existing finite catches, cargo stocks, fuel tanks, passenger
cohorts, business/treasury purses, 40-lot daily water-cell quotas, supplied
rations, and arrival gates. No new careers, boats, or goods are invented.

What Changed
============
1. Fishing Return Buyer Ranking & Home-Region Authority (`civilian_choices059.gd`)
   - When reviewing fishing without an explicit buyer override (`buyer_port_id`
     empty), `Choices.fishing_review` evaluates recorded home-region
     (`shatter_coast`) harbors and prefers reachable (`route.ok`), legally
     accessible (`access.legal`), non-blockaded buyers with a dated positive
     fish quote (`sell > 0`) and liquidity for at least 1 whole lot
     (`floori(businessFunds / sell) >= 1`), ranked by:
       1) highest estimated net after return fuel, provisions, and rounded-up
          calendar payroll (`saleLots * sellQuote - operatingCost`),
       2) highest whole lots covered by the buyer's purse (`saleLots`),
       3) highest unit sell quote,
       4) shortest navigable return distance,
       5) deterministic `portId` tie-break.
   - If no recorded buyer qualifies, `fishing_review` falls back deterministically
     to the closest reachable home-region harbor while preserving all explicit
     route, quote-age, liquidity, legal-access, and blockade warnings.
   - Explicitly selecting a buyer harbor (`buyer_port_id != ""`) always preserves
     that explicit choice and its warnings.
   - `buyer_ports` and `_leg` enforce `shatter_coast` home-region authority so
     ports in other regions with similar local XY coordinates are never treated
     as home-local return routes.
   - `fishing_review` now exposes `buyerAlternatives`, `horizonAlternatives`
     (`0.5`, `1.0`, and `2.0` elapsed days), `groundAlternativeNote` (explaining
     when shifting 500+ units to an adjacent water cell accesses a fresh 40-lot
     daily quota), `reinvestment`, and `convoyOpportunities`.

2. Freight, Fuel Delivery & Passenger Charter Alternatives (`voyage_jobs.gd`, `civilian_choices059.gd`)
   - `_freight_offers` and `_civilian_offers` (`fuel_delivery`) now generate the
     `"larger"` load tier whenever exportable/demanded lots exceed the `"small"`
     tier (`qty > small`, i.e. 9..16 lots instead of requiring `>= 16`), matching
     `passenger_charter` (`count > small`). Mid-stock harbors and mid-demand
     tanker recipients now present genuine small-vs-larger capacity and risk
     choices on the work board.
   - `Choices.job_review` remains a single-offer pure read (`snapshot() == before`,
     never opening board offers or settling pending calendar days) while exposing
     `carrierAlternatives` across active hulls, `reinvestment` quotes, and
     `convoyOpportunities`.
   - `Choices.role_alternatives086` summarizes available board offers of the
     same role (`small` vs `larger` load tiers, destinations, outward/return
     days, gross fee, operating cost, and estimated net).

3. Reinvestment Quotes & Paid Conversions (`civilian_choices059.gd`, `career_costs.gd`)
   - `Choices.reinvestment086` quotes both:
     - the role's next hull (`brinehook_longliner` for fishing,
       `tideledger_coaster` for freight, `blackwater_fleet_tanker` for fuel
       delivery, `osprey_cabin_cruiser` for passenger charter) with local ready
       price, commission price, minimum crew advance, and current yard stock, and
     - the role's interior hull conversion (`cargo_rig` +25% hold or
       `auxiliary_tanks` +35% engine fuel tank capacity) with exact material,
       shipwright labor, yard margin, and tax costs without mutating live
       campaign relationships.

4. Witnessed Civilian Value -> Escort & Piracy Opportunities (`civilian_choices059.gd`, `campaign_guidance.gd`, `career_costs.gd`, `voyage_ui.gd`, `guidance_ui.gd`)
   - `Choices.witnessed_convoy_opportunities086` scans active loaded `trader`
     fleets within the 1800-unit watch radius in `shatter_coast`, reporting
     physical cargo (`tradeGood`, `tradeCargo`, `cargoValueCoins`), observed
     hostile threats (`Escort.threat`), deployment-point fit (`<= 300` DP),
     `escortEligible` (`Escort.start` for +5 standing and 1 service credit), and
     `piracyInterceptEligible` (`Escort.prepare_intercept`).
   - `CareerCosts.plan`, `Guidance.report`, `GuidanceUI.career_plan`, and
     `VoyageUI` surface these route/capacity alternatives, reinvestment quotes,
     and witnessed convoy opportunities while remaining 100% pure on read/close.
