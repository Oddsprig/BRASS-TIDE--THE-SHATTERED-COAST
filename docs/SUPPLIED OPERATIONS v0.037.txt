BRASS TIDE v0.037 - SUPPLIED FACTION OPERATIONS

Open Regional goals with L. Choose two corridor harbors, visit an endpoint,
and choose Secure a trade corridor. A suitable existing friendly patrol can
receive support from the sponsoring harbor's treasury. The panel names the
actual force and explains its current assignment and readiness.

THE SHARED OBJECTIVE
The patrol can cover a loaded friendly convoy using the corridor, intercept
a nearby hostile fleet threatening the route, or patrol the actual harbor
approaches. Boats travel and fight through the existing campaign simulation.
Observed arrivals, encounters and merchant passages supply the progress.

SUPPLY THE FORCE
Read the outbound and return reserve quote. Fuel, food, ammunition, wages
and functioning machinery all matter. The normal dock, payroll, repair and
replacement systems pay for actual goods, labor and equipment.
At the sponsoring harbor, use the corridor supply controls to deliver owned
fuel, ammunition, food or repair supplies from your hold into local stock.
The patrol still buys and loads its stores through its usual paid dock work.

The treasury grant covers up to seven wage-days, capped at14,000 coins, and
preserves two public-payroll days. It is paid from the existing treasury.
The same corridor cannot repeatedly collect that grant. Withdrawing leaves
already paid grants and delivered goods in their actual receiving accounts.

SHORTAGES AND RECOVERY
The operation reports its current shortage, unsafe route or machinery issue.
A force needing recovery returns for paid repairs, resupply or replacement
before taking another assignment. Restore the missing stock, money or working
equipment and allow its normal dock and campaign activity to resume.

COMPLETE THE CORRIDOR
Keep it clear for seven consecutive economic days and prove its use through
a real merchant passage, a local escort or victory, or two endpoint visits
by the assigned patrol. Current threats and supply failures remain visible.
The operation and its journal persist with the campaign.

SCOPE
This extends the existing corridor goal. Other regional goals and the36-boat
roster continue to use their existing systems. Career balance and guidance
are the separate v0.038 step.
