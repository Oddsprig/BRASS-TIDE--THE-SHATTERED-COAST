BRASS TIDE v0.066 — OPEN OCEAN TRAVEL

Currents change sailing speed along a course. A stopped fleet does not drift.
Weather changes speed and visibility. The chart uses inexpensive effects.
Expected voyage time uses current weather; the conservative allowance allows
for slower weather and keeps a separate two-day reserve. Conditions can change;
check the forecast's validity and refresh before choosing an action.

A visible contact shows boats you can currently observe. A dated sighting keeps
its last-known position and boats until it is seen again or the report expires.
Investigate sails toward that report, without tracking a hidden live position.
Avoid plots a physical escape course. It grants no immunity or free supplies.
Resume replots your remembered destination from your current position.

A capable pursuer or pending encounter can block a chart crossing. Ordinary
course changes remain available to flee. Closing a panel does not clear pursuit
or settle pending wages. Explicit actions refresh the current voyage first.

The first offshore raider uses its existing boats, sailors, stores and purse.
It must leave and return physically and buy harbor service with actual coins.
This version has one such raider, not a finished interregional shipping network.
Fuel, food and repair stores are finite; sailors earn wages and purchases cost
coins. Keep the return allowance and the original long-voyage preparation guide.
