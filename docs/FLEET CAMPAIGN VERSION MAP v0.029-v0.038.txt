DIRECTION
Build a recognisable flotilla, prepare a fitting, choose an approach, fight for
a useful objective, bring damaged boats home and reinvest in the next voyage.
Explorer, merchant, escort, privateer, fisher and passenger careers benefit.
Business and
shipping management remain optional. The first improvements work on the existing
starter boat; they must not require buying a costly second hull to enjoy them.
Every step includes visual clarity tied to what the boats actually do.

WHAT ALREADY EXISTS
24 original hulls; physical interchangeable mounts; paid weapon/engine/armor
refits and lockers; 300 deployment points per side; default autopilot; WASD
camera; selectable move/attack/escort/hold orders; numerical crew; flooding,
pumps, patching, fire, engine damage, hit effects and sinking. The original
10400x7200 coast has navigation, exploration, persistent wrecks, four story
chains, multiple jobs, contacts, factions, moving NPC fleets, blockades,
specialists, storage, businesses and regional campaigns. Versions .019-.028
added training, reserves, machinery, work conditions, regional civilian goods,
private shipping, customs, boarding, coastal defenses and civilian transport.
Current NPC-NPC encounters already have a coarse numerical resolution and
paid recovery; deeper observed decisions, coordinated tactics and consistent
conflict are the future work. The new versions extend existing foundations.


v0.029 — FLEET WORKSHOP AND BOAT PRESENTATION
Player outcome: make a purposeful combat or voyage fit, with detailed weapons
that visibly occupy the correct deck mounts and a bill you understand.

Build:
- A fleet fitting workspace: compare known hulls, select actual numbered sockets,
  inspect firing arcs and preview weapons, engine and armor. Separate fitting
  capacity, deployment points, hold, berths and daily obligations clearly.
- Save named fit templates as knowledge. Applying one uses actual modules,
  locker/harbor stock and paid installation. Show the full change and return
  reserve first; reject an unavailable or unaffordable plan before any mutation.
  Removed modules remain one physical item, including their condition.
- Improve decks, cabins, transom machinery and separate weapon models across
  the existing 24 hulls. Give MGs receivers/feed detail, cannons proper breeches
  and recoil assemblies, rockets distinct cells and torpedoes real launchers.
  Keep believable scale, reserved deck footprints and the same mount origin for
  preview, aim and muzzle effects. Retain cached inexpensive artwork.

Visual deliverable: labeled all-24-hull gallery, bow/beam/stern headings, empty
and fitted mounts, and representative battle-context views. Keep the rejected
under-boat dark reflection/shadow artifacts removed.
Depends on: delivered refit, mount-layout, boat and weapon artwork.
Exit gate: compare two fits on the same owned hull; apply one affordable starter
refit, use an actual locker, reject missing stock safely, save/restore and enter
an isolated lab battle with the previewed modules and arcs. Verify bills and
presentation separately. No purchase of another hull is a prerequisite.


v0.030 — TACTICAL ROLES, ENCIRCLEMENT AND HARASSMENT
Player outcome: boats fight intelligently in coordinated roles. Light craft
can flank, encircle and harass rather than all chasing the nearest opponent.

Build:
- Selection groups, line/screen/column stations and explained move/attack/escort/
  hold/withdraw intent. Manual control and explicit commands stay authoritative.
- Role-aware target/position scoring: threats to allies/objective, useful firing
  arcs, observed vulnerability, actual weapons/ammo, ally commitments and escape
  cost. Keep steady behavior and add cautious/aggressive alternatives without
  hidden stat/resource bonuses. Include conditional rally-point withdrawal.
- Light boats assign different reachable approach sectors, pressure an exposed
  side, make firing passes, draw aim, peel away under focus fire and regroup.
  Fixed-tube strikers align for a real solution and turn away after attack.
  Heavies/screens counter flankers; support/civilian boats protect their mission.
- Preserve actual acceleration, turning, mount origins/arcs and weapon costs.
  Spacing, stable target/station commitment, leader reassignment and feasible
  fallback prevent jitter, ally pileups and compulsory orbiting around land.
  Expose weapon groups/reserve fire and withheld-fire reasons. Keep WASD camera.

Visual deliverable: readable order/group intent and motion evidence of purposeful
harassment, firing passes and breakaways. A circle animation alone does not pass.
Depends on: .029 fitted-stat previews and existing battle AI/orders.
Exit gate: AI standard A1-A5, including both team orientations, screened and
unscreened heavies, blocked arcs, lost leaders and successful counters. Required
behaviors are observed; no test demands that light boats always win. Preserve
300 DP, reserve boundaries, seeded replay and responsive manual overrides.


v0.031 — DAMAGEABLE WEAPONS AND MACHINERY
Player outcome: a gunboat can lose a gun or pump and still be worth saving.
Damage-control decisions trade fighting power against survival.

Build:
- Bounded condition/disable state for actual fitted mount IDs, engine and pump
  machinery: three logical families, weapons/propulsion/pumps. Extend current
  section damage, flooding and fire; no fluid mesh or animated crew.
- Physical hits/fire can disable the relevant exposed fitting. A disabled
  fitting stops its actual function, with a visible cause and repair path.
  Do not make a random hit silently break an unrelated distant weapon.
- Extend current crew policies with a selected repair priority and concise
  inflow, pump/bailing, patching, supplies and crew reports. Work uses real
  crew time, kits/parts and applicable wages. Condition follows modules through
  refit, capture, salvage, lockers and saves; swapping is not a free repair.

Visual/audio deliverable: localized sparks, smoke, stopped mounts/machinery and
legible flood/fire warnings. Use strong recorded impacts and the bounded sound
pool, preserving the user's levels. Automated tests remain silent.
Depends on: .029 stable module identity and .030 doctrine decisions.
Exit gate: disable a gun and pump, reprioritize actual crew, exhaust repair
supplies, restore a save and recover/pay to repair the same hull and modules.
Include empty sockets, secured prizes, loss and no duplicated people/equipment.
AI must react to real pump/gun/engine loss, fire and flooding: reprioritise
crew, break a failed attack or disengage according to observed danger and
doctrine. Returning or swapping a module cannot erase its condition.


v0.032 — CONVOY BREAKTHROUGHS AND RECOVERY BATTLES
Player outcome: win by getting actual cargo through danger or extracting a
useful damaged boat, even while opponents survive.

Build:
- Extend the current survival-timer escort into a moving convoy breakthrough.
  Named real carriers must reach a navigable physical exit with their cargo.
  Arrival and actual losses determine each carrier's result.
- A recovery encounter uses an existing disabled hull or persistent wreck:
  secure it, assign actual prize/tow crew, hold the approach and leave with it.
  Reuse boarding/salvage custody; one object cannot become two reward hulls.
- Present objective, deployment, known allies, exits and withdrawal consequences
  before battle. Add a target-linked aftermath triage view comparing towing,
  stripping, spare hands, captive provisions and repair-to-service costs. Settle
  actual hull/cargo/job outcomes once; an objective win does not loot survivors.

Visual/audio deliverable: readable carriers/exits, differentiated muzzle and
impact mixes, convincing bounded explosions and staged sinking. Keep weapon
sounds grounded in recordings rather than musical hit pings.
Depends on: .030 orders, .031 condition, existing escort/boarding/recovery.
Exit gate: one carrier escapes while another sinks; an extracted hull retains
its damage; an abandoned tow yields nothing; a secured prize can flood. Repeat
settlement safely and reconcile real people, hulls, cargo, wrecks and escrow.
Apply objective-aware roles to both sides. Threatened carriers remain physical
attackable boats even when designated support; armed transports consume normal
300 DP, unarmed objective slots are bounded and confer no free combat buffs.
Define overflow/reserve risk and physical exits explicitly. Test AI standard A9.


v0.033 — AUTONOMOUS FLEET CONFLICT, SIGHTINGS AND PURSUIT
Player outcome: a living coast where pirates raid, escorts/patrols respond and
hostile factions engage each other even while the player remains docked elsewhere.

Build:
- Observe -> evaluate threat/value/return costs -> trade/raid/escort/patrol/war
  objective -> sail/intercept/avoid/join -> disengage/settle -> paid recovery and
  eventual return to duty. Use real hulls, crews, stores, purses and actual paths.
- Pirates prefer valuable vulnerable traffic and can avoid strong escorts;
  patrols protect/respond sustainably; hostile factions independently interdict
  opposing fleets/supply. Merchants seek safe routes, wait, request funded
  escorts or retreat. Pairwise hostile/allied/neutral relations govern every
  engagement and joining side. No player trigger or nearby-player requirement.
  Three-way pirates/faction/neutral interactions preserve participant-specific
  hostility, deployment and prize responsibility. Do not collapse unrelated
  enemies into a single side. Reserve each hull UID for one active encounter,
  persisting entry/join/exit, orders, timers and commitments through saves/detail
  transitions. Shared sightings do not expose undiscovered cargo or weapons.
- Personal/shared reports have provenance, age and uncertainty. Last-seen
  course/area permits search or abandonment, not following globally visible
  unseen positions. Decisions need commitment/hysteresis and explained changes.
- Interception/reinforcement requires physical contact/arrival. Share canonical
  encounter and hull IDs across distant/near resolution, saves and aftermath;
  casualties, captured stock, wrecks, costs and standing settle exactly once.
  Repair/recruit/replacement consumes real harbor capacity, time and money.
- Cheap distant resolution preserves real fitting/store/damage/crew/deployment
  rules and calibrated role/doctrine/escape effects. Player presence or a detail
  switch cannot resurrect/heal a fleet, restart combat or create free loot.
  Keep customs witness history independent of subsequently losing visual contact.

Visual deliverable: dated confirmed/lost contacts and fleet intent, readable
pursuit/escape estimates, actual NPC encounter/aftermath reports and cheap overlays.
Depends on: .030 roles, .031 damage, .032 objectives and existing autonomous fleets.
Exit gate: AI standard A6-A8. With the player docked far away, observe pirate /
loaded convoy / responding patrol plus hostile-faction combat. Neutral and peace
controls do not fight. Save during pursuit/encounter; survivors physically return,
pay or fail for insolvency. Validate coalition/double-pair prevention, calibrated
near/distant outcomes and stable serial/worker publication.


v0.034 — COASTAL GEOGRAPHY AND LARGE-HULL NAVIGATION
Player outcome: routes and encounters have a recognisable place; lighter boats
can exploit sheltered passages while heavy hulls choose a clear approach.

Build:
- Author three original terrain treatments: open roadstead, narrow island
  passage and reef-fringed approach. Improve coast silhouettes, approaches,
  route distances, zoom labels and local landmarks without empty travel padding.
- Select bounded battle geometry from the actual encounter region. Position
  coast, passages, shore defenses and exits consistently using static polygons
  and route grids. The same location/seed must produce a valid repeatable map.
- Add fixed shallow restrictions only where a readable hull-class choice is
  useful. Check route feasibility before orders. Migrate old voyages copy-first,
  preserving port/site IDs, inventories and paid routes. Route-cache keys must
  include changed geometry and relevant hull eligibility; invalidate old entries.

Visual deliverable: three distinct regional battlefield/approach galleries with
spatially correct batteries, cheap shoreline art and restrained wakes.
Depends on: .030 movement, .032 objectives and .033 observation geometry.
Exit gate: light/heavy fleets follow valid routes, objective exits stay reachable,
required harbors remain connected, actors never spawn in land and old voyages
migrate without inventory/position loss. Fixed terrain is this version's scope;
dynamic tides and simulated water require separate design and measurement.
Validate galleon-scale civilian envelopes and route/dock/spawn clearance in
preparation for .035. Encirclement must choose reachable sectors and safe
escape routes; large transports must not exploit tiny collision footprints.


v0.035 — CIVILIAN HULL PROGRESSION
Player outcome: buy/build an appropriate working civilian boat, from a small
fishing/transport craft to a galleon-sized powered merchant ship or elite yacht.

Build:
- Target12 original additional designs: 3 fishing (skiff/netter/trawler),4 cargo
  (launch/coaster/freighter/galleon-scale motor merchantman),2 fuel tankers
  (lighter/coastal tanker) and3 yachts (courier/merchant/high-ranking official).
  Preserve all 24 existing hull IDs; report the actual delivered count honestly.
- Give each a distinctive physical hull/layout/scale, crew and machinery bill,
  working stores, hold/tankage/guest accommodations, speed/turning/route footprint
  and compatible mounts/defensive conversion. Larger means substantial structure,
  cost, working crew and maneuvering needs, not a cheap scaled-up combat hull.
- Construction/availability use actual staffed yards, materials, experts, paid
  time and launch clearance. Refit/lab/UI/cargo/deployment/save schema cover the
  new designs without24-entry assumptions. New catalogue knowledge mints no
  boats, people, money or loaded cargo in legacy saves.

Visual deliverable: recognisable fishing working decks/gear, transport hatches/
hoists, tanker piping/tanks and finished yacht cabins/decks/pennants. Include
large hulls at actual combat scale and all new fitting/layout/motion galleries.
Keep cheap cached art and numerical crew; avoid crowded animated passengers.
Depends on: .029 fitting/art, .031 condition, .032 carriers, .034 size/route access.
Exit gate: civilian plan C1,C8,C9 and applicable A9. All 12 designs have real
catalogue/stats/bills/physical art and intended use, with small and galleon-scale
builds consuming actual paid materials/labor. Test dock/turn/path/selection/mount
clearance, capacity/custody and old24-hull migrations. Full economic voyage loops
are the explicit next milestone, not implied by the hull thumbnails.


v0.036 — WORKING CIVILIAN VOYAGES, PIRACY AND RIVALS
Player outcome: earn through fishing, freight, fuel supply or passenger charters;
protect or raid real traffic and recognise the captains whose fortunes change.

Build:
- Fishing boats travel to bounded grounds, use actual work/stores and carry a
  finite catch home. Existing shore production shares its source accounting;
  no stationary/uncrewed passive catch or duplicated fish. Buyers pay real coins.
- Transports carry finite origin stock into real buyer demand and funded bulk
  orders. Player companies and NPCs use the same hull/cost/custody conventions;
  a large hold must have useful affordable routes, not just a large statistic.
- Tank cargo is separate from working bunkers/general hold. Close/docked timed
  transfer moves real fuel once; no passive endurance bonus or remote refuel.
- Yachts carry actual guest parties/baggage and manifested valuables from finite
  household/business/government owners. Premium service has funded charter terms.
  Guests are not duplicated workforce; rank affects legitimate escort/diplomatic
  responses, not magic loot. Use existing captive custody/release, not sale.
- Independent civilian AI works, transports, waits, evades, seeks funded escorts
  and uses objective exits. Pirates evaluate these real targets; faction patrols
  and hired escorts can deter/fight them without player participation. Support
  labels cannot protect their manifests from interception/capture/sinking.
- Give up to 6 existing owners persistent original captain identities and bounded
  witnessed memories. Survivors repair/replenish at cost; captives/dead captains
  do not spawn free revenge fleets. Personal trust and faction standing differ.

Visual deliverable: working civilian route/landing/refuelling/charter summaries,
recognisable owners/pennants and readable cargo/passenger/escort encounters.
Depends on: .033 independent conflict, .035 civilian hulls, existing industry,
companies, workforce/passenger, contact and political ledgers.
Exit gate: civilian plan C2-C7 plus A6-A9. Complete affordable starter fishing,
small-to-large haulage, tanker partial transfer and a funded high-status yacht
charter. With the player elsewhere, run actual piracy/escort response, loss,
paid recovery or insolvency. Repeated action/save/arrival cannot clone cargo,
fuel, guests, purses, reward/standing or replacement boats. Show voyage net costs
and preserve a captain's witnessed history across recovery/loss.


v0.037 — SUPPLIED FACTION OPERATIONS
Player outcome: open a contested corridor through freight, escort, exploration
or privateering, with consequences you can see in fleets and harbors.

Build:
- Deepen existing regional campaigns with one bounded three-leg corridor
  operation involving two actual factions, named harbors and existing fleets.
  The stages prepare supplies, secure passage and settle recovery/aftermath.
- Only funded real ships/crews/stores participate. Missing ammunition delays
  actual forces; delivered engines enable an actual paid repair. Allies fall
  back when their wages, stock or surviving hulls cannot support the plan.
  Coordinate several actual friendly owners and reports against an opposing
  faction objective, with arrival/rally/withdrawal conditions and 300 DP joining
  rules. Normal independent NPC conflict must already work in .033; this story
  is not the only mechanism that makes factions or pirates fight each other.
- Offer armed and exploration/negotiation approaches through existing rights,
  permissions and sponsor-funded contracts. Merchant contributions supply actual
  needs. Alternative completions close one shared award; describe personal and
  faction consequences. Embargo/treaty rules govern aftermath, without forcing
  independent players into a commission or town-management career.

Visual deliverable: a readable operation chart with dated convoy sightings,
short supply explanations and observed passage/security changes.
Depends on: .032-.036 and existing industry, companies, law and regional goals.
Exit gate: fund the operation, lose a real shipment, use an alternate approach,
resolve passage and settle once. Test peace, insolvency, withdrawal, captain
loss and saves. Reuse the existing war/economy books; whole-map conquest is
outside this milestone.


v0.038 — AI QUALITY, PROGRESSION, POLISH AND PERFORMANCE
Player outcome: start in a cheap launch and grow a distinctive viable fleet,
with understandable decisions and convincing battles across a longer campaign.

Build:
- Tune explorer, merchant, escort, privateer, fisher and passenger-service routes
  from real wages,
  materials, travel costs and actual outcomes. A useful first refit, second
  boat and return reserve need achievable earning routes using existing assets
  and funded jobs. Do not refill purses/stock to conceal an insolvent loop.
- Audit all 24 legacy hulls plus the delivered civilian roster and fittings for
  useful roles. Resolve unreadable
  artwork, dead-end upgrades, failed doctrines and repetitive encounters.
  Improve guidance, order editing, aftermath and journal filters from observed
  friction; keep simulation/debug counters out of ordinary play screens.
- Extend isolated Fleet Laboratory/dev fixtures for every new objective and
  loss branch. Record seed/config/version and bounded discrepancy reports.
  Profile long careers, offscreen fleets, saves, effects and 300v300 DP combat.

Visual/audio deliverable: full-roster fitting/battle galleries, objective/context
captures and mixer/voice-budget evidence. Automated CPU/codec checks do not prove
rendered frame pacing, a convincing sound mix or enjoyment. Record voluntary
play/listening observations if available; do not force a foreground test or
claim a human session occurred when it did not.
Depends on: the preceding nine completed versions.
Exit gate: six representative career flows, tanker-support fixtures and adverse
branches reconcile coins,
people, goods, hulls and condition; extended runs keep bounded records and
worker/serial results agree. Compare measured CPU baselines. Record rendered
measurements separately only when obtained without foreground takeover. Verify
copied legacy saves and exact-tag Windows menu/save/battle/icon/PCK/archives.
Complete AI standard A1-A9 and civilian plan C1-C10 with motion/context review.
Document counterplay, failed attacks and practical trading/civilian progression.
The owner's minimum quality target cannot be marked met by native-suite count
alone; disclose remaining observable behavior and subjective review gaps.
Predeclare supported fleet/party/carrier load, decision latency and performance
budgets, held-out matchup outcome tolerances and ordinary civilian progression
bands before tuning. Test survival/capture, damage, crew, ammo, manifest and costs
separately, including economic shocks and several real replacement cycles.


SEQUENCE-WIDE RULES
- Complete each playable increment on its verified predecessor. If a feature
  needs splitting, move later targets forward; never reuse a tag or count an
  unfinished feature as delivered. Plan changes alone do not advance BUILD.
- Coins are the visible currency. Preserve 100 coins per sailor-day. Derive
  equipment/boat costs from actual materials, ordinary/expert labor, transport,
  duties and explicit fees. Templates, doctrines and intelligence own no stock.
- Real households, workers, businesses, governments, fleet owners, companies and
  escrow hold finite money. Capital is not profit. Each payout/transfer has a
  payer and recipient; goods are owned, reserved, consumed or lost once.
- A person/module/hull/cargo lot occupies one canonical custody. Shore work,
  boarding parties, captives and civilian cohorts remain conserved. Salvage and
  capture cannot both yield the same equipment. Installed condition persists.
- Preserve24 legacy hull IDs and add the audited civilian roster; keep shared
  mount IDs, cheap 2D water, numerical crew, default autopilot, WASD
  camera and separate 300-point deployment. Use original art/map/names/content.
- Bounded copied-data workers join before main-thread transactions/UI publication.
  Existing economy cap is 8 lanes on verified Core Ultra 9 275HX / 24 logical
  processors, reserving 2 logical threads. Retain Separate rendering; profile
  before raising concurrency. RAM/GPU and rendered FPS are not established.
- Observations, routes, objectives and memories need bounded records and stable
  update order. Cache keys cover geometry and hull eligibility. No per-frame
  all-fleet path solving or unbounded visual/audio emitters.
- Prevalidate new state before live restore changes; migrate copies of legacy
  saves, preserving IDs, purses, stock, rights, custody, progress and damage.
- All tests stay background/hidden and serialized, one game/engine at a time.
  No mouse/keyboard/focus takeover, automatic audible testing or global changes.
  Prefer short question-specific scenarios within the existing 3-minute cap.
- Each future release needs targeted checks plus an actual campaign flow, full
  required regressions, exact-tag Windows export, hashes/source/build archives.
  Preserve older versions, original saves and music. Update the launcher only
  after the new build is independently delivered.
- Update in-game TXT notes when gameplay actually changes. Maintain CHANGES,
  code/system maps, current version/build records and handoff. Preserve raw
  Bridge reports and a separate lead verdict. Publication is separately verified.
