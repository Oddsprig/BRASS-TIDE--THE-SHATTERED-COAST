BRASS TIDE v0.065 — LONG VOYAGES

Open Ocean & coastal approaches to prepare a journey. Choose the destination,
the north or south approaches, a home harbor and any planned waiting days.
The route shows outward spending, a return leg and a separate two-day buffer.

Expected spending follows the calendar days crossed during your journey.
The larger allowance leaves room for delays. Already-earned unpaid calendar
wages are shown separately. Reviewing, refreshing and closing the plan spends
nothing. Course, crossing, loading and transfer buttons are explicit actions.

Each boat has its own fuel tank. Fuel carried in the hold remains cargo until
loaded. Stop your boats to select a donor, receiver and exact transfer amount.
Tankers can supply other boats through those transfers; tanks are not pooled.
Loading hold reserves moves ammunition, fuel and repair kits into working stores.
Food remains in the hold. Sealed job freight remains separate.

Food, sailors, professional premiums and existing wage arrears all need an
allowance. Stored crews still need their pay and food. Cargo space includes
equipment lockers and store overflow. Healthy dry boats need no repair kits
for an uneventful voyage; damaged boats use real crew work and supplies.

Estimates use your current fleet speed and conditions. Repairs, fuel shortage,
damage, fighting and other business spending can change the result. Reserve
warnings are advice; the coins and supplies stay yours until actually used.
After reaching Glasswind Keys, choose Shatter Coast to plan the trip home.
