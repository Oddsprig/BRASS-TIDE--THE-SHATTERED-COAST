BRASS TIDE v0.041 - PLAY

Use the plain Brass Tide desktop icon for the latest completed version.
Numbered older icons open their preserved older builds.

COASTAL CLASH
Start the standalone escort battle from the title screen. Keep the loaded
merchant alive and help it reach the marked physical exit. Raiders will try
to stop it. This battle does not spend or award your campaign's coins,
crew or cargo. Your voyage stays separate.

BATTLE CONTROLS
Boats start on autopilot. WASD pans the camera; Shift pans faster. Scroll
zooms, Home centers. Click or drag to select; Shift adds boats. Right-click
issues move, attack or escort orders. Space pauses for planning. Tab opens
the tactical view. U toggles manual flagship control; arrows steer and the
mouse aims/fires in manual control. Return to autopilot with U.

CAMPAIGN
Continue voyage resumes your saved captain. New voyage starts a campaign.
Choose harbors and plan expeditions around cargo, fuel, ammunition, supplies
and crew wages. Fleet ownership is separate from 300-point deployment.
The campaign chart uses fleet symbols; battle and refit show the boat assets.

Press Escape for the menu. The fleet battle laboratory remains available
for choosing other fleets and fittings.

This early build remains under development. Native headless checks verify
specified behavior; they do not establish displayed FPS or sound quality.
