Boats blocked by traffic can try another existing route offer. A blocked boat
can turn at zero throttle only when the integrated movement remains clear of
terrain and the physical swept hull floor. Existing firing checks remain.
Losing an automatic screen assignment no longer replaces explicit navigation
orders with an idle hold. Hold, Move and Withdraw stay player commands.

The scoped review uses existing stock boats and finite ammunition through the
actual combat worker. A declared laboratory opening is not an earned voyage.
Current native, rendered and complete-cycle observations belong in tests/;
source inspection alone proves no completed encounter or performance gain.
Earlier light-fleet latency, sound listening and whole-game quality are open.
This version does not claim all fleet sizes, terrain or ship matchups reviewed.
