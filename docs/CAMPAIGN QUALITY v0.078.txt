Existing career guidance shows actual full-fleet payroll, return reserves,
fuel and physical cargo limits. A quoted opportunity is not an earned voyage.
Fishing readiness uses the selected boat's current work quotation, including
its own hold and already-assigned gear; another carrier's empty hold cannot
make the fishing boat's full hold available.

The primary contract action appears near the top of voyage review. Held jobs
offer Review delivery & payment; the existing live settlement checks decide
whether cargo, passengers, surveys and other terms actually permit payment.

Completed journal entries keep their outcome and next step visible, with
Details for the full record. Each earned cache location appears once.
A paid recovery record points to the existing contact choice without asking
for the paid load again. Existing contact permissions and costs still apply.

Actual verification, timings, screenshots and remaining quality limits are
recorded in this portable build's tests/ directory. Prior performance misses,
unreviewed listening quality and overall game polish remain separate work.
