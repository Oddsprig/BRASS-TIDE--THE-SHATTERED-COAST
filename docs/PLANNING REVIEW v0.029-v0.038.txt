ADVISOR GAPS ACCEPTED AND INCORPORATED
1. Contact knowledge now separately covers identity, weapon/cargo/condition
   observations, intentions, report provenance and growing uncertainty.
2. Encirclement stations need executable approaches/exits, turn clearance,
   moving-allied spacing, order lifetime and chase/harassment termination.
3. Three-way piracy/faction conflict preserves per-participant hostility and
   prize/aggression responsibility; enemies are not merged against the player.
4. Each ship UID has one authoritative active encounter reservation, including
   waves, joining, escape/capture, saved timers and pending ammunition. Once-only
   rewards alone do not prevent two fights using the same hull simultaneously.
5. Civilian progression declares ordinary starter assets, economic reachability,
   resource recovery, customer income/sinks and stranded/insolvent failure paths.
6. Near/distant calibration uses held-out per-matchup survival/capture, damage,
   crew, ammo, manifest and cost measures; declare supported load, decision and
   performance targets before tuning. Numerical win rates alone do not pass.

LEAD SCOPE DECISIONS
- .030 role/pack tactics, .031 damage-aware survival and .032 physical objective
  behavior precede .033 independent sighting-led conflict. Faction operations in

- .035 now delivers12 original civilian designs: 3 fishing, 4 cargo, 2 tankers, 3 yachts;
  .036 delivers physical production/trade/fuel/guest loops and pirate/escort
  traffic plus persistent captains. The extra expedition branches move beyond
  .038; four existing chains and exploration remain supported.
- Civilian support cannot mean invulnerability. Real threatened unarmed carriers
  use bounded objective slots and armed conversions use normal 300 DP. Define
  overflow risk, actual manifests, exits and no free combat benefits explicitly.

  player/NPC boats. Yacht rank yields meaningful funded service/relations, not
  free guests/treasure. Tanker endurance requires actual conserved fuel transfer.
- Large hulls require proper geometry/turn/dock/route/art rather than only scaled
  statistics. Benchmark actual count/scale and retain cheap water/numerical crews.
