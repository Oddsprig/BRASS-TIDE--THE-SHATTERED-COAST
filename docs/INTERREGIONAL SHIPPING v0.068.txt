BRASS TIDE v0.068 - WORKING INTERREGIONAL SHIPPING

Open seas link local island groups. An island group may contain holdings of
different factions; owning one harbor does not own the entire archipelago.

Westglass Anchorage is a Reedwater branch on Westglass Isle in Glasswind Keys.
Its residents, operating reserves and opening stores are accounted for through
Kelp Reach. Its market has finite stocks and coins; local wages and production
continue while you sail elsewhere. Sight the harbor before using its chart entry.
Approach its actual berth, stop and open the anchorage market to trade.

An existing Kelp merchant buys cargo and supplies, sails through the coastal
approaches and ocean, trades at the branch, then returns for paid service.
Its boats, crew, cargo, damage and purse stay with the same owner throughout.
An unfunded or unready merchant holds at harbor instead of receiving free fuel,
crew or cargo. Inspect dated sightings carefully; old positions are not live.
Piracy and existing hostile-faction relationships can interrupt shipping.
Destroyed or stolen goods cannot also be delivered by a later arrival.

Use existing investigate, avoid, engage and resume-course actions. Attacking
peaceful traffic has the existing political consequences. Paid ship/crew work,
cargo room and return provisions still matter. No new player escort-order mode.

Glasswind's three surveys and finite wreck cache remain available. Recall a
shore party before leaving its landing. Harbors and settlement businesses on
Shatter Coast retain their existing functions; this branch offers its limited
market and supplies. A full generated sector and territorial expansion remain
later roadmap work. WASD moves the camera; flagship autopilot is the default.
Use the plain Brass Tide desktop icon for the latest completed build.
