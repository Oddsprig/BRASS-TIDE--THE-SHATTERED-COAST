BRASS TIDE v0.056 - FACTIONS AND PEOPLE

Open Factions & safe passage from Voyage jobs & contacts. Faction colors,
interests, doctrine, public standing, witnessed offenses and pair relations
explain whose waters and equipment are available to you.

Review commission terms as often as needed. Reviewing or closing the window
does not sign. Only Accept this commission signs the current terms; eligibility
is checked again. Declared enemies and the standing cost appear beforehand.
No stipend is created. The current commission remains recorded after reload.

Paid safe conduct grants temporary inspected civilian harbor access. Its exact
expiry is shown; claims and military restrictions retain their rules. The pass
does not protect your fleet from hostile ships at sea. A contacted patrol is
identified as a patrol, with its faction and public hostility explained.

Original harbor contacts remember completed work separately from faction
standing. A fulfilled promise can earn one compatible existing board job at
that contact's harbor. The board names the person, qualifying work, date and
recorded location. An introduction does not guarantee future stock or funds.
Acceptance still requires real sponsor funds, cargo, space, crew and access.
The named sponsor remembers completion or failure, including delivery elsewhere.
NPC fights you merely witness do not become your paid work.

Exact focused verification and exported-view evidence are in tests/. This is
one scoped step; later exploration, careers, art and whole-game quality remain
on the saved roadmap. Crew are numerical and all values remain bronze coins.
