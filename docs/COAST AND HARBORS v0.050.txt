BRASS TIDE v0.050 - COAST AND WORKING HARBORS

Eight regions now have distinct terrain: chalk orchards, tidal reed beds,
ironwood crags, polders, ash quarries, wooded levees, slate and basalt.
Ten harbor layouts show their actual work: bonded trade, fish curing,
timber yards, grain stores, foundries, assembly slips, covered markets,
refining, salvage stores and armored shipbuilding.

WASD pans the campaign camera; scroll zooms. Use the territory view for
the whole coast and move closer to inspect ports. Harbor names and berth
click centers remain tied to actual destinations. Land follows the same
shoreline used for navigation and translated coastal battles.

Terrain is prepared once and drawn from three cached detail levels.
Water, world size, island shapes, ports, routes, inventories, purses and
boat construction costs are unchanged. Boat assets remain the v0.049 set.
