BRASS TIDE — CIVILIAN FLEET PLAN v0.029-v0.038
23 October 2026 | OWNER REQUIREMENT | PLANNED, NOT IMPLEMENTED

PURPOSE AND CURRENT STATE
The owner wants viable player trade/piracy supported by actual civilian fleets:
fishing boats, transports from small launches to galleon-sized motor ships,
fuel tankers, and high-ranking civilian yachts. These are working owner-operated
vessels with vulnerable physical cargo and people, not decorative map markers.

Current world.json contains 24 original combat/armed hulls. Some carry useful
cargo and existing support conversions improve capacity/endurance. Dedicated
fishing/transport/tanker/yacht progression is NOT established by those hold values.
Preserve all 24 legacy IDs/fits and add original civilian designs. Planning target:
12 additional designs below, which would mean36 total only after all12 actually
exist and pass delivery checks. Do not report a future target as the current count.

INITIAL CIVILIAN ROSTER TARGET — ORIGINAL DESIGNS, WORKING CLASS LABELS
Fishing (3):
- Open fishing skiff: cheap local trips, small catch hold and modest working crew.
- Motor netter/cutter: more gear and catch capacity, longer trips and higher costs.
- Working trawler: larger catch/storage, meaningful labor/fuel and slow handling.
Cargo transport (4):
- Cargo launch: small owner-operated coastal parcels and starter trade.
- Deck coaster: bulk local goods, loading gear and a reason to hire escort cover.
- Coastal freighter: larger hold and dependable route work with expensive losses.
- Galleon-scale motor merchantman: a genuinely large civilian hull, substantial
  hold/crew, slower turning and suitable deep approaches. This is powered by
  machinery; the size analogy does not require making it a sailing combat ship.
Fuel tankers (2):
- Fuel lighter: short delivery and flotilla reserve support.
- Coastal tanker: bulk route/expedition fuel, costly working stores and machinery.
Yachts/high-ranking civilian craft (3):
- Courier yacht: fast private/official passage, limited baggage and cabins.
- Merchant yacht: wealthy civilian travel, comfort and credible paid protection.
- State/official yacht: high-ranking passenger parties, greater political value,
  distinct quarters/finish and an actual funded escort when available.

Final original names/art follow each design's faction, scale and function. Rank,
wealth and passenger importance do not imply a free treasure chest or warship.
Some civilian hulls may take limited defensive mounts or a paid armed conversion;
that consumes deck/hold, labor, fittings and deployment budget honestly.

DESIGN AND PRICE BEFORE A STAT NUMBER
For each design record length/beam/draft envelope, hull/deck/cabin/tank layout,
usable cargo volume/load allowance, passenger accommodations, minimum/optimal
working crew, machinery, working stores, fittings, speed/turning and deployment
cost where applicable. Choose a consistent game scale, not a stretched gunboat
with a multiplied capacity statistic. Large hulls must fit real docks/routes,
spawn areas, selection bounds, firing origins and collision/path clearance.

Bill = physical inputs + ordinary worker-days + expert/design/oversight days +
engine/handling/fitting equipment + transport/overhead + explicit fee/margin.
Trace wages to actual workers/experts and existing 100-coins-per-sailor-day terms.
A galleon-scale hold has real hull structure, labor and operating obligations.
No round vessel price is chosen first and retrofitted with a justification.

Show purchase/build cost separately from crew hiring/advances, fuel, provisions,
repair reserve, cargo investment, fees and return costs. Construction uses an
actual capable staffed yard, finite materials, paid time and launch clearance.
Large hulls require suitable existing/paid yard capacity, not a level-button
spawn. Player and NPC acquisition follow compatible physical accounting.

CAPACITY AND CUSTODY
- General hold, dedicated fuel tankage, working engine fuel, ammunition, crew
  berths and guest cabins are distinct. No unit occupies two capacities at once.
- Cargo/guest/tank allocations belong to actual hull UIDs. A full hold cannot
  also provide empty deck space for captives or extra cabins without a stated
  paid conversion and a coherent layout/load budget.
- Guest berths are separate from working crew. Luxury passenger capacity cannot
  be used to duplicate qualified workers. Existing civilian/migration/boarding
  cohorts retain their provenance and counts; new empty accommodations create
  capacity, not people or money.
- Loading affects the appropriate capacity and handling/endurance consistently.
  Use cheap bounded numerical penalties. Do not add a fluid/rigid-body cargo sim.
- Damage, fire, loss, capture, salvage, unloading and transfers move/consume the
  same manifest once. No generic money/loot award is also paid for that cargo.
- A support designation does not protect intercepted boats from combat/capture.
  Armed conversions obey300 DP; unarmed objective carriers use bounded real
  mission slots, no free combat buffs. Define overflow/reserve risk explicitly.

WORKING FISHING
Fishing is a playable productive voyage and an independent NPC livelihood.
- A working boat physically reaches a suitable known ground, assigns its real
  crew/gear, spends elapsed work time, fuel/provisions and gear maintenance,
  then carries its actual catch home. Empty fuel/crew/hold stops useful work.
- Use bounded authored fish-ground capacity and explicit daily recovery/source
  rules; retain depletion through save/load. New fleet catches and existing
  harbor fishery production cannot each claim the same catch allocation.
- Reuse appropriate food/preserved-fish goods/recipes. Processing needs actual
  inputs/labor and suitable equipment or a real shore business; no instant
  free transformation into high-value cargo aboard every fishing skiff.
- Selling requires physical landing, a real buyer, money and inventory transfer.
  Catch is not profit until sold, and cost includes crew, fuel, food, repairs,
  gear and applicable handling. Congestion/depletion/oversupply can make a bad trip.
- Publish one affordable starter fishing loop using existing owned assets or a
  genuinely costed small vessel; no mandatory purchase of a giant ship to begin.

TRANSPORT AND PLAYER TRADE
- Cargo boats and NPC carriers carry actual origin stock to a reachable buyer.
  Show free hold, loaded weight, purchase basis, crew/escort/working-store costs,
  expected arrival and dated sale assumptions. A quoted price is not a guarantee.
- Existing private shipping companies can use the same new hulls. Capital and
  inventory stay separate from earned profit; losses, waiting and recalls remain
  physical. Multiple concurrent owners compete for finite stock and liquidity.
- Test useful progression from launch/coaster to a large merchantman. The large
  hold needs credible real demand/production and funded bulk orders. Enlarging
  holds without buyers able to purchase cargo does not establish feasible trade.
- Sponsored freight/charters escrow actual sponsor money and identify goods,
  carrier, deadline/route terms, failure and fees. Partial deliveries settle once.

TANKERS AND FLEET ENDURANCE
- Fuel carried for sale/supply is distinct from the tanker's own propulsion
  bunkers. Dedicated tankage is not an infinite general warehouse.
- A tanker extends range by transferring real fuel into working tanks at a
  harbor or a safe close transfer with elapsed work/rate limits. Fuel leaves one
  owner/tank and enters another exactly once; interruption keeps the remainder.
- No passive endurance multiplier, cargo fuel counted twice, remote map refuel,
  free fuel on undocking or fuel restored when a support boat rejoins.
- The tanker still burns its own fuel, pays crew and needs repair. Trade demand
  comes from actual refinery/harbor/fleet use and available buyer purses.
- Existing bounded fire/leak damage can affect actual tank contents. Any added
  spectacle is capped visual feedback; no expensive simulated oil/water layer.

YACHTS AND HIGH-STATUS PASSENGERS
- Give yacht owners actual household/business/government provenance and finite
  funds. Guest parties reserve real existing people, accompanying attendants,
  baggage and any manifested valuables. No free high-value passengers or purses.
- Distinguish visiting guests from resettling workers. Arrival does not add a
  visiting party to workforce and another ship's passenger book simultaneously.
- Premium charter payment derives from a real customer's money and terms such
  as route, time, risk, accommodation and delivery. Fees are funded/escrowed;
  high rank is not an automatic reward multiplier or infinite passenger demand.
- Yacht AI favours suitable safe routes, reachable refuge, hired escorts and
  withdrawal. Display passenger importance when legitimately known. Damage,
  rescue, interception and witnessed neutral attacks have explained diplomatic
  consequences through existing faction/customs/contact systems.
- Escorts require existing boats/crew/stores and a payer. A destroyed/insolvent
  yacht owner cannot conjure a guard fleet. Capturing a prestigious yacht keeps
  the same hull/party/manifest and can be politically costly. Captives are not
  sold for coins; use existing safe release/custody handling.

PIRACY, ESCORTS AND INDEPENDENT ACTIVITY
Commercial traffic must create real opportunities while the player is elsewhere.
Pirates evaluate observed cargo/importance, vulnerability, escorts, own ability
and return cost. Escorts deter, screen and fight, and can fail. Patrols respond
to actual recent reports. Hostile factions can interdict the other's actual
trade/supply traffic; allied/neutral relations still constrain target selection.
Caught carriers try an objective-aware escape/cover, not a gunboat charge.
Losses remove the real delivered supply and may delay repairs/production.

For player piracy/commerce, show likely costs and known risks. A successful theft
can yield existing cargo or a recoverable hull; it is not both that cargo and a
second full-value coin payout. Repeated sightings, capture and resale must not
repeat political events or create a subsidy loop. Finite trade and sponsorship
must support practical routes, with oversupply and failed voyages possible.

ECONOMIC REACHABILITY AND FAILURE CONTRACT
Authored current fresh start (world.json): owned Tinwake Skiff at Brass Haven,
5 crew, 15,000 coins, 20 kits, 30 food, 60 fuel and 80 ammunition. This is a declared
new-game fixture, not a refill applied to a continuing save. Keep the100-coin
base crew-day anchor. Future starting changes must be explicit and costed.
- Predeclare ordinary starting assets, operating requirements, intended voyage/
  game-day progression bands and repeatable earning opportunities before tuning.
  A useful early refit should be reachable through a few successful local trips;
  record actual measured net margins and days, not a rich test-only company.
- Large-vessel progression depends on actual build costs, market throughput and
  funded demand. Model customer income sources and consumption/demand sinks;
  wealthy charter clients cannot pay again until they actually have the funds.
- Extend campaigns through several catch/sale/replacement cycles and an economic
  shock, not one successful sale. Include shared depletion, lost tanker supply,
  a poor buyer and an insolvent employer, with specified viable alternatives.
  Failure is allowed; expose actual bankruptcy instead of silently subsidising.
- Define fuel exhaustion, stranded craft, unavailable berths, blocked ports and
  failed employers. Rescue/tow/paid delivery or liquidation uses existing actual
  resources/actors; no forced free fuel or replacement. A failed business may
  remain failed while the player takes another feasible line of work.
- New hull creation defaults must not mint working fuel/ammo/kits. Load them
  through an explicit paid bill/stock transfer, or launch with empty stores.
  Fishing/cargo/yacht NPC careers cannot receive a fresh starter purse each trip.
C2/C3/C5 must use the declared ordinary assets and progression interval. C6 must
exercise the economic shock and affordable recovery or persistent insolvency.
No route is guaranteed profit; the planned economic career must be viable under
its declared normal conditions without erasing losses or inventing customers.
