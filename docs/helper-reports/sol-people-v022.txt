Policy inputs:
set_policy(core, ID, "normal" / "overtime" / "rest" / "relief")
or set_policy(core, ID, {"policy":"overtime","cap":500}).
The cap covers this module's extra payments per business/economic day.
Base payroll remains outside that cap. Policy changes never clear strikes.

Normal: full existing base wages; factor 1.0.
Overtime: ceil(half each actual employee wage), paid to the corresponding
household/expert purse; factor 1.25 and fatigue increase.
No affordable complete premium means ordinary paid work, without a bonus.
Fatigue >=80 prevents overtime.
Rest: complete base payroll required; factor 0 and paid recovery.
Relief: one owned food per employee, complete base wages retained;
factor 0.9 accounts for food preparation during paid employee time.
No external service is hired by relief, so no hidden service fee exists.

Outstanding arrears warn immediately and cause a persistent strike on
the third processed economic day. Incomplete payroll earns no recovery.
Negotiation requires the real home harbor, service staff, owned food,
one owned supplies unit and complete funding from business.coins.
It pays every recorded arrear plus quarter-day laborer and foreman time.
With supplied wage rates, service payments are 25 household / 44 expert.
Liabilities clear only after those actual transfers succeed.

Successful negotiation retains the strike, requires two later fully paid
rest days, and resumes normal work on a subsequent eligible economic day.
New arrears invalidate the agreement. Recovery may continue if fatigue
or unrest remains high. Repeated negotiation cannot reset or farm relief.
Failed negotiation preserves cash, inputs, liabilities and recovery state.
