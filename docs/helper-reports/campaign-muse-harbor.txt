PUBLIC API (game/scripts/home_harbor.gd, all static)
- ensure(state) -> Array: normalizes plain state["businesses"] (<=12) and
  monotonic state["businessSeq"] IN PLACE so references root holds stay
  live. Each record: {id "business-<seq>", kind, name, homePort,
  charterSite, coins int, inventory {}, capacity, employees[] {name,
  occupation, wage, specialist}, workingCapital (input reserve),
  capitalIn, revenue, wagesPaid, dividendsPaid, arrearsHousehold,
  arrearsExpert, paused, establishedDay}. Unknown kinds/id-less records
  are dropped, never guessed.
- quote(core, kind, port_id) -> Dictionary: read-only. Kinds: "warehouse"
  (aliases rented_berth/berth), "salvage_yard" (salvage), "workshop"
  (repair_workshop/repair); always at EXISTING state.ports, no new ports.
  Permission here: captain docked at that port (real dock access);
  salvage_yard also needs rust_salvage_anchorage OR shatter_wreck_field
  surveyed (exploration level 3), workshop needs cinder_workshop_ruin
  surveyed. One business per kind per port. Bill: physical materials at
  Economy.price (checked against real port stocks) + setup wage-days at
  Goods.WAGES (ordinary/expert split at 150/day) + 8% margin + 5% duty
  (Construction pattern) + workingCapitalDeposit = 14 days of staff
  wages + inputCapital (500 warehouse / 1500 chartered). Returns full
  breakdown incl. staff rows, dailyWages, reserve (== deposit),
  affordable flag. Refuses missing materials, insufficient free workers
  (availableWorkers minus _yardWorkersUsed must cover setup days AND
  permanent staff) or free specialists likewise, before any write.
- establish(core, kind, port_id) -> Dictionary: re-quotes; coin refusal
  (setup + deposit) also precedes every write, so refusal is atomic.
  Pays captain -> port cash (materials+margin), householdPurse (ordinary
  setup wages), expertPurse (expert setup wages), governmentPurse
  (duty), and captain -> new business.coins (deposit, recorded as
  capitalIn). Consumes port stocks, bumps _yardWorkersUsed/
  _yardSpecialistsUsed by setup days, and permanently removes staff from
  the port: workforce and recruits drop by headcount, specialists/
  specialistWorkers/availableSpecialists by expert headcount, then
  Economy.sync_workforce(port). Staff exist only as business employees
  afterwards: never boat crew, never town industry. No free output: the
  business starts with empty inventory and only the deposited coins.
  Staffing: warehouse 2 laborers (200/day, cap 400); salvage_yard
  3 laborers + 1 mechanic (450/day, cap 600); workshop 3 carpenters +
  1 mechanic (525/day, cap 300).
- transfer_goods(core, business_id, good, qty, direction) -> Dictionary:
  direction in {in,store,to_business,deposit,unload} or {out,load,
  to_fleet,withdraw,take}; floats snapped to 0.01. Docked at homePort
  only. "in" moves real state.cargo -> inventory within capacity weight
  (Goods weights); stored goods never count in campaign cargo_weight/
  traveling hold. "out" moves inventory -> state.cargo within the
  fleet's real remaining hold (core.cargo_weight()/capacity()).
- capital(core, business_id, amount) -> Dictionary: captain coins ->
  business.coins via Money.move, capitalIn += amount.
- dividend(core, business_id, amount) -> Dictionary: business -> captain
  capped by min(coins - reserve, revenue - wagesPaid - dividendsPaid),
  reserve = 14 * daily wages + workingCapital. Contributed capital can
  never leave as a dividend; no passive faucet.
- tick(core, days = 1) -> Array[String]: whole days (floor). Arrears
  first, then wages business.coins -> home householdPurse (<150/day
  staff) / expertPurse (>=150/day). Shortfall stays in arrearsHousehold/
  arrearsExpert, sets paused, and that business does NO production or
  sales that tick. Paid workshop runs the existing Goods supplies recipe
  verbatim (0.4 timber + 0.05 iron + 0.5 carpenter-day per kit) from its
  own stored materials only, then sells whole kits; paid salvage yard
  sells its stored salvage-material goods (sorted); it never creates
  wrecks or goods. Sales go to the home port only while port stock is
  below Economy.get_equilibrium_stock AND port cash covers
  Economy.price(port, good, "sell"); coins port cash -> business,
  goods inventory -> port stocks, revenue tallied. Warehouse only pays
  wages. Missing home port pauses with purse preserved.
- list_businesses(state) -> Array: deep copies plus computed dailyWages,
  reserve and storedWeight.

CONSERVATION / NO-CLONING SUMMARY
- Every coin path is Money.move between existing purses (captain, port
  cash/household/expert/government, business). Setup materials are real
  port stocks; production inputs are real transferred cargo; outputs
  are recipe conversions or resold stored goods; income is only a
  liquid, under-stocked home port paying real cash.
- People are conserved: setup labor uses the shared daily yard pool,
  permanent staff are subtracted from workforce/recruits/specialists.
