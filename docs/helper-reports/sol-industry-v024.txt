Integration

1. Instantiate shipping_companies.gd once with:
   new(Goods.ITEMS, Money, Politics, hold_capacity)

   hold_capacity is Callable(core, boat) -> finite nonnegative usable cargo
   weight capacity. Return -1 for unknown capacity. Bind the existing fitted
   hull calculation. The inline brief omitted its spec key, so the module
   guesses no key.

   Politics.permit must return bool or {ok:bool}; otherwise provide a
   read-only adapter.

2. Player cargo is state.cargo. Player/company cargo dictionaries contain
   every Goods key. Ports are dictionaries with id/x/y; current_port()
   represents the actual harbor.

   specialists and shoreCrew are Arrays of {boatUid, active?:bool}.
   Missing active means active. Unknown containers fail closed for transfers.

3. After successful create, call NavalAI.ensure(state,data). Route the new
   actual patrol through NavalLifecycle.route using next_port(core,owner).

   Invoke next_port beneath emergency recovery at arrival/order selection.
   On recall, retarget the physical course through NavalLifecycle.route
   when emergency priority allows. next_port returns "" when recalled and
   docked home, or when the owner is inactive.

   Module does not advance voyages, manufacture income, consume stores,
   repair or replace hulls. Existing native fleet/lifecycle systems do that.

4. Commercial-only helper:
   trade_limit(core,owner,buying:bool,good:String,price:float)->float

   Mandatory limits:
   buyMax: whole coins/unit >0
   sellMin: whole coins/unit >=0
   maxCargo: finite units >0
   reserve: whole coins >=0
   risk: finite [0,1]

   Buys occur only at home. Sales occur at destination, or home after recall.
   Buys respect usable hold, quantity cap, reserve and price ceiling.
   Sales respect inventory/basis and price floor.

   Root must additionally cap actual stock, counterparty liquidity, exact
   integer payment/rounding and route risk. Life-support purchases bypass
   this commercial helper.

5. After each successful real goods/purse mutation, call record once:
   purchase: goods-only payment and actual commercial quantity added
   sale: gross goods-only revenue and actual quantity removed
   loss: coins=0 and actual commercial quantity removed
   wages: actual owner -> crewPurse payment
   fuel: actual working-fuel payment
   repairs/fees: actual owner expense

   Record fees separately from gross trade. Commercial calls immediately
   follow their individual cargo mutation, before another transaction.
   The module checks expected inventory.

   crew_food, deposits, withdrawals and other events are ignored. Food paid
   from earned crewPurse is not another owner expense.

   Monetary expense hooks have no transaction ID: root must avoid duplicate
   calls from overlapping transaction/lifecycle hooks.

6. daily refreshes metadata status only. Insolvent/inactive owners remain
   intact. capital funds the actual owner purse without changing damage,
   reactivating a lost fleet or spawning boats. Native lifecycle owns
   recovery/reactivation.

   Use owned_count for the 96-hull cap across player and company rosters,
   including sunk records. Keep core.capacity() limited to physical player
   hold; collection adds usable capacities of incoming nonsunk hulls.

7. Collection requires recall, player home harbor, owner.atPort==home,
   a holding patrol within 1 world unit of actual home coordinates,
   no remotely stored living hull, settled wage arrears, roster space
   and sufficient combined physical hold for remaining cargo.

   Same boat dictionaries return, including sunk records with flags and
   existing wreck references unchanged. No wreck is healed or relocated.
   Root must preserve existing wreck/UID references.

   Boats/cargo/purses transfer once; empty owner/patrol become inactive.
   Earned crew purse stays separate from company/player capital.

8. Validation:
   validate(state,{ports:port_definitions,hulls:hull_definitions})
   returns "" on success or an error string.

   Definitions accept ID-keyed dictionaries or arrays of IDs/{id:...}.
   Whole-valued JSON floats are accepted for integer fields through 2^53-1.
   Fractions in integer fields, NaN and infinity fail.
   Quantities may be finite fractions.

   Checks company owner/patrol/route/good/port references, global boat UID
   uniqueness, actual hull type IDs, account relationships and exact
   metadata keys. This supplements the existing root validator for world,
   loadout and noncompany state.

   ensure adds only empty shippingCompanies when absent. It never resets
   malformed existing data.

Accounting

NetProfit = revenue - proportional COGS - operating expenses.

Purchases remain inventory basis until sale/loss. Proportional cost allocation
uses whole coins; the final quantity carries the exact remaining cost.
Lost commercial cargo writes its remaining allocated basis into operating.

invested is capital deposited; withdrawn is actual owner cash returned;
returnedBasis is unsold goods returned in kind at cost. Deposits and retained
or returned inventory are not income. Crew purses are not capital investment,
revenue or owner withdrawals.

No dividends or arbitrary hull valuations. Only fleetOwners holds company
coins/crewPurse. Company metadata contains accounting totals, not another purse.

Maximum 8 open companies. Closed metadata persists under stable IDs and does
not consume an open slot. Each company transaction log is capped at 32 rows.

Tests

Self-contained SceneTree native script with synthetic core, Money, Politics
and capacity adapters. Failed checks produce a nonzero exit.

Coverage includes funded creation and proportional crew-purse conservation;
same boat identity, people, stores and refits; failure atomicity; harbor,
service, shore-person and last-flagship restrictions; missing cargo/limits;
8-open-company cap; price, quantity and reserve limits; physical recall,
dock, cargo-space and unpaid-crew checks; single collection; damaged-company
funding and sunk identity; malformed JSON-shaped saves; inventory, COGS,
operating expenses and capital versus profit.
