ROOT ACCEPTANCE REVIEW — v0.018 Regional campaigns
21 October 2026 | root integration of the existing Grok4.7 helper

Read the complete regional_campaigns.gd, helper tests and original report.
campaign-grok-regional.txt is retained unchanged. The helper did not run Godot.
Root owns the integrated implementation, test execution and Windows delivery.

The helper's finite-purse/no-spawn architecture was useful. Root rejected
instant corridor completion, a one-lot peace button, remote material delivery
and industry-name-only development. Root corrected a port-ID/dictionary
interface error caught during native integration.

Final implementation:
- Four optional goals with explicit chosen ports and treaty parties. Stable
  route/harbor identities preserve completions. Withdrawals retain physical
  donations and cannot duplicate once-only treasury grants.
- Actual Friendly, local government support goes to an existing patrol's purse,
  bounded by seven wage-days, a 14,000-coin ceiling and two public-payroll days
  retained ashore. No spawned company, free boat, battle or escrow reward.
- The existing patrol receives opposite-endpoint water routes; ordinary
  recovery, crew availability, supplies and paid repairs retain priority.
- Corridors need seven distinct consecutive clear economic days plus actual
  endpoint visits, a physical paid merchant passage or relevant player combat.
  Repeated same-day reads cannot advance time. Renewed threats reset progress.
- Harbor development combines twelve real owned-goods lots with three actual
  daily business payrolls at that selected port. Donated stock is not sold twice.
- Survey achievement reads three connected, fully surveyed original sites and
  can recognize work already earned; it never performs surveys for free.
- Peace needs five food/kit lots physically delivered to each party, Friendly
  standing with one side, access to both, no witnessed offenses/claims, and
  three elapsed published days for talks. Signing is an actual harbor action.
- A treaty changes one symmetric faction pair for thirty days. It neither
  changes player public standing nor forces a commission. Expiry restores the
  prior dispute only if newer diplomacy has not superseded neutrality.
- Actual contracts, sales and combat feed separate finite factual counters.
  NPC cargo used for its own tanks/racks is not a shipment: the adapter records
  only the actual successful paid sale quantity.
- Optional new books/focus/facts/grants/treaties are validated before restore;
  old campaigns receive harmless empty books. Bounded copied-port workers
  remain isolated and joined before next-day fleet decisions.

Verification includes unit regressions for finite funding, physical supplies,
duplicate progress/payment prevention, cooldown/expiry, cancellation and survey
roundtrips. Actual Main/campaign tests exercise paid harbor development, worker
publication, protected invalid-save rejection, the real goals UI, contract
receipts, NPC sale-versus-resupply distinction, patrol routing and restore.
A separate 180-day actual campaign run checks all ten ports, eight worker lanes,
finite stocks, conserved purses, business insolvency and three save roundtrips.
Full source and clean exact-tag results are in the verification report and
portable delivery receipts; no subjective or foreground-test claim is made.

Scope limits: bounded two-port regional watches, three business types and four
original authored chains, not a territorial conquest/ownership simulation.
There are 24 hull designs, numerical crews and cheap 2D water. Government
support does not guarantee a solvent fleet forever. Insolvent owners can wait
for affordable stocks, workers or replacement funding. New artistic content,
human play balance and GPU frame pacing are not claimed by these CPU checks.
