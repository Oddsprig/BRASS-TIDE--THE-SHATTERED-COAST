SCHEMA
port.training:
  schema: 1
  nextId: positive whole number
  qualified: sailor/gunner/mechanic whole-number counts
  jobs: array

Each job exposes root's required fields:
  id, role, count, progress, days, escrow, paused

Additional job fields:
  pauseReason, traineeWages, instructorWages, paidTrainee, paidInstructor,
  instructorRole, instructorCount, reservedMechanic, materials, materialCost

progress means elapsed course days, not a zero-to-one fraction.
IDs are port-local monotonic strings: training-1, training-2, etc.
Completed/cancelled jobs leave the array.

report returns detached qualified/jobs copies, ok/message, escrow,
trainingWorkers, and reservedInstructors.
report.escrow is derived display data, not another purse.

JSON SAVE CORRECTION
Whole-number validation accepts both integers and finite, exact whole floats.
It rejects booleans, strings, fractions, negative values, NaN, and infinity.
Supported whole-number range is zero through 9,000,000,000,000,000.
Existing malformed training is reported rather than silently replaced.

CONCRETE INPUT ASSUMPTIONS
Port fields are top-level numeric counters:
  workforce, availableWorkers, availableSpecialists, recruits,
  cash, householdPurse, expertPurse.
Stocks are port.stocks.food and port.stocks.supplies.
Government purse and publicStores are untouched.

port.cash is assumed to be the material merchant settlement purse.

Cancellation is assumed to receive the original payer's wallet.
This schema does not persist payer identity; root controls refund routing.

WORKER ACCOUNTING / ROOT HOOKS
Enrollment removes count trainees from workforce.
Those trainees then exist canonically in job.count.
Enrollment also reserves one existing available specialist as instructor.
The instructor remains within workforce, but leaves availableWorkers and
availableSpecialists until completion/cancellation.

Root's sync_workforce must preserve both reservations:
- workforce excludes active job.count trainees.
- availableWorkers excludes ordinary assigned workers and active instructors.
- availableSpecialists excludes active instructors.
Do not subtract trainees twice.
report.trainingWorkers and report.reservedInstructors expose these totals.

Initial qualifications are bounded subsets of existing recruits/specialists.
Pools are treated conservatively as disjoint; already eligible recruits,
specialists and gunners are not borrowed as unqualified trainees.

Completion returns existing trainees and releases the existing instructor.
Sailor completion adds recruit eligibility.
Mechanic completion adds specialist eligibility within unchanged total people.
Gunner completion adds gunner eligibility.
Cancellation grants no trainee qualification.
No existing ship crew records are modified.

Recruitment/hire integration:
1. Gate the requested role/count against qualified.
2. consume_qualified before removing canonical workforce/recruit counters.
3. Root performs the existing paid recruitment/hire operation atomically.
4. On a failed operation, restore eligibility while counters still permit it.
5. On disband/return, restore canonical people/counters before return_qualified.

The qualification helpers change eligibility only. They never add workforce,
recruits, specialists, money, or ship crew themselves.

MONEY / MATERIALS
Enrollment consumes finite food/supplies upfront and transfers their quoted
cost from wallet.coins to port.cash.
Remaining prepaid wages live only in job.escrow.
daily transfers earned trainee wages into householdPurse and instructor wages
into expertPurse, reducing escrow by exactly the same amount.
Cumulative earned wages are rounded down; completion settles exact totals.
Cancellation refunds only escrow actually held, removes the job, and returns
people once. Consumed materials and earned wages remain paid.

Money.port_total must include job escrow exactly once.
Use its existing direct sum OR escrow_total; never add both.
The module performs checked Dictionary transfers directly and does not assume
an undocumented Money.move ledger shape.
Root owns any central ledger/event recording.

COURSE TUNING ASSUMPTIONS
These are explicit candidate values, not claims about the unread detailed plan:
- sailor: 3 days, foreman wage bracket
- gunner: 4 days, mechanic wage bracket
- mechanic: 5 days, engineer wage bracket
- trainees: 100 coins per trainee-day
- one existing specialist instructor per active course
- food: one unit per trainee/instructor-day
- supplies: ceil(trainees * days * 0.25)
- maximum batch 100; maximum concurrent jobs 16

Instructor role names identify existing wage brackets. The supplied interface
exposes only an aggregate specialist count, so individual instructor profession
or identity is not fabricated.
Root must reconcile tuning and specialist semantics with its implementation.

VALIDATION / FAILURE BEHAVIOR
All transaction rejections precede financial/worker mutation.
ensure may initialize the training field before an unsuccessful first quote.
Invalid or underfunded state pauses progress and wages.
Malformed wage budgets and paid/progress mismatches are rejected, preventing
negative wage deltas or free completion.
Duplicate/stale IDs and malformed material receipts are rejected.
An otherwise structurally valid underfunded job can be cancelled, refunding
only its actual remaining coins.
Recipient limits pause/reject before overflow or partial payment.
