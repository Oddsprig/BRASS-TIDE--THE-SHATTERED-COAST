================================================================================
1. JSON WHOLE NUMBERS
================================================================================
version, nextItemSeq, lockerOrder, and mountCount now accept a finite whole
number whether it is stored as an int or as a float. Godot JSON.parse_string
restores whole counters as floats, and SaveValidation already treats that form
as legitimate. Strings, bools, fractions, NaN, and infinity are refused.
floor(n) == n is the whole test, so 1.5 is not truncated to 1 and a bad value
is not rewritten into the dictionary. There is no separate normalizer.
Refusal leaves the caller's dictionary as it was passed in. A corrupt saved
owner is still not deleted or reseeded.

The caps are unchanged and still exact in float64: serial and locker order
1..1000000, mount count 0..4, locker 256, items 263. This does not accept a
whole number above those bounds.

================================================================================
2. CUSTODY
================================================================================
collect_item_uids already walks every item key, locker and installed.
The new test plants one restored UID in one hull's locker and the same UID
on the other hull's mount1. Both owners are left unchanged, and the error is
the shared-part refusal rather than a schema failure.
acknowledge_hull_uid still does not store the new hull uid and does not
change birthNamespace, nextItemSeq, or item UIDs. The test does that on a
dictionary that has been through JSON.stringify / JSON.parse_string, then
swap_exact, transfer_exact, and repair_installed on that same restored owner.
Four mounts still seed. The 256 locker check in the older test is unchanged.

================================================================================
3. REPAIR RATES
================================================================================
Every suppliesPerHp entry must be a finite number strictly above zero.
Zero, negative, boolean, NaN, and infinite rates are refused before any hp
changes. A zero supply budget with a positive rate still heals nothing.
Omitting a family still skips that family. Armor and locker parts are still
not repaired. One work pool is still spent once.

work * hpPerWork or supplies / rate may overflow for finite inputs. That
overflow is treated as large enough to cover the remaining condition gap.
The published debit is still gain / hpPerWork and gain * rate. Those debits
must be finite, nonnegative, and no greater than the remaining budget apart
from 0.000001 of float dust. If they are not, the call rolls the owner back
and does not return NaN or infinity. A comfortable large case (1e20 budget,
1e6 rates) must heal inside the 0.75 jury cap and publish a positive finite
debit. The 1e308 case may either do the same or refuse with the owner unchanged.

This operation is still material repair. It does not perform firefighting
without supplies, and it still charges no coins.
