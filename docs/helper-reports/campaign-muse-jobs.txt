VOYAGE JOBS API (game/scripts/voyage_jobs.gd, all static, core = Campaign)
Runs beside contracts.gd: state.activeContract and state.freight are never
read for writing or modified, and no Contracts API is replaced.
- ensure(core) -> Array: normalizes state["voyageJobs"] (plain dicts, max 6
  kept) and state["doneVoyageJobs"] (unique string ids, newest 128 kept).
  Job fields: id,title,description,port,targetPort,kind,reward,escrow,
  payerKey ("cash"|"governmentPurse"),deadlineDay,acceptedDay,
  freight {good,qty} or {},state ("offered"|"active"|"expired"), plus
  siteId/ownerId ("" unless survey/bounty).
- available(core) -> Array: deterministic offers at the docked harbor only.
  Ids embed port+day (+site for survey) - "vfreight-<port>-<day>",
  "vsurvey-<port>-<day>-<siteId>", "vbounty-<port>-<day>" - so the same day
  never rerolls, and accepted/closed ids never reappear. Up to one of each:
  freight (first of supplies/food/timber/fuel the source port physically
  stocks, qty 6+day%5, destination a deterministic other port, payer cash),
  survey (one deterministic not-yet-surveyed exploration catalog site,
  payer governmentPurse), bounty (first actually active patrol-* raider
  fleetOwner with living boats, payer governmentPurse). An offer is listed
  only while its sponsor purse covers the whole reward.
- accept(core,id) -> Dictionary: max 6 active; re-derives the offer, checks
  sponsor harbor/funds, physical source stock and combined hold space
  BEFORE any write (atomic refusal). On success: source stocks -= freight
  qty, Money.move sponsor purse -> job.escrow for the full reward, job
  becomes active with acceptedDay. Survey acceptance sights the rumored
  site via Exploration.discover_near at its coordinates (level 1 knowledge
  only; no goods, coins, crew or later progress are granted).
- finish(core,id) -> Dictionary: pays job.escrow -> state.coins exactly
  once and closes the job into doneVoyageJobs. Conditions: freight at
  targetPort (target stocks += sealed qty); survey reported at the
  sponsoring port with Exploration level == 3 for job.siteId; bounty
  claimed at the sponsor with the named owner inactive or all boats sunk.
  Incomplete escrow refuses without paying.
- abandon(core,id) -> Dictionary: refunds remaining escrow to the sponsor
  purse and closes the job. Sealed freight unloads into the CURRENT
  harbor's stocks; abandoning at sea with freight is refused, never
  jettisoned or deleted.
- pending_freight_weight(state) -> float: total weight of all side-job
  freight, for root to fold into campaign cargo accounting.
Expiry (lazy, on accept/finish/abandon when state.day > deadlineDay): job
state becomes "expired", escrow refunds to the sponsor, freight remains
sealed in job.freight until harbor abandon unloads it; expired jobs can
never finish for pay. Deadlines: freight ceil(2x one-way days + 6), survey
ceil(2x + 14), bounty +20 days from the offer day.
Rewards are wages-derived like contracts.gd: freight = crew_count x 100
coins x max(1, one-way days) x 2 + Goods.cost(good); survey = 12 survey
crew-days (chart 2x1.0 + survey 4x2.5 from exploration.gd) x 100 x 2 plus
half the route-wage term; bounty = 45% of living enemy hull prices + 10
sailor-days, mirroring the existing side bounty. Capacity on accept =
core.capacity() - core.cargo_weight() (which already includes the story
contract's state.freight) - pending_freight_weight(state).

CONTACTS API (game/scripts/contacts.gd, all static)
- ensure(state, world = {}) -> Array: builds/normalizes state["contacts"]
  from four authored definitions whose port exists in world.PORTS/state
  ports, and state["marketSnapshots"] ({}). No visits or trust invented.
  Contacts: Mara Voss, shipwright, anvil_dock; Tobin Hale, convoy broker,
  brass_haven; Sister Wren, survey patron, reed_floats; Cass Rye, salvage
  agent, rust_anchorage. Record: {id,name,role,port,title,trust -100..100,
  met,lastVisitDay,events[<=32 ids],log[<=20 {day,event,amount,trust}]}.
- visit(state, port, day) -> Dictionary: the ONLY writer of market
  snapshots; stamps {day, cash, governmentPurse, stocks(all 16 goods)} for
  that port and marks its contact met/lastVisitDay. Returns copies.
- list_contacts(state) -> Array: deep copies sorted by id.
- record(state, contact_id, event_id, amount) -> Dictionary: applies a
  trust delta (clamped to +-50 per event, trust clamped -100..100) exactly
  once per event_id per contact; repeats return applied=false unchanged.
  Trust is personal per contact and fully separate from any faction rep
  (the v0.012 factions module is not read or written here).

ROOT INTEGRATION ASSUMPTIONS / NEEDS (intentionally not done here)
- Money.total must add every active job.escrow (root stated this); the test
  asserts conservation under exactly that assumption. Until then,
  Money.total alone undercounts by live side-job escrows.
- save_validation must validate voyageJobs (<=6 documented plain records,
  escrow == reward while active, payerKey, known ports/goods/site/owner),
  doneVoyageJobs (<=128 strings), contacts and marketSnapshots.
- campaign cargo accounting should add pending_freight_weight(state) to
  cargo_weight() (and then accept()'s local subtraction must be removed to
  avoid double counting - flagged in the module header). Sealed side
  freight must stay unsellable, like state.freight today.
- UI: a job board listing available() beside Contracts offers, active job
  list with deadline/state, finish/abandon buttons gated by the returned
  messages; contacts panel showing trust and snapshot day/age per port.
- Day ticks may call any mutating API to surface expiry promptly, or root
  can add its own expiry notice; escrow/freight semantics already hold
  whenever expiry is observed. Bounty completion relies on root battle
  settlement sinking/deactivating the named owner as it does today.
- Survey jobs depend on root having integrated exploration.gd progression
  (advance by game days); if a site is surveyed through another path first,
  the job can legitimately be reported and paid once.

CAVEATS / KNOWN SHORTCOMINGS
- One offer per kind per port per day is deliberate anti-reroll design;
  board depth (several freights per day) is root tuning if wanted.
- Bounty uses the first active patrol raider like contracts.gd does; both
  systems could name the same owner on the same day - root may want to
  suppress one board's bounty when the other is accepted.
- Survey reward assumes the full chart+survey crew-day cost even when a
  site is already charted; sponsors never pay for work already rewarded
  because each survey job id settles once, but root may rebalance.
- Expiry is lazy by design (no new tick hook invented); an expired job's
  escrow visibly refunds on the next accept/finish/abandon call.
