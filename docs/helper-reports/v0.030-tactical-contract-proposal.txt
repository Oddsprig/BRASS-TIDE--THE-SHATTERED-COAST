4. What certification actually proves

B checks swept footprint corridor geometry across ingress, firing pass AND exit,
including land/arrival spacing/occupied stations; no endpoint-only approval.
The detached _move-equivalent rollout checks ONLY first2s of turn/acceleration/
braking/fuel/condition feasibility. Long curved maneuvers are NOT physically
certified end-to-end by that rollout; later segments require recertification
before execution. Cached geometry carries no perpetual movement permission.

validUntil<=checkedAt+0.2s, and never later than intent expiry.
Before every helm/fire consumption check order/control/terrain/dynamics identity,
current pose against the predicted current-tick pose (<=2px position,
<=2px/s velocity, <=1degree heading), target legality and local allied clearance.
Deviation, collision or _avoid_land deflection invalidates; brake/replan.
Next snapshots may reuse an unexpired certificate only after those checks.
Current _avoid_land is local nine-heading steering; _collisions is overlap
separation; campaign Navigation is not battle routing. Nearest-only fallback
and cosmetic orbit cannot satisfy this contract.
