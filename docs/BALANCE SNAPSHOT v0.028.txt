The full native JSON records material, assembly and supervision bill components.
Ready hull prices do not include the ten-day crew advance or all voyage stores.
The detailed in-game yard bill traces these components. Capital and material basis
are not earned profit. The bounded eight-lot timber pressure-test charter records
its actual deficit; unprofitable routes require a different spread, volume or cost.
No nominal historical motorboat price or arbitrary starting hull price is asserted.
