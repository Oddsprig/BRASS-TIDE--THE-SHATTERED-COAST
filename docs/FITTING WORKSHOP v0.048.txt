BRASS TIDE v0.048 - FITTING WORKSHOP

Open Fleet, choose a boat, then open its fitting bay. Current and draft boats
use the actual interchangeable mounts. Select a numbered socket to compare
compatibility, firing arc, bearing, range, reload and shared ammunition use.
The Current / Draft / Change table shows handling, armor, hold, berths,
ammunition capacity, fuel use and the existing fleet reserve calculation.

Review the itemized yard bill before applying. Existing owned equipment,
condition, scarce harbor stock, materials, workers, specialists, wages, tax
and the yard's purse still determine the actual transaction. Coins move
through the existing economy. Fitting a larger rack buys no ammunition.
Integral pumps remain hull equipment; crew and repair kits do their real work.

Three Bastion cruiser examples show fast gun pressure, an armored cannon
anchor and an economical torpedo striker. Each uses current catalog equipment
and has explicit weaknesses. Browse them from any owned fitting bay; loading
one into a draft requires ownership of that hull. Loading is knowledge only;
the normal yard quote and payment are still required before installation.

Try current draft in Lab starts a repeatable isolated encounter with the
current owned hull and draft against the same opponent. Practice equipment
and finite stores stay in the Lab. Returning restores your voyage. Templates
save weapons, engine and armor. Automatic weapon holds and round reserves
last for a battle and use Selected tactics & weapon groups in the Lab.
