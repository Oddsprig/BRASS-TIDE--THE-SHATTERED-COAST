BRASS TIDE v0.046 - TACTICAL AI

Autopilot remains the default. Light boats seek separated firing passes;
strike boats use their fitted launcher range and turn away after real salvos.
Heavies hold useful firing water and can use secondary tubes without charging.
Escorts screen a carrier or wounded ally; unarmed support seeks protection.
Nearby allied assignments share pressure without overriding a direct order.

Gradual repairs and bailing no longer cancel a useful maneuver every tick.
Actual damage, disabled machinery, urgent danger and new orders still matter.
Ordinary autopilot checks hostile hull space and seeks a separating exit from
a close pass. Deliberate boarding and manual contact remain available.
When a route is unsafe or blocked, the existing selected-boat information
explains the wait; an AI plan does not grant impossible clearance or free shots.

PRACTICE FROM THE MAIN MENU
Open Fleet battle laboratory and choose one of the six named practices:
- Flank a heavy: two light gun boats against a heavy cannon boat.
- Escort against raiders: protect actual loaded cargo reaching an eastern exit.
- Fitted strike pass: a small torpedo boat against a heavy cannon.
- Pressured withdrawal: one labelled damaged starter seeks an escape.
- Cover a wounded ally: an escort protects a labelled damaged boat.
- Mixed roles and rear support: heavy, light, strike and civilian boats together.

The two damaged practices begin after one actual shell hit, disclosed in their
guidance. Crews, repair kits, fuel and ammunition remain finite. These are
isolated practices and grant no campaign reward. Edited fits use normal rules.
WASD camera; Space pause; select a boat and right-click for orders; U toggles
manual flagship control; Escape opens the menu.
