BRASS TIDE v0.067 - THE GLASSWIND EXPEDITION

Prepare crew, cargo space, food, survey kits and the return allowance before
leaving home. Sail through a coastal approach into the ocean, then enter
Glasswind Keys. Approach the island shores to notice landings.

Chart a sighted landing, then survey it. Shore parties need spare sailors above
the boat minimums; assigned hands stay ashore, wages continue, and tools and
rations are paid. Keep survey kits in cargo. The quote shows actual crew,
time and stores. Recall the party or finish its work before sailing onward.

Read the bearing you earned and investigate the next island yourself. A clue
does not reveal every landing or add goods to your hold. Survey all three
sites to identify the collapsed supply coaster's surviving cargo.

The cache is finite: one engine assembly, eight copper lots, twelve repair kits
and sixteen plank bundles. Its64hold units may exceed your free space. Choose
a good and quantity, review crew work, kits, wages, food and space, then commit.
Unrecovered goods stay there. This collapsed cache yields cargo, not a new hull
or weapon fitting. Recovery uses normal time; an interruption is still real.

Open return preparation and sail the approach/ocean/home legs with your existing
boats. Carry recovered goods home for ordinary market trade; prices and coins
come from the existing economy. Discoveries do not grant a free coin reward.

Add music to music/. WASD moves the camera; flagship autopilot is the default.
