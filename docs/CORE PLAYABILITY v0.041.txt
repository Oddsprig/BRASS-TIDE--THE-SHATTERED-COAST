WHAT CHANGED

terrain, visible harbor buildings/piers and fleet symbols at normal zoom.

consistent directional shading; heavy warboats use painted steel decks.

Coastal Clash starts directly from the title: screen one loaded packet,
fight nearby mixed raiders, and reach the marked physical exit. There is
no timed objective win or campaign payout. The voyage remains separate.

future-damage replays and duplicate route work. Real steering, collisions,
weapon arcs, ammunition, fuel, component damage and crew repairs remain live.
Water stays inexpensive. No animated crew or new economy subsystem.
