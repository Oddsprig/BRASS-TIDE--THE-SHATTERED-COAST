SUPPLIED HARBOR CONTROL

One known generated harbor on the existing merchant corridor can receive a
funded control operation. The corridor must connect two different island groups.
An actual defending fleet of the current governor must be nearby on that harbor's
chart. A distant patrol or a fleet on another chart cannot establish a claim.

Stop within40water units of the remote berth. Earn Friendly standing with the
sponsor, who must already be at war with the current governor; that governor
must be hostile to you. Your two healthy spare sailors remain attached to their
original boats. The800-coin operating contribution goes to this harbor's treasury.
Each of two fully paid support days delivers two food and one repair-kit lot from
your hold. Ordinary fleet rations and100coins per sailor-day remain additional.

Defeat the selected actual defender, finish the supplied paid work and negotiate
explicitly at the berth. Fleet presence or a missing defender is not a victory.
Leaving, missing stores, unpaid wages, lost crew or peace interrupts the work.
Withdraw recalls the original sailors; actual wages, delivered goods and treasury
support remain spent. Resume the same operation to retain its paid progress.

The surrender changes this harbor's governor and access rules. It does not annex
an island or island group. Existing businesses, employees, machinery, goods,
private purses and paid warehouse receipts remain with their actual owners.
Services use the new governor's actual access rules and their existing charges.
A previous completed home-harbor control receipt is retained when starting the
one remote operation. Further remote conquests are outside this bounded scope.

The operations panel records the witnessed day, old governor, sponsor, paid days
and interruption reason. Distant traffic keeps its existing dated intelligence;
its surviving boats continue ordinary funded objectives and paid recovery.
