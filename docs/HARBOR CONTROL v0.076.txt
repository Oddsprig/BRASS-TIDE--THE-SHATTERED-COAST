BRASS TIDE v0.076 — HARBOR CONTROL

Press L, choose Support a harbor industry, then use Harbor control at the top.
Choose an existing Shatter Coast harbor. Generated harbors keep their limited
services in this version.

Bring recovered machine tools, the warehouse materials and two healthy sailors
above minimum crew. The quote shows the warehouse bill, working capital and
fleet payroll. Starting donates the carried materials to harbor stocks. After
two fully paid local work days, the ordinary warehouse setup charges its bill,
hires local workers and receives its working capital. Tools stay aboard and
retain wear. Ordinary sailor pay is 100 coins per person-day.

For a contest, earn Friendly standing with a sponsoring faction at war with the
current hostile governor. An actual defending fleet must be present. Funding
moves 800 coins into the existing harbor treasury. Each of two paid support
days also delivers two food and one repair-kit lot. Win the selected physical
contest, then choose Negotiate surrender.

Missing pay, supplies, healthy sailors or the hostile mandate interrupts work.
Withdraw recalls the original party. Spent goods, wages, support and wear remain
spent; resuming the same operation keeps its progress.

One harbor changes authority. Private businesses, workers, boats, stores and
purses retain their records. Islands and entire groups are not annexed.
