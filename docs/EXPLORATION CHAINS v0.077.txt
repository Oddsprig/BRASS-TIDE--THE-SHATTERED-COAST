BRASS TIDE v0.077 - EXPLORATION CHAINS

Two new journal leads sit beside the four already there: Middle Sea Chart,
Ironwood for the Mill, The Toll Camp, and The Cold Workshop. Those four
are unchanged. Neither new lead pays you coins, a boat, crew, or goods
by itself.

THE INVERTED TIDE BOOK
Meet Sister Wren at Reedwater Floats and hear the lead while stopped
there. Wren's harbor is the Reedwater Commune. A new standing book starts
Reed at 0, neutral, so ordinary service is legal.

Chart the Verdigris Salt Pans (3700, 5400) to charted. Copying the
salt-staff marks records that chart and earns Middle Marsh Landing
(reed_marsh_landing, 5050, 5250). Fully survey the marsh and record the
matched scales. No freight exists yet.

Return to Reedwater Floats and choose one register. Public gives Reed +4,
Compact -2, and Wren +6, and may unlock one passage at Reedwater Floats.
Bonded gives Compact +4, Reed -2, Tobin +6, and Wren -3, and may unlock
one passage at Brass Haven. The other register stays closed. The chain
ends on this choice.

ONCE-FUNDED FREIGHT
Public id chainfreight-tide-public077 moves four repair kits from
Reedwater Floats to Silt Hollow. Bonded id chainfreight-tide-bonded077
moves four repair kits from Brass Haven to Silt Hollow. It is listed only
at that origin, only while the origin still has four supplies, a route
exists, and the harbor treasury can pay the kit invoice plus the passage
escrow. It is absent when the other tide flag is also set, when that id
is already in usedOffers, or when the job is already on your voyage list.

Accepting it is a normal voyage job. Four supplies leave the origin
stocks, the treasury pays their invoice into harbor cash, and the passage
fee is escrowed. Your working holds must fit the sealed weight. Delivery
at Silt Hollow puts those four kits into Silt Hollow stocks and releases
the escrow. Accepting stores the id in usedOffers, so delivery, abandon,
or a later restore does not offer it again. Abandon unloads the sealed
kits at the harbor where you abandon. This is not a gift and not a
guaranteed fee. The older public and private chart couriers stay separate.

THE LAST SOUNDINGS
Meet Tobin Hale at Brass Haven. The Compact also starts at 0, neutral.
Fully survey, in order, Westglass Bearing Stones (1360, 1380), the
Windglass Keeper's Log (2780, 1540), and the Longglass Supply Wreck
(1820, 1640). Each record earns the next bearing. All three are in the
Glasswind Keys.

The cache is not its own exploration site. After those three surveys are
rewarded at level 3, and only on the Glasswind local chart, the existing
seeder can place one abandoned cache on the south wreck: one engine
assembly, eight copper, twelve repair kits, and sixteen timber.
Components stay empty. Reaching level 3 does not pay you and does not
load that cargo.

While the cache stage is active, one completed explicit cargo lot from
that wreck sets paid_longglass077. The receipt must have ok and completed
as real bools, a positive quantity, and source
site:glasswind_keys:abandoned_cache067. The receipt you see is unchanged.
A failed or interrupted load does not count. Other fleets working the
Shatter wreck field do not count. The flag is written once.

You then take that paid record to Wren or to Cass. Those are two separate
harbor stages.

With no paid flag, you may instead record exhaustion. That needs the three
surveys at level 3, the cache tombstone set, the wreck row present, and
every store and component at or below zero. A missing wreck, a negative
figure, or stock still aboard is refused. Exhaustion sets
empty_longglass077 and grants nothing. It does not refill the pile.

TURN-IN
Wren's turn-in is at Reedwater Floats and uses the same service permit as
other harbor work. Reed +4, Raiders -3, and Wren +6 apply only after that
permit has already succeeded.

Cass Rye is at Rust Anchorage. Raiders start at -70, which is hostile, and
hostile standing refuses entry. Service there needs ordinary legal access:
paid safe conduct, or standing that is no longer hostile. Cass's register
is Raiders +4, Reed -3, and Cass +6, and it is applied only after that
permit succeeds. From the starting -70, +4 is still hostile. Neither
register grants harbor entry, recovered goods, machinery, or pay.

KNOWN BEARINGS
Journal leads carry regionId. A home site with no authored region resolves
to shatter_coast. Glasswind sites keep glasswind_keys. The cache lead is
the south-wreck row, with the cache source id attached, once the seeded
wreck exists. Site reflections read only a saved survey level and the
flags already on the chain.

Every earned lead is also stamped layer local. On the home chart the
comparison layer is home, so a Shatter Coast lead fails that check. Its
button opens voyage preparation for the Shatter Coast even while you are
already there, and it does not open the site panel. The separate Chart
control looks the site up again and can plot a home coordinate on the
home chart. A Glasswind lead opens regional navigation from home, and the
site panel when you are on the Glasswind local chart.

SAVE
Each chain keeps stage, done, flags, and leads in stories.chains. New
flag strings and one chainfreight id fit the current save check: up to 16
chain records, unread flag members, and at most 8 usedOffers. No separate
ledger is added. A depleted cache stays depleted. A used freight id stays
used.
