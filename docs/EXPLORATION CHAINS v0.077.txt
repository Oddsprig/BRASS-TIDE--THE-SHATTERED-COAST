BRASS TIDE v0.077 - EXPLORATION CHAINS

Two new journal leads sit beside Middle Sea Chart, Ironwood for the Mill,
The Toll Camp, and The Cold Workshop. Those four are unchanged. Neither
new lead pays you coins, a boat, crew, or goods by itself.
Listed reputation changes are base amounts; ordinary rival reactions also apply.

THE INVERTED TIDE BOOK
Meet Sister Wren at Reedwater Floats and hear the lead while stopped
there. Her harbor is the Reedwater Commune. A new standing book starts
Reed at 0, neutral, so ordinary service is legal.

Chart the Verdigris Salt Pans (3700, 5400) to charted. Copying the
salt-staff marks earns Middle Marsh Landing (reed_marsh_landing,
5050, 5250). Fully survey it and record the matched scales. No freight
exists yet.

Return to Reedwater Floats and choose one register. Public gives Reed +4,
Compact -2, and Wren +6, and may unlock one passage there. Bonded gives
Compact +4, Reed -2, Tobin +6, and Wren -3, and may unlock one passage at
Brass Haven. The other register stays closed. The chain ends on this choice.

ONCE-FUNDED FREIGHT
Public id chainfreight-tide-public077 is four repair kits from Reedwater
Floats to Silt Hollow. Bonded id chainfreight-tide-bonded077 is four
repair kits from Brass Haven to Silt Hollow. It is listed only at that
origin, only while four supplies, a route, and enough treasury for the
kit invoice plus the passage escrow all exist. It is absent if the other
tide flag is set, if that id is already in usedOffers, or if the job is
already on your voyage list.

Accepting it is a normal voyage job. Four supplies leave the origin
stocks, the treasury pays their invoice into harbor cash, and the passage
fee is escrowed. Your holds must fit the sealed weight. Delivery at Silt
Hollow adds those kits to Silt Hollow stocks and releases the escrow.
Accepting stores the id in usedOffers, so delivery, abandon, or restore
does not offer it again. Abandon unloads the kits where you abandon.
This is not a gift and not a guaranteed fee. Older chart couriers stay
separate offers.

THE LAST SOUNDINGS
Meet Tobin Hale at Brass Haven. The Compact also starts at 0, neutral.
Fully survey, in order, Westglass Bearing Stones (1360, 1380), the
Windglass Keeper's Log (2780, 1540), and the Longglass Supply Wreck
(1820, 1640). Each record earns the next bearing. All three sites are in
the Glasswind Keys.

The cache is not its own site. After those three surveys are rewarded at
level 3, and only on the Glasswind local chart, the existing seeder can
place one abandoned cache on the south wreck: one engine assembly, eight
copper, twelve repair kits, and sixteen timber. Components stay empty.
Reaching level 3 does not pay you or load that cargo.

While the cache stage is active, one completed explicit lot from that
wreck sets paid_longglass077. The receipt must have ok and completed as
real bools, a positive quantity, and source
site:glasswind_keys:abandoned_cache067. The receipt you see is unchanged.
A failed load does not count, and neither does another fleet on the
Shatter wreck field. The flag is written once. You then take that record
to Wren or to Cass as two separate harbor stages.

With no paid flag, you may record exhaustion instead. That needs the three
surveys at level 3, the cache tombstone set, the wreck row present, and
every store and component at or below zero. A missing wreck, a negative
figure, or stock still aboard is refused. Exhaustion sets
empty_longglass077, grants nothing, and does not refill the pile.

TURN-IN
Wren's turn-in is at Reedwater Floats and uses the same service permit as
other harbor work. Reed +4, Raiders -3, and Wren +6 apply only after that
permit has already succeeded.

Cass Rye is at Rust Anchorage. Raiders start at -70, hostile, and hostile
standing refuses entry. Service needs paid safe conduct or standing that
is no longer hostile. Cass's register is Raiders +4, Reed -3, and Cass +6,
applied only after that permit succeeds. From -70, +4 is still hostile.
Neither register grants entry, goods, machinery, or pay.

KNOWN BEARINGS
Leads carry regionId. A home site with no authored region resolves to
shatter_coast. Glasswind sites keep glasswind_keys. The cache lead is the
south-wreck row, with the cache source attached, once that wreck is seeded.
Reflections read only a saved survey level and flags already on the chain.

Home bearings carry the home layer; distant-group bearings carry local.
An earned bearing on the current chart opens its site panel. A bearing on
another chart opens the existing voyage preparation window. Chart controls
resolve the actual region before plotting; foreign coordinates are not
plotted on the current chart.
SAVE
Each chain keeps stage, done, flags, and leads in stories.chains. New flag
strings and one chainfreight id fit the current check: up to 16 chain
records, unread flag members, and at most 8 usedOffers. No separate ledger
is added. A depleted cache stays depleted. A used freight id stays used.
