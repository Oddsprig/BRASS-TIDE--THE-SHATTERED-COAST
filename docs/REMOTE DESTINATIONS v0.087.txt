BRASS TIDE v0.087 - DISTINCT REMOTE DESTINATIONS

Three destination packages sit alongside the existing coastal stories and
v0.077 exploration chains (`inverted_tide077`, `last_soundings077`) without
altering those six earlier chains. In a fresh or saved 48-group seeded ocean
career (`sector069`), `Stories.ensure()` deterministically binds three
distinct unvisited remote groups plus one unvisited frontier group from the
campaign seed into `state.stories.destinations087`. None of the three leads
mints coins, hulls, crew, or cargo by itself; outward preparation uses ordinary
harbor provisions, recruitment, and funded `VoyageJobs`.

Listed reputation changes are base amounts; ordinary rival reactions also apply.
Because `Factions.adjust` stores execution IDs truncated to 80 characters,
v0.087 story consequence IDs use `"story087:" + effect_id.sha256_text()` when
posting to the faction book while keeping human-readable IDs in the journal.

1. THE BROKEN MERIDIAN (`broken_meridian087`)
Meet Sister Wren at Reedwater Floats. Her harbor is the Reedwater Commune.
Hearing the lead enters the first bound remote landing (`firstSiteId` in
`firstRegionId`) into `leads`. It is not sighted while the fleet remains in
Shatter Coast; it is sighted only when the expedition reaches that remote local
chart (`Exploration.discover_near` checks `_site_region(site) == current_region`).

Chart the first meridian post to level 2 (`chart_first_meridian_post`). You
then choose how to commit your remaining reserves:
- `push_farther_meridian087`: Commit provisions and crew time to the outer
  counter-post (`secondSiteId` in `secondRegionId`). Only this choice appends
  the second remote landing to `leads`. Fully survey that second remote landing
  to level 3 (`survey_outer_meridian_post`) and record the paired sight
  (`record_paired_meridian087`, setting `meridian_full_survey087`).
- `turn_home_dated087`: Turn home within safe return reserves after the first
  station (`meridian_turned_home087`). The second remote landing is never added
  to `leads` and never sighted by the story.

Return to Reedwater Floats (`file_meridian_return`):
- `publish_full_meridian087` (requires `meridian_full_survey087`): Reed +4,
  Compact -2, Wren +7; sets `meridian_public087` and may unlock one once-only
  four-kit sight passage at Reedwater Floats (`chainfreight-meridian-public087`
  to Silt Hollow).
- `retain_full_meridian087` (requires `meridian_full_survey087`): Wren -3,
  Tobin +5; sets `meridian_retained087` and may unlock one once-only four-kit
  factor passage at Brass Haven (`chainfreight-meridian-retained087` to Silt
  Hollow).
- `file_partial_meridian087` (requires `meridian_turned_home087`): Reed +2,
  Wren +3; sets `meridian_partial_filed087` and closes the chain honestly
  without unlocking outer follow-on freight.

2. THE UNFINISHED KEEL (`unfinished_keel087`)
Meet Mara Voss at Anvil Dock. Hearing the lead enters one bound unvisited
remote landing (`siteId` in `regionId`) into `leads`.

Sail to that remote group and complete a full level-3 shore survey
(`survey_stranded_slipway`). Charting to level 2 does not spawn the slipway
cradle. When the live level-3 survey completes while `unfinished_keel087` is
active, `StoryOps.refresh()` calls `Salvage.seed_keel087`, which seeds at most
one finite cargo-only workboat cradle (`sourceId`:
`"site:unfinished_keel087:" + siteId`, tombstone in `state.wreckSeededSources`)
on that remote local chart with finite stores:
- 1 `engines`, 6 `iron`, 8 `supplies`, 12 `timber` (weight 54.0, empty
  `components`, no hull shell or equipment UIDs).

Record the slipway manifest (`inspect_keel_manifest087`, setting
`keel_surveyed087`), then weigh recovery (`decide_keel_recovery`):
- Paid recovery: Use normal `Campaign.recover_wreck(id, "strip", good, qty)`
  through `Remote067`. That recovery requires spare able sailors above minimum
  crew, 0.25 repair kit (`supplies`), hold capacity, elapsed working days, and
  funded daily wages (`100` coins/sailor-day) and food. Completing at least one
  positive lot sets `paid_keel_source087` once via `StoryOps.after_recovery077`
  and enables `log_paid_keel_strip087` (`keel_recovered_paid087`).
- `leave_keel_for_margin087`: Available only while no paid lot has been taken
  and positive stock remains on the cradle. Leaves the heavy frame on the
  slipway (`keel_left_intact087`) to preserve hold and ration margins.
- `record_exhausted_keel087`: Available only when no paid lot was taken, the
  slipway landing is surveyed to level 3, the tombstone is set, the canonical
  wreck row is present, and every store and component is at or below zero. A
  missing wreck or remaining stock is refused. Sets `keel_verified_empty087`.

Return to Anvil Dock (`settle_keel_ledger`):
- `close_forge_keel_ledger087`: Forge +4, Raiders -2, Mara +6
  (`keel_ledger_forge087`).
- `keep_private_keel_notes087`: Mara -3, Cass +5 (`keel_ledger_private087`).
Remaining slipway stores stay finite and depleted across save/restore; the
tombstone prevents refilling or re-seeding.

3. TWO-LANTERN ANCHORAGE (`two_lantern_anchorage087`)
Meet Tobin Hale at Brass Haven. Hearing the lead binds one unvisited frontier
group that has both a landing (`siteId`) and a generated frontier harbor
(`portId`).

Sail to that frontier local chart, chart the headland spit alignment to level 2
(`chart_lantern_headland`), and record the shifted range
(`record_lantern_alignment087`, setting `lantern_headland_charted087`).

Dock at the bound frontier harbor (`supply_lantern_anchorage` / ` settle_lantern_channel`;
`Stories._at_port` and `StoryOps.choose` honor `work_port070()` on remote/frontier
local charts with normal faction service permission):
- `deliver_lantern_kits087`: Transfers 4 `supplies` (repair kits) out of
  `state.cargo.supplies` and adds 4 `supplies` into the frontier harbor's
  `stocks.supplies`, recording Tobin +4 (`supplied_lantern_berth087`).
- `post_open_lantern_notice087`: Reed +4, Compact -2, Wren +5
  (`lantern_channel_public087`).
- `bind_local_lantern_desk087`: Compact +4, Reed -2, Tobin +6
  (`lantern_channel_bonded087`), and may unlock one once-only four-kit
  replacement passage at Brass Haven (`chainfreight-lantern-bonded087` to Silt
  Hollow).

ONCE-FUNDED FOLLOW-ON FREIGHT
`chainfreight-meridian-public087` (Reedwater Floats -> Silt Hollow),
`chainfreight-meridian-retained087` (Brass Haven -> Silt Hollow), and
`chainfreight-lantern-bonded087` (Brass Haven -> Silt Hollow) each move 4
`supplies` through `VoyageJobs`. They appear only at their origin harbor when
the matching outcome flag is set, the rival flag on that chain is absent, the
offer ID is not in `storyRights.usedOffers`, the origin harbor has at least 4
`supplies`, a valid water route exists, and the origin harbor's
`governmentPurse` covers both the material invoice and the passage escrow.

VISITED LANDINGS, LEGACY TWO-REGION SAVES & SAVE VALIDATION
- If a player visits a bound landing before starting its v0.087 chain,
  `Stories.ensure()` rebinds that unstarted package to another unvisited
  remote/frontier landing (`level == 0`). If no unvisited candidate remains,
  that unstarted package is marked `available: false` and omitted from
  `Stories.available(c)`.
- Legacy two-region saves (saves without `sector069`) are never rerolled or
  mutated into 48-group worlds. Their `destinations087` state is recorded as
  `bound: false, available: false, status: "unavailable_legacy_two_region"`,
  and none of `CHAINS087` are offered.
- Raw save validation (`Salvage.validate_expedition067` ->
  `Salvage.validate_keel087`) refuses forged or duplicate slipway wrecks,
  missing tombstones, over-cap slipway stores, legacy saves claiming bound 087
  destinations, or turned-home meridian saves that also claim the second lead.
