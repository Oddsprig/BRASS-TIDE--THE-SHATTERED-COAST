BRASS TIDE v0.040 - COMBAT FEEDBACK AND SOUND

EQUIPMENT CONDITION
Damaged fitted guns, engines and integral pumps show an amber broken slash.
Disabled equipment shows a red cross. These marks follow actual installed
equipment and its mount, engine bay or cabin/bilge location. Crew repairs
update the marks from the same equipment condition used by combat. Distant
boats omit these small marks. Ordinary hull damage, motors and sinking keep
their existing drawing and numerical systems.

WATER AND IMPACTS
Wood throws splinters, armor throws sparks and misses throw water plumes.
This pass adjusts their existing geometry and contrast. Stern foam follows
the traveled path behind the modeled machinery, including long civilian
boats. The wake remains a bounded group of inexpensive strokes; there is
no water simulation or offset hull reflection.

RECORDED WEAPON MIX
The existing recorded weapon and impact bank remains in use. Audio credits
and the source manifest accompany the build. The mix controls repeated
rapid-fire reports and contacts so larger weapon impacts and sinking cues
can remain audible. Mute, volume, distance attenuation and the existing
voice pool remain in effect. No new sound downloads or synthetic pings.

VERIFICATION LIMITS
The selected checks exercise actual damage, repair, event scheduling and
native drawing calls. Captured primitive frames can be rasterized in the
background for a model and motion review. These previews are distinct from
a GPU gameplay recording. Headless silent tests do not establish subjective
audio quality or displayed frame rate. See tests/RESULTS.txt for the actual
completed batch and its source receipt.
