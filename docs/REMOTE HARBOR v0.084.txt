REMOTE HARBOR DEVELOPMENT AND SERVICES (v0.084)
================================================

Scope
-----
v0.084 extends the v0.076 paid harbor warehouse development (`Territorial076.improve`) and
the resulting finite warehouse/berth storage service (`Home` warehouse operations) to an
eligible generated harbor (`sector069`, such as `settled_01_harbor_01`), while preserving
every finite setup bill, workforce/specialist requirement, wage transfer, and supplied
material credit.

Authoritative Docking and Local Permission
------------------------------------------
1. Presence Gate (`Territorial076._improve_supported` and `Territorial076._improve_near`):
   - An eligible remote harbor must be a registered `sector069.harbors` port with a live
     `state.ports` account, an explicit non-home `regionId` (`!= "shatter_coast"`), and a
     valid faction owner (`compact`, `forge`, `reed`, or `raiders`).
   - Remote development presence requires an empty fleet path, `world064.layer == "local"`,
     `world064.regionId == port.regionId`, `Campaign.work_port070()` (via
     `ShippingFlow068.remote_gate`) resolving that exact harbor ID, and physical berth
     distance below the existing remote service limit (`40.0` water units).
   - Calling from the `open_ocean` layer, from another region (`shatter_coast`), while
     moving on a plotted local course, or while moored at a sibling harbor in the same
     island group (`settled_01_harbor_02` in `settled_01`) is refused.

2. Local Harbor Access (`Politics.permit` and `Home._service_port`):
   - `Campaign.current_port()` retains its existing Shatter Coast service boundary.
   - `Home._service_port(core, true)` and `Politics.permit(core, "service", "", port)`
     evaluate `Politics.access(core, port)` against the specific target harbor's owning
     faction and port-keyed `safeConduct[port.id]`.
   - Mixed-faction island groups (`settled_01`) do not share service permits: a sibling
     harbor's governor or `safeConduct` never authorizes warehouse development or storage
     at another harbor in the group.

Finite Setup Bills, Coexistence, and Usable Service
---------------------------------------------------
1. Two-Day Berth Work and Single Material Credit:
   - Remote warehouse development requires two healthy spare sailors (`crewByBoat`),
     carried `machine_tools` (`0.01` wear per paid day), carried construction materials
     (`4` timber, `3` logs, `1` iron), `0.5` repair kits per paid day, and fully paid
     ordinary fleet payroll and rations across two distinct economic days.
   - Withdraw the shore party before sailing. Withdrawal releases the original sailors and
     retains accrued `paidDays` and `reservedMaterials` without recreating or double-charging
     materials upon return. An active or interrupted reserved party can reload only at its
     exact generated work berth, with its validated book, original healthy crew and machinery.
   - On Day 2 completion, `Territorial076.daily` supplies the reserved materials to
     `Home.quote` / `Home.establish`, records `grossCost`, `materialCredit`, and `netCost`,
     and credits `materialCost` once from the harbor's `cash` purse. Direct calls to
     `Home.establish` at a generated harbor without completing the two-day berth work
     are refused.

2. Coexistence with Completed v0.076 Home Warehouse (`priorCompleted`):
   - Starting a second warehouse development at a different eligible harbor after completing
     the first preserves the completed operation receipt in `improvement.priorCompleted`
     (validated by `Territorial076.validate` and surfaced by `Territorial076.view` for
     that prior harbor) and keeps the existing `state.businesses` warehouse intact.

3. Usable Remote Storage and Ongoing Payroll:
   - Once established at the generated harbor, the remote warehouse pays its two local
     laborers daily through `Home.tick` even while the player is away.
   - When docked back at that specific generated harbor with legal service access, the
     player can deposit and withdraw cargo (`Home.transfer_goods`), manage working capital
     (`Home.capital`). Existing labor-policy and negotiation controls remain home-only;
     ordinary remote warehouse payroll continues while the player is away.

Explicit Boundaries (Deferred to v0.085+)
-----------------------------------------
- Harbor/island sovereignty contests (`Territorial076.start` / `control`) remain strictly
  Shatter Coast home-only (`_home` and `_near`) until v0.085. Completing a warehouse
  development never alters any harbor's or island group's faction ownership.
- Salvage yard and workshop charters remain Shatter Coast home-only.
- Boat storage berths, refit and salvage services are not unlocked at generated harbors.
- No generated sector reroll or visited asset endowments are introduced.
