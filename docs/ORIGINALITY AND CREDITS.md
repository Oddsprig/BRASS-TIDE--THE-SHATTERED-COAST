# Originality and credits

Brass Tide: The Shatter Coast is an original motorboat fleet game. Blood Wake inspired coastal gunboat action; Starsector inspired broad fleet/campaign structure, numerical crew and economic interdependence. Neither is affiliated with or endorses this project.

World geometry, islands, settlements, factions, story, hull names, data, code-drawn boats/villages, UI art, icon and generated sounds were made for this project. No ripped screenshots/models/textures/audio/maps/code/story are included. General naval roles do not reproduce either game's protected expression. The coast is original rather than a close recreation of Blood Wake's map. Renaming alone would not clear a close copy; no commercial trademark clearance for this proposed title has been performed.

The user directed requirements. Codex led implementation/integration; one Sol6.1 specialist handled damage/combat and finance review; two Argon High BigBridge helpers handled regional production and construction/world systems. Raw helper reports remain in docs/helper-reports, with root verdict separate.

Godot4.7.2 powers the Windows executable. Exact engine/component copyright and full license texts are in docs/legal. [Godot licensing guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/complying_with_licenses.html). The game requests installed Windows typefaces through SystemFont; no Windows font files are bundled. ECONOMY.md cites the limited historical/boat-scale references; motors/guns and their prices are fictional.
