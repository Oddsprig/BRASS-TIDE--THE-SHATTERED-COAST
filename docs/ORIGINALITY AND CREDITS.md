# Originality and credits

Brass Tide: The Shatter Coast is an original motorboat fleet game. Blood Wake inspired coastal gunboat action; Starsector inspired broad fleet/campaign structure, numerical crew and economic interdependence. Neither is affiliated with or endorses this project.

World geometry, islands, settlements, factions, story, hull names, data, code-drawn boats/villages, UI art and icon were made for this project. From v0.004 the weapon/material/water effects use edited CC0 recording previews, credited in docs/audio/RECORDED AUDIO CREDITS.txt with original pages and hashes in game/assets/sfx/manifest.json. The quiet engine bed is an original procedural asset and is identified separately. Music MP3s are owner-supplied Lyria outputs; music/MANIFEST.json records their hashes. No ripped screenshots/models/textures/audio/maps/code/story are included. The coast is original rather than a close recreation of Blood Wake's map. No commercial trademark clearance for this proposed title has been performed.

The user directed requirements. Codex led implementation/integration; one Sol6.1 specialist handled damage/combat and finance review; two Argon High BigBridge helpers handled regional production and construction/world systems. Raw helper reports remain preserved in the consolidation cold archive (docs/ARCHIVE INDEX.txt); the current helper-reports directory points there, with root verdict separate.

For v0.003–v0.006 the owner changed the team to root plus two Argon High helpers only. Later helper work and root corrections are recorded in the version verdicts; earlier contributors remain credited for preserved work.

Godot4.7.2 powers the Windows executable. Exact engine/component copyright and full license texts are in docs/legal. [Godot licensing guidance](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/complying_with_licenses.html). The game requests installed Windows typefaces through SystemFont; no Windows font files are bundled. ECONOMY.md cites the limited historical/boat-scale references; motors/guns and their prices are fictional.
