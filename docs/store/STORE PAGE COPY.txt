TITLE
Brass Tide — The Shatter Coast

CATALOG DESCRIPTION
Motorboat fleet combat, trading and island exploration. Windows PC.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A single-player motorboat fleet game with trading, weapon fitting and island
exploration. Crew wages, fuel, supplies and repairs are paid in bronze coins.

ABOUT THE GAME
Take harbor jobs, move goods between settlements and put the proceeds into
boats and equipment. Cargo capacity, fuel, supplies and crew wages all affect
what a voyage can earn. Prices and wages use bronze coins. Harbor fitting quotes list materials and
paid yard labor.

Choose from 36 existing boat designs, including gunboats, fishing boats,
transports, tankers and yachts. Fit compatible machine guns, cannons, rockets
and torpedoes to weapon mounts, then compare engine and armor options. A
larger fleet gives you more choices, while each battle has a 300-point
deployment limit per side.

Your flagship starts on autopilot. Give fleet orders or take manual control
while the rest of the fleet follows its assignments. Damaged equipment,
hull breaches and flooding can force a withdrawal; crew, pumps and repair
supplies help keep a damaged boat afloat.

Use the Open Seas chart for long voyages, then enter local waters to reach
harbors, survey islands and investigate wrecks. Different factions own and
trade through the same ocean. Contracts, trading access and hostile traffic
give you reasons to choose a route and prepare for the return trip.
