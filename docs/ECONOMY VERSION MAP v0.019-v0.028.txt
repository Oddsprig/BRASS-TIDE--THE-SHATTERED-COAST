ECONOMY AND FLEET CAMPAIGN VERSION MAP
Prepared 22 October 2026 | v0.019–v0.028
STATUS: PLANNED. Current delivered game: v0.018 (integer BUILD 18).

OWNER REQUEST
"OK PREPARE A VERSION MAP"
This records the next development sequence after the completed v0.008–v0.018
campaign series. It prepares the plan; no later playable build is delivered
by this document. Each future playable build advances exactly 0.001 and is
built on its completed predecessor. If a step needs another release, move
the later targets forward rather than skip an increment.

PURPOSE
Make the coast worth understanding. A voyage can train and provision a crew,
earn trade income, discover valuable machinery, help a harbor recover and
change which fleets that harbor can sustain. Strong economies support real
boats; damaged trade and poor working conditions create useful decisions
and opportunities. The player can remain a captain, explorer or merchant;
a shipping company and shore business are optional progression paths.

DESIGN BASIS
The owner's installed LTV Economy - Powell RC9 files describe physical
stock/reserve sharing, workforce allocation floors, crew training inputs,
food consequences, marine reserves, industrial tooling and civilian goods.
Its policy/plan/event configuration also describes paid policies, worker
conditions and allocation choices. These are design references read from
the installed files, not a claim that its current Starsector save was run.

v0.019 — CREW TRAINING AND LABOR POOLS
Player outcome:
Choose between hiring available qualified sailors, paying to train local
workers, or keeping experienced crew alive. A depleted port cannot fill
every job and replace every lost sailor immediately.

Scope:
- Add small numerical cohorts for civilian availability, enrolled trainees
  and qualified sailors. Track people once across residents, shore staff,
  trainees, fleet crews, wounded and assigned survey parties; recruitment
  availability is a subset, never an extra population.
- Start with general sailors and training for the existing specialist roles.
  Reuse the actual specialist berths and wages from fleet identity.
- Training takes elapsed days, an available instructor, provisions and
  supplies, with actual wages/fees paid to their proper recipients.
- Training reserves trainees away from other work. Cancellation returns
  people and unspent escrow; consumed inputs and completed wages stay spent.
- Show queue, finish estimate, paid/unpaid status and effects of shortages.
  Food disruption slows training; good supply enables it.
- Introduce simple cohort experience only where it has a readable benefit
  to gunnery, engine care or damage control. Keep bonuses capped and expose
  the tradeoff; experience does not replace engine/hull/weapon fittings.

Economic rule:
Training converts the occupation of existing people. It cannot manufacture
people, repeat a signing bonus or silently create their savings. Preserve
existing crew and specialists in migrated saves without charging old fees.

Depends on: v0.018 workforce, crew savings, specialists, wages and saves.
Exit gate:
Enroll/cancel/complete/recruit/discharge at an actual harbor; check people,
coins, materials, missed payroll, casualties and save/load once-only effects.
An early-game captain can still hire a viable starter crew.


v0.020 — HARBOR RESERVES AND PRODUCTION PRIORITIES
Player outcome:
See why a port wants a shipment, how long its stock will last, and whether a
cargo is truly profitable after voyage expenses. Food relief and engine
parts become useful because they solve visible shortages.

Scope:
- Show food, fuel, ammunition and repair-supply coverage in days, using actual
  consumption and incoming contracted cargo. Show on-hand and in-transit
  separately; a promised delivery is not stock already in the warehouse.
- Set protected operating minima, working targets and exportable surplus
  from local demand, resupply route time, storage and a safety margin.
  Do not import LTV's 90+30 day reserve targets into this shorter economy.
- Public and commercial stocks retain their separate owners. A reserve rule
  changes allocation/quotes; it does not copy goods between purses or stores.
- Give essential producible outputs bounded workforce floors, then assign
  remaining paid labor using input availability, shortage and net value.
  Reserve staff needed by construction and existing businesses explicitly.
- Provide supply-security and commercial-priority policies only where the
  player has a business/charter or the relevant government offers a contract.
- Explain stock held for contracts, local essentials, operating use and sale.
  Emergency release has a named authority, recipient and actual transfer.
- Market quotes include cargo space, demand, buyer funds, route costs and
  observation age. Reuse the existing dated intelligence system.

Economic rule:
Coverage targets do not create inventory or force unaffordable purchases.
Exports cannot consume the same reserved unit as a local contract.
Production cannot assign the same worker to several floors/jobs.

Depends on: .019 cohort accounting; existing trade, public stores and recipes.
Exit gate:
Run a normal supply route, a blockade and a real relief delivery. Essential
production must avoid allocator starvation when feasible, exports must still
occur, and a real shortage must remain possible. Check serial/worker parity.


v0.021 — RECOVERED MACHINERY AND SHIPYARD DEVELOPMENT
Player outcome:
A recovered lathe or engine fixture can be sold, towed home or restored to
improve a real workshop. Exploration offers an investment decision beyond
collecting another pile of supplies.

Scope:
- Add a small original set of machinery: machine tools, engine tooling and
  hull/weapon jigs. Discover them through existing sites, wrecks and chains.
- Each item has physical ownership, cargo/tow requirements, condition and
  compatible installation sites. Use stable unique IDs.
- Installing requires actual foundations/materials, labor, oversight,
  commissioning time and operating capital. Dismantling moves the same item.
- Benefits affect a named bottleneck: worker-hours per recipe, permitted
  component quality or concurrent yard work. They never create missing
  materials, trained staff or coins.
- Add paid yard expansion where demand justifies it. Display capacity,
  utilization, staffing, maintenance, marginal output and payback estimate.
- Price machinery and new output from bills of materials, worker time,
  expertise, transport and condition. Reuse construction/refit quotes.
- Keep a normal, slower production route viable without rare machinery.

Economic rule:
Track material cost basis and earned profit through the existing business
ledger. More assembly space does not create a second copy of one workforce.

Depends on: .020 reserve/assignment accounting; salvage and home-harbor rights.
Exit gate:
Recover -> transport -> install -> staff -> produce -> maintain -> remove.
Compare the actual production bill before/after; prove losses, save/load and
cancelled projects neither duplicate the item nor refund consumed inputs.


v0.022 — WORKER WELLBEING AND PAID POLICIES
Player outcome:
Choose sustainable operation, a paid rush order or a recovery period.
Food deliveries and fair payroll matter to the people operating a shipyard,
and a stoppage has a reason the player can address.

Scope:
- Deepen prosperity with a few readable drivers: food security, pay/arrears,
  health/fatigue and unrest. Keep the detail in a harbor report.
- Add bounded, timed policies such as overtime, recovery shifts, food relief
  and public health. Limit the player's authority to owned businesses or
  negotiated harbor agreements.
- Overtime pays actual additional wages and consumes appropriate inputs.
  Fatigue limits sustained use; recovery takes elapsed time.
- Staff action can restrict an affected workplace after persistent poor
  conditions. Show warning, cause, demands, negotiation and recovery.
- Government aid is a treasury transfer with an affordable budget. Crew
  morale uses the same wage/food facts, without double-counting penalties.
- Routine policy renewals can use a chosen spending cap; no daily clicking.

Economic rule:
A productive policy must have a source of workers, materials and money.
No arbitrary tribute, unpaid output multiplier or instant reset of arrears.

Depends on: .019 training, .020 food coverage and .021 actual workplaces.
Exit gate:
Paid rush work -> fatigue -> recovery, plus arrears -> warning -> stoppage ->
negotiated paid restart. Check affordable and insolvent cases and save/load.


v0.023 — REGIONAL GOODS AND HOUSEHOLD DEMAND
Player outcome:
Ports gain commercial identities. A cargo boat can make a living carrying
useful civilian goods instead of only military supplies.

Scope:
- Introduce a small first set of regional goods, chosen from preserved fish,
  fine timber, lacquer products and medicinal plants after recipe review.
- Tie production to actual geography, workers and facilities. Processed
  goods consume their raw inputs and paid labor.
- Household purchases follow available savings and priority: essentials
  first, ordinary goods next, discretionary goods last.
- Goods can substitute within a need where sensible. Luxury shortages alone
  must not starve a port or make every settlement unstable.
- Add contracts and exploration leads that connect regional supply to actual
  buyers. Reuse cargo manifests, sealed freight and dated price observations.
- Add spoilage only where it creates a useful route decision and fits the
  existing numerical daily update. Otherwise leave it for later.

Economic rule:
Civilian purchases transfer finite household coins to sellers and consume
actual goods. Demand is not a guarantee that the buyer can afford the cargo.

Depends on: .020 coverage, .021 production and .022 household conditions.
Exit gate:
Follow raw harvest -> processing -> carriage -> sale -> household use.
Prove that full holds, lost cargo, poor buyers and oversupply affect profits.


v0.024 — PLAYER SHIPPING COMPANIES
Player outcome:
Turn spare cargo hulls into a working company. Assign a captain, a funded
route and escorts, then see net earnings, delays, repairs and losses while
the flagship explores.

Scope:
- Assign actual owned hulls and hired crew to a separate company roster.
  They cease providing traveling-fleet capacity or simultaneous deployment.
- Use the existing NPC navigation, convoy, combat, retreat and recovery
  machinery. Reuse physical berths, warehouses and paid business staff.
- Define origin/destination, goods, maximum buy price, minimum sell price,
  operating purse, wage/repair reserve, escort assignment and risk policy.
- Handle insufficient stock/funds, closed ports, blocked routes, damage and
  undelivered cargo with explicit wait/return/repair states and notifications.
- Present net profit after wages, fuel, provisions, repairs, fees and losses;
  distinguish inventory/capital from earned dividends.
- Add safe recall/reassignment and automatic spending ceilings. Only actual
  arrival unloads goods. Nearby engagements use the existing battle engine.

Economic rule:
A route is a use of real hulls, people, hold space and capital. No passive
income timer or invisible replacement convoy. Losses leave finite wrecks.

Depends on: .019–.023; existing independent fleets and home-harbor accounting.
Exit gate:
Two concurrent routes with actual hulls/cargo; loss, reroute, repair, recall,
default, escrow and save/load. Reconcile both company and world ledgers.


v0.025 — SMUGGLING AND ECONOMIC DIPLOMACY
Player outcome:
Take a risky relief run through an embargo, negotiate legitimate access or
trade quietly through contacts. Politics changes useful routes and prices.

Scope:
- Add a limited original set of restricted goods/trade permissions, using
  existing commodities first. Show the law and consequences before purchase.
- Legal, military and illicit offers draw from one physical owner inventory
  with explicit reservations; buying through one removes the same lot.
- Contacts provide finite offers, access and dated intelligence. An illicit
  counter is not an unlimited supplier or a second ship/equipment inventory.
- Inspections use proximity, patrol presence, route, concealment and witness
  facts. Confiscation transfers goods to a real custodian; fines transfer
  coins. Expose the reason and preserve viable independent play.
- Add limited export licences, paid concessions and negotiated access using
  existing faction pairs, claims and treaty timing.
- Embargoes affect actual eligible carriers and trade permission. Every
  observed sale/inspection event changes standing once.

Economic rule:
No duplicated open/black/military stock, purchase/resale money loop, free
permit income or tariff charged twice on one movement.

Depends on: .020–.024; existing faction ledger, contacts and treaty rules.
Exit gate:
Legal trade, permit trade, undetected run, witnessed inspection, refusal,
confiscation and embargo expiry. Verify physical stock/purses and saves.


v0.026 — BOARDING CREWS AND COASTAL DEFENSES
Player outcome:
A disabled launch can be captured at a real human and equipment cost.
Supplying a harbor battery or garrison changes whether convoys can pass.

Scope:
- Train and equip numerical boarding parties from existing people using the
  training system. Track their wages, provisions, transport and casualties.
- Add a readable battle boarding order against a close, suppressed, disabled
  or surrendering target. Conditions, time, defender resistance, retreat and
  losses are numerical; no animated crew or separate infantry game.
- Capturing transfers the same surviving hull and fittings, with its actual
  damage and remaining cargo. Captors need crew, tow/repair capacity and
  legal grounds or accept the political consequences.
- Add paid harbor garrisons and a small battery/defense module set with real
  crews, ammunition, repair needs and line-of-approach influence.
- Temporary coastal raids target a named store, defended installation or
  shipment. Permanent territory conquest is outside this milestone.
- Handle captives/surrender/release with bounded records and clear decisions.

Economic rule:
A capture cannot also produce a duplicate salvage hull. Crew losses reduce
the correct party; defense casualties and ammunition are paid, finite losses.

Depends on: .019 training, .020 reserves, .021 tooling and .025 political rules.
Exit gate:
Boarding success/failure/abort, allied-safe order rejection, casualty accounting,
captured hull recovery, supplied/empty battery and persistent aftermath.


v0.027 — REGIONAL CRISES AND RESETTLEMENT
Player outcome:
World events emerge from what happened. A lost food route, exhausted yard or
damaged defense creates relief, escort, repair and transport opportunities.

Scope:
- Connect sustained shortage, arrears, blockade, losses and worker unrest to
  a small set of observable regional situations with causes and next steps.
- Produce jobs only when a named sponsor can actually fund/escrow them.
  Reuse convoy, survey, salvage, relief and diplomatic systems.
- Add optional paid evacuation/resettlement using actual passenger capacity
  and real people. Origin, destination and fare/sponsor purse are explicit.
- Move people and their allocated savings once; retain wounds, training and
  profession. Destination employment/training takes time and capacity.
- Feed genuine recovery, losses, route security and supplied defenses into
  optional regional goals. Keep journal entries bounded and dated.
- Allow repair/relief/negotiation where remaining resources make recovery
  possible. Show an insolvent institution's actual limitation.
- Defer birth/aging simulation and whole-map territorial conquest.

Economic rule:
No crisis teleports a rescue fleet, creates replacement workers or refreshes
the government purse. Migration conserves people and coins across regions.

Depends on: .019–.026 and the existing regional campaign/event books.
Exit gate:
Interdict a real convoy -> measured shortage -> funded response -> delivery ->
gradual recovery. Run a paid relocation with save/load and no duplicate workers.
Check peaceful, hostile and insolvent branches and bounded event history.

COST AND ACCOUNTING RULES FOR EVERY STEP
- Coins are the only visible currency label.
- Boat/weapon/machine cost = actual input quantities at stated valuations +
  ordinary worker-days + specialist/oversight days + transport + applicable
  duties/yard overhead + explicit seller margin. Show each line and recipient.
- Training cost = instructor/trainee time and pay + provisions + supplies +
  facilities/service fees, with the same physical accounting.
- Daily company result = actual sales receipts minus material cost of sold
  goods, payroll, fuel, provisions, repairs, duties and fees. Working capital
  and retained inventory are not income; a dividend is a transfer of profit.
- Use the existing wage schedules, measured recipes, production time and
  route costs to tune prices. Do not assign a round boat price first and
  invent a bill to justify it.
- Record transfers among households, experts, businesses, governments,
  fleet owners, crew, companies and escrow exactly once. Goods are either
  owned/in transit/reserved or consumed/lost, never present in two stores.
- Hiring, training, boarding, landed surveys and migration conserve people.
  Keep specialist/trainee counters as subsets or disjoint canonical cohorts.
- Genuine loss and insolvency are allowed; negative purse/stock, silent
  subsidy and automatic nightly balance resets are not.

PERFORMANCE AND BACKGROUND DEVELOPMENT
- Keep daily local production/training/conditions on independent copied
  harbor snapshots. Bounded workers join before main-thread publication.
- Cross-port moves, company purchases, migration, diplomacy and battles need
  stable ordering and canonical transactions on the main thread.
- Do not run a path query for every company every frame. Cache static routes,
  stagger strategic decisions and update offscreen movement in bounded steps.
- Keep UI/rendering on the correct threads; do not read scene-tree nodes or
  share live dictionaries from worker jobs.
- The verified system reports Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX / 24 logical processors.
  Existing economy cap is 8 lanes, reserving 2 logical processors. Benchmark
  before increasing it; more threads are not automatically faster.
- RAM and GPU model are not verified in this plan. Do not claim GPU FPS or
  memory budgets from CPU-only test results.
- All tests stay hidden/headless and serialized, one engine/game at a time.
  No mouse, keyboard, focus takeover, automatic audible test or global change.
- New scenarios should answer one question in under a minute where possible;
  hard-limit individual tests to the project's existing 3-minute maximum.
