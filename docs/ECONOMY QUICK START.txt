BRASS TIDE — ECONOMY AND FLEET PLAY GUIDE
v0.028

Begin with Captain's priorities on the coastal chart. It reads the current fleet,
selected harbor route, food, working fuel, kits, crew pay and cargo. Choose Captain,
Explorer, Merchant, Privateer or Harbormaster to see useful system links. These
views do not spend coins or commit you to a career.

SAILING AND BATTLES
WASD pans the camera; scroll zooms. The flagship starts on autopilot. Select boats
and issue tactical orders, or choose manual control. Space pauses. Escape opens
the menu. Fleet Laboratory on the title screen provides separate engagement tests.
Ownership is separate from 300-point battle deployment. Support boats carry real
cargo and supplies; stored boats do not create traveling hold space.

PACK FOR THE RETURN
Every sailor earns 100 coins per day. Food, fuel and repair kits are real stock.
Loose reserve fuel and ammunition need loading into boat working stores. Include
wage arrears, specialist premiums and a return allowance before buying cargo.
The native starting-harbor ready Tinwake bill is 41,581 coins: 30,904 materials,
3,264 assembly and 1,353 supervision, plus yard overhead, margin, duty and storage.
The detailed yard bill traces these costs. Crew advances and stores are separate.
Local stock and the fitted design can change the actual bill you receive.

TRAINING AND WORKERS
Visit a harbor's academy to pay for existing workers and an instructor. Materials
are consumed, wages are held and earned over actual days, and reserved people
leave other work. Qualifications describe those people; they are not extra crew.
Sailor, gunner, mechanic and boarder courses have different costs and durations.
Hiring consumes real recruits and physical berths. Crew savings live in the shared
crew purse. Unpaid wages accumulate and hurt morale.

HARBOR SUPPLY AND INDUSTRY
The supply report separates commercial stock, public stores and incoming cargo.
Protected operating lots keep feasible essential production from selling all its
inputs. A friendly partner can advise reserve policy or pay for a public food
release. Actual shipments change what workers and yards can produce.
Surveyed abandoned sites can hold finite machine tools, engine fixtures or hull
jigs. Recovery reserves your real crew and supplies. Carried machinery uses a real
hold, can be lost, and needs a paid staffed workshop installation. Yard expansion
also consumes real materials, wages and commissioning time.

PRIVATE BUSINESS
Businesses have their own workers, inventories and purse. Those workers leave
ordinary town jobs. Materials and wages are costs; deposited coins or goods are
capital, not earned profit. Normal shifts, overtime, paid rest and food relief
trade output against worker strain. Arrears can lead to a strike; recovery needs
actual settlement, paid negotiation and subsequent paid rest days.
Regional fish, timber, lacquer and medicinal plants use local production chains.
Households first need basic food, then can afford limited civilian demand.

SHIPPING COMPANIES
Charter spare actual boats and their existing crews. Keep a seaworthy flagship.
Choose a route, commodity, operating capital, price limits, cargo lot limit and
reserve. A master is one of the crew and earns 75 additional coins per day.
The company sails the actual coast, buys shared inventory, pays wages and bears
losses. Sales minus sold material cost and operating costs determine its result.
Invested capital does not count as profit. Small volumes on poor price spreads can
lose coins; compare the quote and voyage bill before committing. Up to 8 companies
can be open, with 128 retained charter records going forward. Existing records are
preserved. Recall and collection require a physical return; boats do not teleport.

CUSTOMS AND TRADE AGREEMENTS
Neutral harbors restrict raw guns and engine assemblies. Friendly standing admits
them; a paid local thirty-day licence covers a finite consignment. The registry
bill is 69 coins for clerk labor and foreman oversight. Installed fittings and
sealed freight follow their own admitted manifests.
The unregistered broker uses the same physical stock, charges 28 coins labor and a
15% buy premium or sale discount. A declared manifest is open to inspection;
concealment can be discovered by an actual inspecting port or nearby local patrol.
Confiscation moves goods into a named harbor's public stores. Fines transfer what
you can afford and preserve any debt. Visit that harbor to settle it.
A friendly authority can negotiate a 15-day guns-or-engines restriction for an
existing hostile faction pair. Food and ordinary civilian goods remain outside
that military agreement. Faction hostility does not vanish when an agreement ends.

BOARDING AND THE COASTAL WATCH
Train and hire boarders into spare berths. They receive normal crew wages. A party
leaves its source boat only on contact with a weak or disabled enemy. Keep your
minimum working crew aboard. Exchanges consume actual people and take time.
Secured prizes retain their hull, damage, weapons and stores and leave active
combat. Tow them home for paid repair before using their weapons. Captives occupy
real berths or empty prize deck space and need rations. Release them safely in a
harbor to return existing people to the workforce; no sale or reward is invented.
Friendly coastal defenses need actual public materials, workers and paid expert
labor. Their separate crews receive treasury wages and consume public food, kits
and ammunition. An unpaid or unprovisioned watch cannot fire. A nearby harbor
brings its actual position and finite-ammunition projectiles into an engagement.

CRISES AND CIVILIAN PASSAGE
Three observed bad days of food, unrest or recorded unpaid obligations establish
a local crisis; three adequate days establish recovery. Read the dated evidence.
Optional sponsored passage removes existing idle workers, skill subsets and their
savings from the origin. A named boat needs spare crew berths and hold space for
purchased provisions. Waiting, camera movement and direct coordinate edits earn
no transport work. Actual sailing distance earns the fixed route work allowance.
After arrival, two distinct provisioned and staffed reception days precede worker
admission. Standing capacity uses the original workforce plus one vacant worker
place per 200 authored residents, with existing commitments and incoming cohorts
counted. It never refills on loading a save.
Buy more real provisions if delayed. Return requires actual origin docking;
unearned work money returns to its sponsor and unused food enters public stores.
Carrier loss records its people and food once; savings stay in counted estate
custody. Cohorts already ashore do not sink when your fleet departs.

DEVELOPMENT AND PERFORMANCE
Development notes are in the title and Escape menus and the portable TXT files.
Earlier notes and handoffs are preserved in history files. Native tests run in
isolated background/headless processes; no desktop input or focus is used.
Godot uses Separate rendering. Pure harbor work owns copied state, with at most 8
workers and 2 logical threads reserved. UI, fleet/law/passenger transactions and
publication remain on the main thread after joins. Water is inexpensive 2D art.
CPU measurements and native assertions are evidence for their specific fixtures;
they do not establish GPU frame rate, a human listening test or subjective fun.
