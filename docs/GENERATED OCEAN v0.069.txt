BRASS TIDE v0.069 - A GENERATED OCEAN SECTOR

A fresh campaign creates 48 island groups: 8 settled, 12 frontier and 28 remote.
The existing Shatter Coast and Glasswind Keys are included. Open seas connect
local charts; there are no major continents. Different factions can hold
separate islands and harbors inside the same group.

WASD moves the camera. Scroll to zoom, M for an overview, Home to your fleet.
Click a group or its approach to set a course. Sail to the marked approach,
stop there and choose Enter. Local approach controls also take you back out
to the open ocean. The same boats, crew, cargo, fuel and clock continue.

Settled charts include public harbor information. Frontier and distant
holdings must be observed locally. Unknown ownership is separate from a
known unclaimed shore. Sightings and paid surveys reveal useful information.

The new groups have 19 finite harbor markets and 46 survey landings. Stop
at a known water berth to trade, subject to faction access and cargo space.
Buy supplies into the hold and load reserves into boat tanks and repair racks.
Full shipyards, crew recruitment, refitting and storage remain at Shatter Coast.

Chart a sighted landing with two spare sailors and a kit; a fuller survey
uses four spare sailors and two kits. Food, time and wages still matter.
Resource findings describe what is there; they do not add free cargo to a boat.

Each new harbor begins with a declared finite workforce, stores and purses.
Workers earn wages and businesses trade from those same accounts. Loading a
region or revisiting a harbor does not recreate its opening reserves.

Existing voyages retain their original two-region world, depleted discoveries,
shore parties, ships and accounts. Start a fresh campaign for the seeded ocean.
Save and return visits retain resolved geography rather than rerolling it.
