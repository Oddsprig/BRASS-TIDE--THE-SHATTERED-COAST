Twelve civilian boats join the Shatter Coast yards. Each yard now also offers the new designs of its own faction — Reed, Compact or Forge — alongside its familiar warships: three fishing boats from the little Kelpnet Dory to the broad Mother Gull Trawler, four transports rising from the Fenhopper Lighter to the wide Brasswind Motor-Galleon, two Forge tankers with powerful pumps, and three yachts crowned by the Aurelian Viceroy Yacht. None is free: commission one and the yard charges its usual physically worked-out quote in coins and materials, wages held in escrow while the workforce builds, or buy a yard-built hull in stock when one is ready. Fishing boats carry small crews and modest holds, transports carry the biggest loads afloat, tankers haul large liquid cargoes, and yachts trade hold space for speed — each with a single light gun for defense. For now they simply sail as boats you own; what they might earn their keep doing comes later.
