BRASS TIDE v0.038 - SIX WAYS TO MAKE A LIVING

Open Captain's priorities from the campaign panel or Guide. Choose a career
to see a first goal, entry requirements, current operating costs, reward
source and next useful step. You can combine careers during one voyage.
The guide keeps entry advice visible when urgent fleet warnings need space.

EXPLORER
Choose an island lead, provision the voyage and survey it with actual crew
and supplies. A funded survey job can pay from a harbor's reserved fee.
Follow the revealed lead or inspect a discovered wreck after returning safely.

MERCHANT
Compare actual stock, prices, buyer funds and a safe return route. Start with
a manageable freight lot or a funded delivery, then consider a larger hold
or paid shipping company. Cargo ties up coins; a sale needs a real buyer.

ESCORT
Find actual loaded coastal traffic and protect its carrier through the
physical exit or destination objective. Check the offered terms, crew,
ammunition and repair reserves. Voluntary escort success awards service
and faction standing; it currently carries no automatic coin fee. Combine
it with funded work or trade when you need voyage income. Improve endurance before
taking on a more dangerous route; the carrier's survival is the objective.

PRIVATEER
Read faction standing, commissions and customs before choosing a target.
Budget ammunition, boarding crew, damage and the return of a captured prize.
A bounty, recovered equipment or a sale pays only through its existing
funding and custody rules. Keep the legal and repair costs in the decision.

FISHER
Acquire a crewed fishing hull, fuel, food and free hold. Use K, then Fishing,
after stopping in safe water away from a harbor. Unpaused time earns a
bounded catch from the site. Land it at a harbor with actual purchasing
funds, then improve carrying space and endurance for the next trip.

PASSENGER OPERATOR
Prepare a transport or yacht with actual spare berths. The K work board
shows funded charters when a sponsor, passengers, route and provisions are
available. Carry the real cohort to its destination and settle there.
Improve berth and supply capacity before carrying a larger commitment.

COMMON COSTS
Sailors earn100 coins per person per day. Food, fuel, repairs, machinery,
weapons and construction retain their existing material and labor costs.
The guide uses current records and quotes; it does not guarantee profit.
Protect return wages and stores before committing cargo or passenger space.
An unavailable safe route is marked as such; reserve-only estimates do not
quote a sailing journey. Actual action panels remain the place to commit.

SCOPE
This is one career guidance and balance pass across the existing systems.
The general Captain and Harbormaster views remain available. The36-boat
roster, paid economy and300-point battle deployment limit remain in use.
