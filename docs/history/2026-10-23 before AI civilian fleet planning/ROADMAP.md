# Brass Tide: the next ten versions

Prepared 23 October 2026 with one **Sol 6.1 Max** consultation through **Bridge**.
Current delivered game: **v0.028**; exact game commit
`2b11d4d1af69b62a2f8436bcdee4a5675432bd98`.
The economy stage v0.019-v0.028 is complete. All rows below are **planned**.

The next stage connects useful fitting choices, readable tactical battles and
rewarding voyages. Early improvements work on your existing boat. Each release
builds on its verified predecessor and advances exactly 0.001.

| Version | Focus | What becomes playable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0.029** | Fleet workshop and boat presentation | Compare and save real fits; improve all 24 boats and their fitted weapon models. |
| **0.030** | Tactical formations and captain doctrines | Use screens, selection groups, fire discipline and conditional withdrawal. |
| **0.031** | Damageable weapons and machinery | Lose individual guns or pumps; allocate real crews and supplies to repair them. |
| **0.032** | Convoy breakthroughs and recovery battles | Get actual carriers or damaged prizes through a physical extraction route. |
| **0.033** | Sightings, pursuit and ambushes | Scout, lose contact and intercept using actual positions and dated knowledge. |
| **0.034** | Coastal geography that changes a battle | Fight through distinct passages, reefs and harbor approaches tied to the chart. |
| **0.035** | Outer-island expedition chains | Follow two original connected branches with finite equipment, rights and discoveries. |
| **0.036** | Rival captains and remembered encounters | Recognise existing captains whose losses, repairs and memories persist. |
| **0.037** | Faction operations with physical supply lines | Open a contested corridor through real freight, combat or negotiated access. |
| **0.038** | Campaign progression, polish and performance | Prove four viable careers and tune presentation, balance and measured performance. |

The [detailed TXT roadmap](docs/FLEET%20CAMPAIGN%20VERSION%20MAP%20v0.029-v0.038.txt)
sets each version's scope, visual work, dependencies and acceptance scenario.
The [lead review](docs/PLANNING%20REVIEW%20v0.029-v0.038.txt) explains how the Bridge
advice was checked and adapted; its original report is retained in docs/planning/.

First improve outfitting, boats and combat decisions (.029-.032). Then connect
pursuit, geography and expeditions (.033-.035). Finish with persistent rivals,
a supplied faction operation and complete career polish (.036-.038).

Keep 24 original hulls, physical interchangeable mounts, numerical crews,
finite coins/people/goods, default autopilot, WASD camera and separate 300-point
battle deployment. Water stays inexpensive. Business and shipping remain
optional; new content should create voyages worth taking.

This is a planning update. The game, version, release tags, portable packages,
saves, music and desktop launcher remain at their delivered state. No engines or
game tests were run for the plan. Earlier roadmap/index/handoff records are
preserved verbatim under docs/history/2026-10-23 before v0.029-v0.038 planning/.
