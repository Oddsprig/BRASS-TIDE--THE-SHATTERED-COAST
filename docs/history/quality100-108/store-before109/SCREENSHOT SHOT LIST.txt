FINAL GALLERY — CAPTURE AFTER ART/UX FREEZE, FROM THE EXACT SALE CANDIDATE
Aim for eight clear native gameplay/UI frames, normally 1920x1080. Respect the
actual supported aspect ratio; do not stretch the game. Preserve original PNGs;
web-sized derivatives, if later needed, remain linked to their originals.
One screenshot should explain one player action. Keep text readable at the page's
display size. Use actual frames with modest, honest camera composition; retain
the native HUD when it explains the action. Captions live outside the images.

01 — FLEET COMBAT (lead image)
Frame: a real engagement with distinct small/medium/heavy silhouettes, a readable
target/order, short stern wakes and an identifiable impact. Avoid empty water,
wall-to-wall damage numbers and an artificially enlarged one-boat glamour shot.
Caption: Give orders while your boats fight on autopilot.
Proof: actual order/target/fire events and short motion witness from 100/101;
the frame alone proves neither flanking behavior nor 300-point smoothness.
