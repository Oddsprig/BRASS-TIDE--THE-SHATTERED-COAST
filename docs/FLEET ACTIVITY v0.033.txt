Watch nearby fleets through the Coastal traffic board, which lists each observed fleet within range with its current business, its reason for sailing, its visible hulls, and whether it is under way or holding in harbor, and you can hail any listed fleet from there. Your captain's log reports only encounters inside that same watch range: when independent fleets fight near enough for you to witness it, the log names who engaged whom, how much sinking followed, and whether a side retreated or won, while distant fights stay out of your log and are kept only in the full encounter records. Because these owners pay real wages, burn real stores, suffer real damage and buy repairs and replacements from real yards, a fleet reported hurt near you may next be seen on the board holding in its home harbor recovering, while a convoy that survived its passage sails on about its delivery.
