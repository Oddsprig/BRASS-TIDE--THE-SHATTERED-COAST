BRASS TIDE v0.044 - COMMIT, BREAK AWAY, RECOVER

WHAT CHANGED
Light skiffs retain their response. Larger hulls build and shed velocity more
slowly, and carry momentum through changes of course. Bow turn limits and top
speed are unchanged; live boats and AI forecasts use the same movement math.

Large cannons have a1.8-second base reload at full efficiency. Cannons on the
large primary forward socket have135degrees of traverse. Crew/equipment damage
can lengthen the real interval. Smaller weapons, rear and side mounts keep
their existing rules. Fitting values, mount arcs and actual firing agree.

Select a boat to read its real reload time, water trend, engine/fuel condition
and repair hands/kits. Loaded means the timer expired; aim, range and a clear
shot are still required. The crew-priority buttons act on the FLAGSHIP.
No new shield, heat, fatigue or pressure meter was added.

TRY THE LOOP
Main menu -> Fleet battle laboratory -> Preset.
- Practice: torpedo crossing. Take the Tinwake across the heavy gun's bearing,
  line up the tube, fire, and leave during the reload. A kill is not required.
- Practice: cover a breakaway. Use the escort to cover Tinwake after its pass.
  Bail rising water, patch with carried kits when safe, and judge another pass.
These are normal isolated battles, with normal finite stores and no career
rewards. They give no scripted win, free repair, extra ammunition or hull buff.

Autopilot starts ON. Space pauses. WASD moves the camera. Right-click issues
movement/attack/escort orders to selected boats. U toggles manual flagship helm;
manual steering uses arrows and mouse. Escape opens the menu.

Damage control uses real crew time. Water costs speed; leaving late can remove
the advantage that let the skiff escape. Bailing, patching, fire suppression,
machinery work and guns compete for hands. Repairs use finite kits; reloading
returns no ammunition. Keep enough crew, fuel, ammunition and kits to return.
