BRASS TIDE v0.071 - READING A BATTLE

The flagship starts on autopilot. WASD moves the camera. Pause to issue orders,
choose targets or change damage-control priorities. Resume to watch the fleet.

Machine guns use short repeated flashes and sharp recorded bursts. Cannons
use a stronger single flash, recoil and a heavier report. Rockets have exhaust
and a distinct launch sound. Torpedoes run along the water and strike with a
water plume. A splash can be a miss; it does not automatically mean hull damage.

Watch the boat as well as its health bar. Engine trouble, active fire and water
aboard are different conditions. Crew repair and pumping use the existing crew,
time and supplies. A damaged boat can remain useful if it can withdraw in time.
Sinking boats settle into the water; not every loss needs a fiery explosion.

Wakes begin at the stern and follow the boat's recent movement. A boat slowing
or stopping should lose its wash. Water uses inexpensive drawing rather than
fluid simulation. Crews remain numerical; the combat view does not animate them.

Sound options control weapons and ambience separately from your music tracks.
Weapon attacks use existing licensed recordings; source credits are in
docs/audio/RECORDED AUDIO CREDITS.txt. Generated music stays in music/.

This version improves the existing presentation. Complete boat and gun asset
production remains v0.073; tactical behavior v0.074; campaign strategy v0.075.
Read tests/RESULTS.txt for the actual checks, recorded motion and audio review.
