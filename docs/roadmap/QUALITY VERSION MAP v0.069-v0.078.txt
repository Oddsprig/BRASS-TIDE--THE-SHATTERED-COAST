BRASS TIDE - NEXT TEN QUALITY MILESTONES
29 October 2026 | v0.069-v0.074 VERIFIED SCOPED; v0.075-v0.078 PLANNED | Current delivered game: v0.074

PURPOSE AND PLAYER LOOP
Reach the standard the owner expects in fleet combat, exploration, fleet
building, trade, presentation and responsiveness. Judge the result through
complete voyages, battles, rendered motion, recorded sound and player feedback.
Feature names, assertion totals and a higher version number do not establish
that quality. Every version below is a bounded delivery, not a parity claim.

Find an opportunity -> prepare an affordable fleet -> choose a route and risk
-> trade, explore, fight or negotiate -> recover and bring something home
-> make a useful fleet or fitting choice -> discover the next opportunity.

This extends the existing plan: preserve the 0.069-0.072 sequence, then add
0.073-0.078. Do not skip those four unfinished versions or rebuild accepted v0.068.
v0.069-v0.074 are delivered within scope; the remaining four entries are planned.

0.069 - A GENERATED OCEAN SECTOR [VERIFIED SCOPED]
Focus: persistent seeded geography and starting political ownership.
Build a settled core, frontier and distant archipelagos. Preserve the starting
coast and existing voyages. Open seas connect local island groups; individual
islands and harbors have owners, resources, services and discoveries. Different
factions can own different islands or harbors within one group. Keep geographic
sectors distinct from political control and respect unknown information.
The design target remains 48 groups: 8 settled, 12 frontier, 28 remote. Destinations
need recognizable geography and useful opportunities; publish any shortfall.
Reuse prepared geography/site/industry templates with seeded variation and
author a few signature places. Remote islands do not each need a new market.
Acceptance: the same seed produces the same reachable geography and starting
owners; return visits/save-load retain it. Demonstrate a useful mixed-faction
group, alternative routes and a distant expedition. Give economies finite,
recorded opening workers, goods and capital; preserve migration accounting.
Boundary: starting ownership only. Expansion and conquest belong to v0.076.

0.070 - VOYAGES THAT PAY AND FLEETS WORTH BUILDING [VERIFIED SCOPED]
Focus: extend existing work and support roles across the generated sector.
Offer compatible freight, escort, survey, salvage, fuel and passenger work
through existing contacts. Show gross reward, likely operating costs, required
hold/berths and return reserves. Make scouts, carriers, tankers and repair boats
useful. Compare existing fittings and explain what a purchase changes.
Acceptance: a normal starter can fund a voyage, return with worthwhile profit
or useful recovery, then afford a meaningful fitting or fleet improvement.
Prove this from actual starting accounts without test-only treasury transfers.
Fund clients and NPC trade from real accounts; delivered cargo changes stocks.
Boundary: improve the existing jobs/refit/economy, not parallel frameworks,
additional hull rosters or guaranteed profit for every route and risk.

0.071 - COMBAT FEEL, WAKES AND SOUND [VERIFIED SCOPED]
Delivered implementation and sampled visual proof; audible quality is UNVERIFIED.
The requested media routes did not supply a listening result. Preserve the
offline sample and exact limitation in docs/audio/OFFLINE REVIEW v0.071.txt.
Focus: make the existing battle understandable and forceful in motion.
Improve firing/recoil, tracers, impacts, engine damage, sparks, fire, flooding,
explosions and sinking. Attach wakes to the stern and actual speed/turning.
Keep machine guns, cannons, rockets and torpedoes visually and audibly distinct.
Use appropriate weapon recordings with recorded provenance and usable licenses;
keep generated music and the owner's tracks separate from weapon sound design.
Acceptance: normal battle zoom makes shots, hits, disabling damage, danger and
withdrawal legible. Review actual motion and an offline sound recording; muted
live tests remain mandatory. Record anything not listened to or played.
Boundary: existing damage/effects/audio systems, inexpensive water, existing
boat assets. New complete hull/weapon asset production belongs to v0.073.

0.072 - ONE COMPLETE VOYAGE AND A RESPONSIVE SECTOR [VERIFIED SCOPED]
Focus: integrate v0.069-v0.071 and fix the largest demonstrated runtime costs.
Complete prepare -> sail -> work/explore -> encounter -> repair/recover ->
return -> upgrade on normal accounts. Review loading, camera, chart labels,
orders, save/load and the explanation of costs and consequences.
Measure representative active 300-vs-300 DP light and mixed battles plus campaign
travel, region changes and distant updates. Queue and stagger data work where
measurements justify it; render resources, UI and audio stay on main.
Acceptance: the voyage works without special setup; expensive targeting,
navigation and economy decisions have bounded schedules. Record frame times,
simulation lag, command response, loading and memory on stated hardware/settings.
Carry forward unresolved worker/lag/FPS targets; report substantive stalls and
costs without spending the release chasing fractional frame-cap noise.
Boundary: a bounded integration/performance pass, not all future features.

0.073 - FINISHED BOAT AND WEAPON ASSETS [VERIFIED SCOPED]
Focus: a consistent original asset standard across the 36 existing hulls.
Give boats distinct silhouettes, construction/material detail, engines or
outboards, deck equipment and recognizable weapon models. Preserve physical
mount IDs, positions, arcs, scale and interchangeable weapons. Hulls, weapons,
damage overlays and wakes must agree in battle, chart views and refit previews.
Use offline authored/baked assets where useful, with cheap runtime rendering.
Reuse authored components and the existing asset workflow across the hull
families; this is not a requirement to invent a new renderer or modeling tool.
Acceptance: identify hull roles and installed weapons at normal play zoom;
inspect every existing boat for mount alignment, floating/intersecting parts,
waterline contact and damage appearance. Review representative models moving
and firing, not only a large gallery close-up. Preserve original asset rights.
Boundary: asset production and its display integration; no new hull roster,
weapon framework, economic rebalance or expensive water simulation.

0.074 - TACTICAL AI WITH COUNTERPLAY [VERIFIED SCOPED]
Focus: make existing roles, loadouts and fleet orders work together.
Light boats flank, encircle, harass and disengage. Escorts screen carriers and
damaged allies; heavy boats cover approaches and punish reckless flankers.
Coordinate firing passes, torpedo/rocket pressure, reload openings and retreat.
Use existing damage, ammunition, flooding, repair and movement constraints.
Acceptance: show small repeatable matchups where screening counters harassment,
different fittings change behavior, concentration can be escaped or punished,
and attack/escort/defend/retreat orders produce readable responses. Orders must
not cause constant target switching, orbiting jitter or stalled formations.
Boundary: improve existing tactical AI and orders; default flagship autopilot,
WASD camera and 300 DP deployment remain. No new officer progression framework.

0.075 - FACTIONS THAT PURSUE AND PAY FOR A STRATEGY
Focus: independent campaign objectives across regions.
Shipping interests pursue actual deliveries; pirates seek worthwhile cargo;
patrols respond to known threats. Hostile factions coordinate supply routes,
escorts, raids and supply interdiction. Survivors retreat for actual service, and funded
yards commission replacements using workers, materials, crews and accounts.
Acceptance: a visible sequence of intention, dispatch, interception/response,
loss or delivery and paid recovery changes actual trade and local availability.
Explain witnessed events through dated intelligence. Distant simulation uses
scheduled data jobs and existing resolution, not full tactical battles everywhere.
Boundary: strategy and military presence; harbor closure/access changes and
sovereign transfer follow in v0.076, using the same ownership records.

0.076 - FUNDED EXPANSION AND CHANGING CONTROL
Focus: an end-to-end territorial operation using existing economy and combat.
Transport workers, machinery, materials and operating capital to establish or
improve a coastal outpost. Supply and defend it. Existing defenses, blockade,
boarding/surrender and negotiation can change a harbor's owner; withdrawal or
peace can end the operation. Keep businesses separate from sovereignty.
Acceptance: demonstrate one funded expansion and one contested control change,
including interruption and its consequences. Workers, goods, damage, stores,
boat/equipment IDs and purses persist through transfer. Record who owns each
asset and why; a fleet's mere presence does not capture an entire group.
Boundary: one complete operation and ownership path, not land RTS combat,
an unrestricted empire simulator or a second settlement economy.

0.077 - EXPLORATION WITH PEOPLE, CHOICES AND PAYOFFS
Focus: authored content on the existing exploration/contact systems.
Build two bounded original chains: one discovery mystery and one lost-expedition
or disputed-recovery story. Connect clues, recognizable island sites, recurring
contacts, a risky recovery and a faction choice. Let discovery provide useful
equipment, cargo, intelligence or access through actual work and consequences.
Acceptance: each chain has a clear lead, a meaningful decision and a worthwhile
return. Contacts remember the outcome; revisiting the place reflects it. Cargo
space, crew/endurance and faction access make taking everything impractical.
Boundary: reuse existing jobs, surveying, salvage, dialogue and state; no new
quest engine, copied stories or requirement to author every remote island.

0.078 - CAMPAIGN POLISH AND QUALITY REVIEW
Focus: finish the loop built in v0.069-v0.077 through a bounded balance/clarity pass.
Review explorer, merchant, escort, privateer, fisher and passenger careers with
short saved scenarios and actual normal-start progression. Refine early goals,
cost/reward curves, fleet/refit comparisons, intelligence, menus, tooltips,
settings, notifications and recovery from setbacks. Recheck representative
battle/campaign performance after the asset and AI changes.
Acceptance: understand the first voyage, choose between useful opportunities,
grow from a cheap boat to a purposeful fleet and recover from affordable losses.
Review actual presentation and play decisions with the owner. Record which
quality goals passed and any bounded follow-on corrections still required.
Boundary: no unrelated systems or guarantee that parity arrives at this version.

QUALITY RULES THROUGHOUT
- Map: useful readable places, routes and faction information at normal zoom;
  original island world, no major continents and no substitute destination dots.
- Fleet: consequential existing fittings and varied roles, clear mount previews,
  physical condition, crew, logistics and affordable progression.
- Battles: readable decisions and effects, useful commands, AI counterplay and
  meaningful withdrawal/recovery. Added content does not certify combat quality.
- Economy: coins, 100 coins per ordinary sailor-day, finite goods/people/accounts,
  paid construction/service and commercially meaningful traffic. Disclose actual
  shortages and insolvency; no test-only funding in progression acceptance.
- Presentation: a numerical pass or still image cannot establish motion, sound,
  enjoyment or visual parity. Use short actual captures and owner play feedback.
- Performance: preserve actual actor counts, equipment and simulation behavior.
  Record existing unmet targets; never silently waive them or claim Separate mode 2.
- Keep water cheap and crew numerical. Preserve the current data worker design,
  safe rendering, physical owners/UIDs and compatible saves.
