BRASS TIDE - OPEN OCEAN VERSION MAP v0.063-v0.072
28 October 2026 | v0.063-v0.068 VERIFIED; v0.069-v0.072 PLANNED

PURPOSE
Build a large ocean world from smaller, detailed island groups. Preserve the
Shatter Coast as the starting inhabited region. Sailing offshore leads to a
playable ocean chart; approaching another group opens its local waters. Combat
continues on a separate encounter map reflecting the actual location.

WORLD DESIGN
- Owner clarification29October2026: one ocean-covered planet, no major
  continents. Open seas serve the hyperspace role; island groups serve the
  star-system role; individual islands serve the planet role. Harbors/outposts
  are markets/installations. Larger geographic sectors contain several groups.
- An archipelago may contain different factions' islands and harbors. Show
  peaceful mixed ownership, alliance/shared administration and contested control
  accurately; one faction's presence does not automatically own the whole group.
- Seeded generation, faction relationships, shipping/trading/pirate actors and
  funded expansion are mandatory campaign direction. Full ownership rules and
  remaining implementation gaps: docs/roadmap/OCEAN WORLD DESIGN.txt.
- Settled core: busy faction harbors, production, fishing and trade routes.
- Frontier: scattered settlements, disputed waters and pirate anchorages.
- Distant archipelagos: discoveries, ruins, wrecks and resources worth a voyage.
- Sector design target: 48 groups, comprising 8 settled, 12 frontier and 28 remote
  groups. This is a proposed content target, not existing or accepted content.
  Groups vary in size and character; remote groups generally have no full port.
  Establish useful destinations and route choices before expanding their count.
- A visible fleet travels on the ocean chart. Routes can be changed or interrupted
  by pursuit, sightings and discoveries. Local and offshore travel share one clock,
  physical fleet, inventory, crew, damage state and conserved accounts.
- Transition at coastal approaches, with a choice of arrival side. No magical
  jump gates, automatic hostile-fleet escape, free refueling or reset purses.
