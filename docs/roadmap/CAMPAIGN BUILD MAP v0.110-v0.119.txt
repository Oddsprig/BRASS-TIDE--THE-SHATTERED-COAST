BRASS TIDE — CAMPAIGN BUILD MAP v0.110–v0.119

Owner: prepare an original storyline campaign, research Starsector and Blood Wake, then build one version at a time on the previous version. Baseline v0.109. This document describes the complete design; version delivery is recorded separately in BUILD STATUS.txt and each released CHANGES file. Unbuilt chapters are plans.

THE COMMON PASSAGE

A merchant escort exposes the seizure clauses in a rescue guarantee. Della Venn, the Compact's charter administrator, once kept vulnerable harbors supplied. She now uses rescue debts and selective protection to control boats and routes. Her organization also performs useful work. Removing its protection without replacing the funded sailings would hurt the same people the captain wants to help.

Tobin Hale at Brass Haven financed the guarantees. His signature permitted more than he admits. Mara Voss at Anvil Dock needs materials and paid work for her shipwrights; opposing Venn cannot make repairs free. Sister Wren at Reedwater Floats wants public accountability but must protect people who testify. Cass Rye at Rust Anchorage sold a route used in a seizure. Her help does not cancel her responsibility. These histories are new original writing for the existing contacts. Venn is a new original character, introduced in the opening through her written claim.

The player remains an independent captain. The campaign is optional and runs alongside the existing eight missions, nine coastal stories, trade, exploration and fleet building. It does not overwrite the old missionIndex or require a faction commission.

ACT I — THE GUARANTEE
110 — Overdue Merchant. Hear Tobin's dated Kelp Reach departure report, reacquire a real loaded merchant, accept its funded escort, accompany its original carriers to their delivery harbor and collect its actual fee. Return to Tobin for Venn's claim and ask why his countersignature appears. A fresh Tinwake, its five sailors and the original15,000coins can undertake this chapter without a compulsory hull purchase or combat. Released boundary: second_signature111.
111 — Second Signature. Accept a native funded bounty on an existing hostile raider implicated in the seizures. Bind its original hulls before the encounter; pursue the target and inspect its actual wrecks. Paid recovery of an original fitting, with current custody, supports the investigation. Exhaustion or prior destruction has a separate honest loss report, with no invented prize or replacement target. Tobin acknowledges that his guarantee authorized seizures. His first optional conversation asks him to read the clause plainly. No additional mandatory return beyond the native bounty's sponsoring harbor.
112 — Exit Channel. Protect actual working boats leaving the threatened Kelp Reach channel. Staying with the withdrawal competes with chasing its pursuers. Record individual escapes and losses. Wren confronts the cost of paying protection crews; only describe unpaid wages when native state supports that claim. Do not invent passengers, restore casualties or award escape from a generic battle victory.

ACT II — THE EXCEPTION LIST
113 — A Useful Enemy. Help move real repair materials toward Cinder Quay, using existing goods and funded work. Venn's useful protection complicates Mara's demand for change. Actual aid needs native evidence; no compulsory scripted rescue. Mara's first optional job uses an existing repair quote if available, with a conversation about telling workers when work cannot be funded. There is no new pump commodity or industrial framework.
114 — Bought Route. Cass identifies an existing seizure operation. Choose permitted release on actual affordable terms, or raid and physically withdraw with the secured property. Cass admits selling the original route. A hit or retreat alone proves neither release nor recovery. Her optional conversation asks what she knew when she accepted payment, without inventing an amount, restitution or forgiveness.
115 — Names on Paper. Move a finite witness cohort and make commitment C1: public testimony at Wren's Reedwater Floats, or protected testimony at Mara's Anvil Dock. Tell the people their destination before departure. Use real transport berths, provisions, savings and safe discharge. Different destinations and protection duties make this a physical branch. Wren's optional conversation concerns an actual surviving witness and cannot reveal a protected identity.

ACT III — THE REPLACEMENT
116 — Two Distress Flags, then Narrow Withdrawal. Cover finite loaded relief boats, prioritizing a threatened carrier or the boats that can keep moving. Then protect the withdrawal or interdict its actual pursuers. The later convoy inherits the surviving capacity and cargo. Venn may cite warden assistance only if available native actors actually provided it. Use supported carriers or serial physical encounters, not a new split-battle system.
117 — Who Pays First. Mara, Tobin and Wren disagree about who funds the replacement. Make commitment C2: mutual harbor-funded escort obligations, or defined service under separate owners' bonds. Complete the first funded escort under those terms. Each existing purse remains separate; there is no new pooled treasury, tax, subsidy or invented insurance payout. Mara's second optional job discusses a genuinely owned damaged hull without giving it an invented rescue history or making repair compulsory.
118 — Fuel Has Owners, then Last Warrant. Negotiate an available affordable passage through a selective Naphtha cordon, or interdict the boats closing it. Passage does not buy the sellers' fuel. Then protect actual claimant boats near Brass, including culpable crews Tobin refuses to abandon before they can answer. Preserve cargo, expenditure, survivors and obligations. Tobin's second optional conversation shows how he now asks who can pay before commissioning work.

ACT IV — THE PASSAGE
119 — Common Passage. Escort a genuinely funded, loaded relief voyage through the existing Shatter–Glasswind/Westglass Open Seas connection. Negotiate permitted passage where offered, or defend the carriers through physical exits. Cargo must actually arrive and settle. Return to Tobin at Brass for closure, changed contact scenes and continued sandbox play.

ENDING RULES
Common Passage: the mutual arrangement actually funds and completes the relief delivery.
Many Harbors: the separate-owner arrangement actually funds and completes it, with explicit limits.
Unsettled Coast: after a failed or incomplete undertaking, the player explicitly chooses to close it at debrief. Failure or retreat alone never closes the campaign or signifies agreement with Venn. An unresolved attempt remains resumable.
No ending creates free boats, territory, wealth, cancelled debts or worldwide peace. C1 affects people, reception and the account of events; it is not a cosmetic price modifier or an extra arbitrary ending gate.

CONTENT AND SCOPE
Twelve main operations, six optional contact jobs in four contact arcs: Tobin2, Mara2, Wren1, Cass1. All six optional jobs can be skipped. Nine preserved chains + one main chain + four optional arcs = fourteen, below the existing sixteen-chain limit. Stable released stage and choice IDs remain valid as later chapters append. A2–3hour main campaign is a design target, not a verified duration or store claim.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT
Use the existing coastal-story catalog/UI with small per-chapter native-operation adapters. The main content is separate game/data/campaign_main110.json, preserving the old catalog. Native jobs own escrow and payment; native sailing/battles own movement and encounters; original wrecks and equipment UIDs own salvage identity. No second economy, synthetic victory or forced NPC respawn. A dead or exhausted source has an honest alternative; an unseen outcome stays unresolved.
110 escort acceptance is VoyageJobs.accept_escort, not the harbor-only accept function. Bind job/client/carrier/cargo/sequence before risk; consume a successful canonical native close immediately, including stock UI paths. Follow alters only the player's course, at most once per simulated second. Client movement and production remain independent.
Later battle proof requires the bound owner, original hull UID and newer physical encounter. Empty legacy won calls and unrelated/offscreen victories do not prove the player's actions. Recovery requires original item UID, real paid work and current custody. Retreat, consent, delivery and payment are distinct fields. Unknown gates fail closed. Saved data validates types before conversion and rejects stale, duplicate or overlapping records.
Keep existing detached economy and30Hz battle workers, bounded decision cadences, Main-owned input/UI and the inexpensive water. Campaign authoring does not justify a rendering or threading rewrite.
