BRASS TIDE — SELLABLE WINDOWS RELEASE MAP
v0.099–v0.108 | 13 November 2026 | PLANNED, NOT BUILT
Owner-set price: US$9.99. Current delivered game: v0.098.

The next ten versions finish a connected game that a buyer can understand,
enjoy and keep playing. They use the existing 36 boats, physical mount slots,
300-point battle deployment, bronze-coin economy and 48-group ocean world.
Each version builds on its accepted predecessor and advances by exactly .001.
Reaching v0.108 does not by itself authorize selling an unfinished build.

0.099 — CAMPAIGN RESPONSIVENESS
Scope: profile an ordinary populated campaign during travel, daily production,
traffic publication, harbor use and an autosave. Fix the largest demonstrated
cause in the current copy/apply/UI/save path, then reassess that workload.
Keep physical traffic, finite purses and goods, dates and economic cadence.
Exit: actual active-world measurements, responsive controls and unchanged
accounting. Attribute worker time, main-thread application and rendering
separately. A faster callback or a paused empty chart is not the result.
Do not: rewrite the economy, hide NPCs, reduce the world or add threads blindly.

0.100 — FULL-FLEET BATTLE PERFORMANCE
Scope: resolve the measured light-fleet worker lag; retain mixed-fleet comparison
and real firing/orders. Keep authoritative combat at 30 Hz with staggered
decisions and interpolated drawing. Measure before changing task sizes/caches.
Exit: existing light and mixed 300-point workloads satisfy the locked performance
gates in RETAIL DEVELOPMENT PLAN; no recurring catch-up, stale target authority,
planner deadlock or teardown error. Keep exact raw timings and hardware context.
Do not: lower the deployment promise, omit weapons, alter damage to shorten the
test or enable previously unstable Separate rendering as a shortcut.

0.101 — COMBAT AND MAP PRESENTATION
Before dispatch: list the blocking defects and select three bounded correction
families in order: physical hull/mount/motor contact, live combat readability,
then chart/harbor visibility. The 36-hull sheet is an inventory/consistency review;
one selected motion witness does not prove every hull, fit, LOD and damage state.
Keep unobserved combinations explicitly open; fix named defects, not all art.
Scope: one consistent normal-play view using the existing art systems. Fix the
identified hull/mount/weapon/motor silhouettes, contact points and LOD defects;
review all 36 existing hulls for consistency rather than adding another roster.
Make fire, hits, disabled machinery, flooding, retreat and sinking distinguishable.
Keep stern wakes attached to movement, bounded in length and cheap to draw.
Use existing recorded weapons and music; balance the mix instead of synthetic
ping cues. On the chart, preserve visible boats, island relief, harbors and
exploration landmarks at useful zoom; reduce panel obstruction and label clashes.
Exit: a current normal-zoom contact sheet, chart/harbor views and a short real
encounter show a light flank, a heavy firing role, escorting and withdrawal.
Issued orders, firing arcs and hit effects agree with actual actions. Distinguish
small, medium and large hulls at ordinary play size. No detached weapons, fake
under-hull reflections, old marker-only substitutions or obscured primary action.
Performance gates from 099/100 remain valid for changed rendering/effect paths.
Do not: introduce fluid simulation, walking crews, a new renderer or universal AI.

0.102 — FIRST VOYAGE AND FLEET GROWTH
Scope: connect the existing guidance, route quotes, freight/survey work, return
reserve and fitting workshop into one ordinary opening. Offer two affordable,
discoverable choices. Explain wages, tank fuel versus loose cargo fuel, repairs,
hold capacity and what the next hull/refit actually improves. Distinguish paid
escort contracts from assistance that awards service credit.
Exit: an unmodified start can prepare, do useful work, return, replenish, pay
the crew, buy an earned improvement and save/continue. A merchant transaction
sells to an actual funded buyer; unsold cargo value is not profit. Low-cash and
unavailable-job messages give a usable next action. Reference saves come from
real prior actions, never grants or test-only relocation.
Do not: make surveying an invisible subsidy for every career or add a tutorial
campaign separate from the ordinary game. First-hour timing is a design target,
not permission for an hour-long automated test.

0.103 — CIVILIAN WORK THAT PAYS
Scope: finish the existing home-water fishing circuit, a funded passenger
charter, and one freight/fuel delivery route. Reuse common departure/settlement
UI and actual civilian hull acquisition. This is operating-cost and opportunity
polish, not a new household or production model.
Exit: reference owners can fund a second outing after wages, food, fuel, fees
and replenishment. Record real catch quotas, buyer cash, berths, passenger
transfers and delivery custody. Passenger savings/escrow are not player income;
carried fuel is not conjured or counted twice. Show both successful settlement
and a refusal without enough stock, buyers, capacity or money.
Do not: promise fishing in every group, guarantee every risky trip profits or
grant a transport merely to make the career demonstration pass.

0.104 — CONTESTED JOBS AND PRIVATEERING
Scope: finish two existing combat-job contexts: a paid loaded convoy meeting a
real hostile fleet, and an earned commissioned privateer sortie. Fix the actual
opportunity/discovery/rendezvous defect, if reproduced, in existing campaign AI.
Show dated sightings, lawful targets and actual fee/commission terms. Pirates,
patrols and conflicting factions must continue to act while the player travels.
Exit: ordinary traffic can create the encounter; physical cargo and survivors
reach an exit or withdraw, outcomes settle once, and costs/repairs carry home.
For privateering, locate eligible hostile cargo, win or withdraw normally, return
and sell the captured remainder with loser debit and save/restore intact. The
successful reference can afford another sortie; withdrawal preserves real losses.
No observed target remains an OPEN opportunity gate, not a staged victory.
Do not: create an ambush every voyage, spawn fake prizes, grant warships, expose
omniscient cargo, sell operating reserves twice or rewrite global faction AI.

0.105 — RELIABLE PROGRESS AND CONTROLS
Scope: protect the last good save with a checked temporary write/commit and
reachable backup recovery; preserve current detached restore validation. Cover
updates, failed writes, incompatible saves, ordinary quit/Continue, defeat,
withdrawal and funded rescue. Finish existing title/settings/Esc/help flows,
volume controls, window sizes and readable key actions; do not add a menu system.
Exit: copied retained saves and the earned cases survive save/reload/update
without changing coins, cargo, people, boat IDs, jobs, geography or knowledge.
Name supported versions before dispatch: include retained v0.095 earned and
v0.098 controlled cases, plus the oldest supported pre-atlas copy actually found
in the inventory. Record originals/migrated hashes and migration-twice stability;
include damage, fitted/locker gear and tank-versus-cargo fuel. No unnamed legacy
version receives a compatibility promise merely because these copies pass.
Bad input leaves good bytes and live state intact. A defeated captain gets an
honest recovery or load/new-voyage choice. A refused action does not spend money.
Review common screens at 1280x800 and ordinary desktop sizes/scaling; primary
actions, prices and close/pause/quit controls remain reachable.
Do not: reset old worlds, weaken conservation, overwrite owner data or advertise
blanket compatibility with versions that were never checked.

0.106 — A COMPLETE EXPLORATION VOYAGE
Scope: finish one existing connected island story across two groups, using
surveys, a lead, a real salvage/work choice, a faction decision and a paid return.
Make geography, individual harbor ownership, standing and access reasons legible.
Connect rewards and consequences to existing stocks, people, jobs and equipment.
Reconcile all six supported career paths using the completed reference loops;
change only a demonstrated blocking quote, payout or availability error.
Exit: an earned expedition can depart with reserves, find a useful lead, choose,
return and improve the fleet; knowledge and consequences survive a remote save.
The player can explain why one harbor allows/refuses access and what changed.
Explorer, merchant, escort, privateer, local fisher and passenger operator each
have discoverable work, real costs and an affordable continuation. Rights and
buyer-support inventories started in parallel are complete enough for 107.
Do not: add 48 bespoke stories, another diplomacy model, continents or claim
that the controlled v0.098 harbor operation was starter-earned conquest.

DEVELOPMENT RULES
These rows are the plan; implementation has not started. Each future brief pins
files and one result. The same merge updates CODE MAP.md, in-game DEVELOPER
NOTES.txt and CHANGES v<version>.md, with actual checks/limits. No giant polish
pass, feature creep or duplicate test framework. Independent review checks the
merged result, not a helper's confidence. A failed leaf gets a bounded correction.
Root owns the commercial asset register from the start of development. Resolve
the exact 18 Lyria-output terms/account evidence before locking their retail use
at 101; otherwise plan an omission/replacement preserving owner originals. Check
the complete final payload/notices at 107. Provenance alone does not clear music.

Follow the current central GAME TESTING RULES.md: one selected pass, ONE batch,
ONE engine, self-quit plus identity-owned watchdog, <=60 seconds TOTAL, then
stop. Shared lane/FIFO behind other games; muted private background sessions,
no input/focus control. Queue wait is separate. No chained short runs to evade
the cap. A required unproved claim remains OPEN. No tests ran for this plan.
