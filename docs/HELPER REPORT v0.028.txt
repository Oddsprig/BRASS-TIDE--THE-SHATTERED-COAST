v0.028 campaign guidance — inline candidate

Deliverables:
game/scripts/campaign_guidance.gd
game/tests/test_campaign_guidance.gd

Public static APIs:
report(c, route_distance=0.0, career="captain") -> Dictionary
careers() -> Array

Guidance performs no purchases, transfers, assignments, canonicalization,
save writes or history updates. Existing Expedition.report supplies
capacity, actual crew wages, stores and trip/return estimates.
Deployment supplies actual costs and the 300-point battle split.

Report fields:
ok, day, career, routeDistance, routeValid, notes, fleet,
route_trip_returnbudget, cards, warnings, warningCount.

Cards have id, title, reason, actionId and urgent. Cards and displayed
warnings are capped at eight. Hull readiness, undercrew, recorded crew
arrears, overload, stores and return payroll precede opportunities and
career suggestions. Five careers each provide two suggestions.

Owned hulls are deduplicated by UID across the player fleet and owners
linked by shippingCompanies.ownerId with playerCompany=true.
Stored, support and sunk counts are subsets of owned hulls; they can
overlap. Company and player hull counts partition the unique owned total.
Deployment reserves do not create additional cargo capacity.

Company guidance reads recorded owner cash, crew purse, route, book
status, recall and netProfit. Invested capital remains distinct from
operating profit. Other opportunities inspect recorded training,
reserves, tooling status, business labor/cash, law and inactive defenses.
Unknown regionalCrises/jobs schemas are omitted for root's .027 adapter.
Fire detection accepts a present numeric or boolean boat.fire field;
unknown fire schemas are omitted.

Invalid, negative or excessively large route distances explicitly use
zero travel distance and retain the expedition reserve estimate.
Invalid careers fall back to Captain with an explanation.
Numeric display is finite and capped; nonfinite estimates become null.
Wage estimates round upward to integer coins. Material stores come from
Expedition; guidance invents no material prices, hull prices or profits.

Tests instantiate actual Campaign/world JSON and compare actual
Expedition/Deployment results. They cover read-only snapshots, invalid
inputs, returned-data independence, careers, company accounting wording,
stored/support/company hulls, fitted spec.cargo, numerical crew, damage,
fire/water, arrears, supply/payroll shortages and overload.

Edited fixtures are read-only test inputs, not accounting transactions.
Money.total is used only on the pristine Campaign fixture.

No files edited, tools invoked, engine run or native acceptance claimed.
Root must execute the test, confirm optional field adapters, register it
with serialized checks, and complete final balance/performance/UI review.
