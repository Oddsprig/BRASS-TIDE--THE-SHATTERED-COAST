BRASS TIDE v0.061 - A ROUTE WORTH KEEPING

Open Your place on the coast and select a regional goal. The consequence view
connects it to dated local sightings, stocks recorded during harbor visits,
legal trade estimates and your actual shore-business books. Unseen ports stay
unseen; old sightings and prices are dated. A trade estimate reserves no cargo,
buyer funds or profit. Sail, observe and check the account before committing.

A corridor needs seven consecutive clear economic days and real use of its
route. An existing patrol can receive a once-only treasury contribution when
the sponsor, local threat and legal standing justify it. That money enters the
patrol's actual purse; fuel, rounds, provisions, wages and repairs still cost
resources. Shortages and recovery take priority over another patrol leg.
New NPC fleets set aside part of their existing operating capital for their first
paid crew provisions. Food marked for sale remains commercial cargo; it does
not count as the crew's working ration reserve.

Completing the achievement does not secure the sea forever. At an original
corridor harbor with legal access, choose Continue paid watch to keep the same
patrol and its reserves assigned. Withdraw regional watch releases that
assignment; the existing boats still travel, pay people and receive paid service.
Repeating the choice gives no second fleet, treasury contribution or achievement.
Later hostiles and exhausted reserves can change the situation.

For harbor development, distinct sold/settled deliveries and separate unpaid
donations add together: six sold plus six donated lots make twelve. Your local
business must also record three actual fully paid working days. A warehouse,
salvage yard or workshop uses real setup materials, hired people and its own
operating purse. Capital deposits are separate from revenue. Wage debt payments
or overtime amounts do not manufacture additional working days; rest, stoppages
and incomplete payroll do not qualify. Legacy wage totals prove no historical
work days, so an old unfinished goal starts recording from a new baseline.

Home harbors shows payroll shortfalls, labor conditions, stored materials,
capacity, capital, revenue and expenses separately. A workshop's missing recipe
inputs can stop output even while paid staff work; a warehouse has no production
recipe. Resolve the stated shortage using owned goods, capital and existing paid
staffing or negotiation. Nothing is supplied automatically.

Opening, reopening, declining or closing these review screens pays nothing and
leaves pending calendar payroll pending. An explicit action first publishes the
due account, then rechecks the actual harbor, boat/business, access and amount.

This scope connects existing systems. It adds no nation-management framework or
permanent safety bonus. The controlled native voyage demonstrates composition;
it does not establish naturally seeded traffic balance, a profitable complete
career or Starsector parity. Whole-game quality and strict performance stay open.
