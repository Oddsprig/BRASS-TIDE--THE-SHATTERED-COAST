ROUTING
event_layers stays pure and returns at most two {sound, gain, pitch} layers.
Gains below are player dB before world attenuation and bus volume.
The loudest layer is -14 dB. Nothing in the table is hotter than -14.

- MG fire: gun -22 at pitch 1.00. One cut is already a burst.
- MG hit: wood or metal contact -23 at 1.06. Armor, metal and engine contacts use metal.
- MG miss: water splash -28 at 1.08, or wood -28 at 1.02.
- Cannon fire: cannon -15 at 0.88. The deep report.
- Cannon hit: contact -18 at 0.84, plus a cannon body. Body is -22.5, or -19 when the event reports a breach.
- Cannon miss: splash or wood -26 at 0.92.
- Rocket fire: rocket -18 at 1.06. Launch only.
- Rocket hit: brighter cannon blast -15.5 at 1.16, plus a quiet contact -23.
- Rocket miss: splash or wood -26 at 0.98.
- Torpedo fire: splash -21 at 0.90. The launch.
- Torpedo hit: louder lower splash -15.5 at 0.80, plus a low cannon body -23.5 at 0.68.
- Torpedo miss: splash or wood -26 at 0.82.
- Engine trouble: metal -18. Pitch 0.76, or 0.84 when reason is fire. This is not the looping bed.
- Sink: splash -14 plus wood. Hull loss is splash 0.70 / wood -20.5 at 0.74.
  Flooding (reason flooding) is darker: splash 0.66 / wood -22 at 0.72.
  Fire (incendiary or reason fire) lifts the hull layer: splash 0.76 / wood -19 at 0.90.
Heavy variant still multiplies pitch by 0.88. Sequence still adds the existing
0.97..1.03 variation. Final pitch is clamped to 0.65..1.25.

WHY THE FAMILIES STAY DISTINCT
MG stays on the short gun cuts and sits about 7 dB under a cannon report.
Cannon report, rocket launch and rocket blast do not share a pitch.
Torpedo launch, torpedo impact and sinking use the same splash bank at different
pitch and level, and only the impact adds the low cannon body.
Sinking always keeps a wood hull layer. Engine trouble stays a low metal knock,
separate from an MG hit on metal.

ADMISSION
Unchanged bounds: 32 voices, 12 admissions per consume including accent layers,
128 rapid-source keys, gun interval 0.16 seconds, cull at 2200 world units.
Primaries are scheduled before accents, so a crowded torpedo salvo keeps splash
attacks and drops the dry body first.
Routine MG fire, hit and miss each collapse on their own key: source, family
and kind. One cue per 0.16s on that key. Fire does not take the contact slot.
A reported breach, engine hit or killing blow is not collapsed.
Cannon, rocket and torpedo reports and hits are never collapsed.
Their misses hold 0.40s per source, family and kind so splash spam cannot
take the 12 slots.
Voices admitted later in a full consume are trimmed by 0.45 dB per voice already
playing, capped at 5 dB. Higher-priority events are admitted first, so they keep
their level and routine overlaps give up the headroom.

PRIORITY AT THE SAME DISTANCE
Sink, breach or killing hit, heavy hit, engine trouble, heavy report,
MG breach, MG hit, MG burst, heavy miss, MG miss.
Close range (inside 900 world units) adds up to +4 for hits and engine trouble
and +2 for sinking. Routine MG bursts and misses lose priority faster with distance.
An equal-distance sink stays above a breach. A point-blank heavy hit stays above
a sink near the 2200-unit edge.

DISTANCE, ZOOM, MUTE, HEADLESS
attenuation_db uses world distance only. It is 0 at the listener, strictly
decreasing out to 2200, and never a boost. Players keep attenuation 0 and a
non-culling max distance, so Godot's own falloff cannot tie loudness to zoom.
Pan still uses the chart's projected position.
enabled false still counts the sequence and schedules nothing, including no rapid
reservation. Headless still refuses play() on weapon voices and the engine bed.
BrassWeapons and BrassAmbience stay separate. Zero weapon volume still uses the
existing silent bus floor. The existing limiter class is kept, and only on an
empty named game bus. Weapons ceiling -1.5 dB and
threshold -6 dB. Ambience ceiling -1.5 dB and threshold -12 dB. soft_clip_db is
3. soft_clip_ratio is assigned 6; current Godot documents that ratio as having
no effect on this deprecated limiter, so the ceiling, the threshold, the lower
layer gains and the stack trim are the actual headroom.
