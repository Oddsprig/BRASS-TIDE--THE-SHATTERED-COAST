BRASS TIDE v0.057 - WREN'S MIDDLE SEA EXPEDITION

Meet Sister Wren at Reedwater Floats through Coastal stories & expedition leads.
Accept The Middle Sea Chart. Her clue sights the first bearing; the journal
keeps earned destinations and later explains the consequences of your choice.

Before sailing, open the work board at Reedwater. An eligible existing funded
survey prefers the accepted expedition objective. It still needs real treasury
funds, room among your commitments and a full level-3 survey. Reading the rock's
story clue only needs level 2: paid survey work asks more and the panel says so.
Payment remains in escrow until the actual return report; it cannot repeat.

Prepare the selected island with enough spare able sailors above every boat's
minimum. A base chart needs two spare hands, one day and one kit. A base full
survey needs four spare hands, 2.5 days and two kits. Fitted survey bonuses can
change the quoted work time. Buy and hire through the harbor's actual services.
For example, the verified Tinwake voyage hired two additional sailors, reaching
seven crew/four spare hands. Seven sailors cost 700 coins for each paid day.

The island preparation panel names a return harbor and separates sailing,
remaining shore work and contingency. It shows missing crew, food, working
fuel, kits, hold space and payroll coins. Accepted matching survey work uses
its reporting harbor. Without that commitment, the default is a nearby legal
harbor with an actual water route: read the name before committing.

Sail to the landing, stop the engines and assign the party. Wages continue for
the fleet while hands work ashore. Shore rations are prepaid from the same
normal food allowance; they are not charged twice. Idle survey days consume
no sailing fuel. Recall the party before leaving; spent tools remain spent.

After charting the rock, decide whether to share the bearing or keep it secret.
Follow the earned clue to Shatter Wreck Field, chart and survey it, then mark
the next bearing. The finite wreck can be investigated before you return to
register rights. Actual hostiles, capacity, hands, kits and work still matter;
marking a wreck is not a free victory or a free new boat.

Compare returning now with the optional Rust anchorage detour. Extra travel,
shore work and payroll are shown together. Return to Reedwater to report the
funded survey and settle your public/private claim. Public rights allow nearby
Commune salvors to work the field's remaining real stock; private rights keep
that public work closed and change the available paid charter conditions.
Neither choice removes cargo you already recovered. A charter grants access
to an actual paid business, not a staffed yard or renewable free salvage.

The journal retains earned leads and completed outcomes. Every later recovery,
charter, repair and operating voyage still needs its own real people, materials
and coins. Exact focused verification and actual exported-view evidence are in
tests/. Broad art, balance, performance and whole-game quality remain open on
the saved roadmap; this guide does not claim Starsector parity.
