BRASS TIDE v0.044 - COMBAT COMMITMENT AND RECOVERY
26 October2026 |36boat designs

THIS VERSION
Light skiffs retain their response. Larger boats build/shed velocity more slowly
and carry momentum through turns. Live movement and AI forecasts share the same
math; bow turn limits, top speeds, existing damage and fuel costs are unchanged.

Size3 heavy cannons have a1.8second base reload at full efficiency. Cannons on
the large primary forward socket have135degrees of traverse. Smaller weapons,
rear/side mounts and launchers retain their rules. Runtime, fitting chooser,
physical mounts and art use the shared values. Crew and equipment damage can
lengthen the actual reload; no ammunition or repair resources are invented.

The selected-boat HUD reports actual reload, water trend, engine/fuel and repair
hands/kits. Loaded means only the timer expired: aim/range/clear-shot rules still
apply. Crew-priority buttons act on the FLAGSHIP. No new pressure meter.

PRACTICE
Main menu -> Fleet battle laboratory -> Preset:
  Practice: torpedo crossing (two boats)
  Practice: cover a breakaway (four boats)
Both use normal canonical fittings, finite supplies and ordinary battle rules.
They provide guidance, not forced damage, rewards, free repairs or scripted wins.
Autopilot starts ON; Space pauses; WASD camera; right-click selected orders.
U toggles manual flagship helm with arrows/mouse. Escape opens the menu.
