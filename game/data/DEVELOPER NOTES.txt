THIS VERSION
- Connect a funded generated-group shipping corridor to existing merchant, pirate and patrol objectives.
- Resolve independent hostile NPC encounters through actual cargo, stores, damage and relations.
- Keep recovery, repairs and replacement vessels tied to physical yards and real accounts, labor, materials and crews.

This is one scoped playable step. Later roadmap features and general tuning
remain in their own versions. Coins and100 coins per sailor-day remain.
Crew remain numerical/fixed asset details; water remains inexpensive.
The portable tests/ directory contains this build's actual focused verification
and startup receipts. Headless checks do not establish rendered/audio quality.

DEVELOPER TOOLS
Campaign.trace_steps075 is off by default. When enabled by a private developer
scenario, step_costs075 records the last real campaign substep in microseconds:
ocean staging, continuous work, naval/patrol decisions and both after-step calls.
It never changes simulation order or enters a saved career. Preparation and
continuation fixtures preserve real state and trace files with SHA256 bindings.
