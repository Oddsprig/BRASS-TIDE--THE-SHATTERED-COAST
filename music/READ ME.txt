BRASS TIDE MUSIC LIBRARY

The two Brass and Salt tracks were supplied by the game owner from Lyria.
Original files are preserved. No music was generated or purchased by the build agent.

Add MP3, OGG Vorbis or PCM WAV tracks to this folder, or Desktop/Brass Tide Music.
The game scans for additions every ten seconds; Options also offers Reload and Next.
Optional subfolders: title, travel, harbor, contact, combat, finale, victory, loss, all.
Known cue names from the supplied prompts are assigned automatically. Other names
join the general playlist. Keep short victory/loss stingers in their own folders.

Music is optional. The game remains portable with this music folder beside the EXE;
it does not need the original Downloads/Desktop files to play the included tracks.
