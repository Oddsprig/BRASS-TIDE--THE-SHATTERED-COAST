EXISTING API / SCHEMA (paths below are relative to game/scripts/)
1. fleet_identity.gd:92 ensure(state), :129 ensure_boat(boat), :144 roles(),
   :153 effective(boat, fitted_spec)->Dictionary, :302 hire_specialist(core,
   uid, role)->Dictionary, :373 tick(core, days=1.0)->Array, :416
   record_service(core, boat_uids:Array, result:String)->void.
   state.specialists[] records id, boatUid, role, name, coins, arrears,
   premiumPerDay, active, hiredDay, portId, discharged/lost. No separate player
   captain/officer roster. Roles: pilot +25/day, gunner +50, chief_engineer
   +100, surveyor +75, quartermaster +25 OVER the 100-coin sailor base.
   A specialist adds ONE real sailor/berth, consumes harbor recruit/workforce
   and specialist availability, and pays Economy.recruit_cost(port,1): a
   1000..1200-coin advance into crewPurse. No free promotion of an existing hand.
   Hiring needs qualified.sailor when a training book exists; gunner/mechanic
   eligibility is consumed if present, otherwise it falls back to sailor.
   Do not describe those professional courses as mandatory in the current UI.
   Paid flags live in boat.specialistActive. Pilot: speed x1.03, turn x1.04;
   gunner: fitted mount fireRate x1.06; engineer: repairFactor +0.25, pumps
   x1.10; surveyor: surveyFactor +0.30; quartermaster: upkeepFactor 0.95
   (rations, NOT a wage discount). effective() rebuilds a detached fitted spec.
   tick floors days, settles premium arrears, and suspends benefits for unpaid
   premium/base arrears. Campaign.apply_economy calls it once per paid day.
2. fleet_support.gd:8 aboard(c,uid), :18 reconcile(c), :32 dismiss(c,id),
   :65 premium(state,arrears=false). Discharge settles arrears, returns a real
   person/qualification and savings ashore. Reconcile marks lost specialists
   when no living berth remains and transfers their premium savings to their
   home estate. It uses numerical survivors; there is no named wound simulation.

   Qualifications are subsets of existing people, NOT extra population or

   instructor, consume food/supplies, and escrow trainee/instructor wages.

   training_ops.gd exposes enroll(c,count,role="sailor") / cancel(c,id).
   quote/report call ensure and can normalize input: use detached review data.
