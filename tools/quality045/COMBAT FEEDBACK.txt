Concrete changes
- Existing real CombatEvents still create all shots, contacts, breaches and deaths.
  No damage, RNG, simulation, thread, custody or equipment accounting change.
- Armor opening uses the event's actual breach flag. A short dark opening replaces
  the reflected streak; no numerical hole or new scar record is created by art.
- Torpedo launch/impact remains cool water, foam and fragments without the orange
  rocket fireball. Rocket impacts retain the short dry flash and fragment profile.
- Sink-event flame/smoke and wreck flame/smoke require recorded incendiary/onFire.
  Cold hull losses settle with foam/debris; cold flooding keeps its water surge.
  Wreck bubbles finish fading after 6.3 seconds; existing faint debris remains.
  Listing, settling, submergence and the 2.2-second simulation duration are retained.
- Hull impact glints use the real material. Engine smoke reads the live installed
  engine hp before the already-existing scalar projection, with a legacy fallback.
  Existing damage/disable symbols still read installed records at real mount,
  engine and bilge anchors each draw; no condition cache is added.
- Torpedo hit primary is the existing splash bank, with a quieter lower cannon
  layer. Rocket hit primary is a sharper cannon cut plus a quiet material contact.
  Gun/cannon reports keep their own recordings; cannon contact is lower-pitched,
  with an extra subdued thump only on a reported breach. Heavy pitch differences
  and rotating recorded cuts remain bounded; no generated pings/tones are added.
- Reports/impacts precede all accents. Accents share the same 12-admission budget.
  Significant reported breaches/engine damage gain priority below sinking at an
  equal camera distance. Camera attenuation now falls continuously and softens
  toward the existing 2200-unit cull, independent of display zoom.
