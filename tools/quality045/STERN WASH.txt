New static API:
  draw(canvas, projector: Callable, samples: Array, opts: Dictionary = {}) -> int
Canvas requires only draw_colored_polygon(PackedVector2Array, Color).
Projector takes (world Vector2, height float) and returns screen Vector2.

BOUNDS AND BEHAVIOR
At most 12 live samples per boat, 64 boats (existing fleet cap).
Renderer reads at most the newest 26 input entries, retains at most 12,
submits at most 3 polygons, each at most 23 vertices: <=69 projections.
Previous drawing used up to 12 polygons and 96 projected vertices while
Chart retained 26 samples and 6s of history per boat. These are source bounds,
not measured timings or FPS. Actual candidate cost remains unmeasured.
Width input clamps to 12 world units; outer half-width <=0.62 of that.
Length <=min(96 world units, newest input half-width *10).
Actual width also shrinks for short travel, age and tight bend radius.
No path extension ahead of the newest stern or behind the oldest retained
point. Teleports, reversal cusps, invalid/expired entries and >135deg of
accumulated turning end the suffix instead of bridging/folding the ribbon.
This deliberately drops older forward wash at a reversal; it is no fluid
simulation and does not attempt propeller jets when stationary.
Chart lowers the velocity cutoff from10 to0.5 units/s, then requires actual
center/stern displacement before emission. Low-speed wash is narrow because
its width is limited by traveled distance. No refresh/drift at rest;
remaining geometry loses width/opacity and all history expires within0.7s.
Numerical crews, stores, saves, simulation and worker scheduling are unchanged.
