STYLE AND CONTRACT
Three connected translucent tapered filled strips: muted turquoise wash,
pale churn and a narrow cream center. No spaced bubbles, repeated dashes,
V rails, second hull silhouette, reflection or shadow. Tail widths taper
to one point; all strip centers follow the recorded world stern path.
BoatArt.stern_of remains the physical transom/drive authority.

Root integration: replace BoatArt.draw_wake's body with this delegation:
  return preload("res://scripts/wake_art045.gd").draw(canvas, projector, samples, opts)
Keep its public signature. No other BoatArt change is in this candidate.

New static API:
  draw(canvas, projector: Callable, samples: Array, opts: Dictionary = {}) -> int
Canvas requires only draw_colored_polygon(PackedVector2Array, Color).
Projector takes (world Vector2, height float) and returns screen Vector2.
Every wake point uses height 0.0, matching Chart's water projection.
Returned int counts actual submitted polygons, including viewport rejection.
No Nodes/resources are created and no gameplay data is mutated by the helper.
Call on the main thread with the existing CanvasItem drawing phase.

Samples stay oldest-to-newest dictionaries {p: Vector2, age: float, w: float}.
Legacy d may be present but does not orient drawing. Chart's d now records
actual stern travel, including reverse. Its internal c stores the last
emission's boat center to reject rotation-only/stale-velocity churn.
Chart continues using presentation clock timestamps t internally.
opts.life can shorten the lifetime, but cannot lengthen it beyond 0.7s.
opts.clip_rect is a screen Rect2 for conservative rejection of wholly
offscreen strips. Partial geometry is left to CanvasItem/viewport cropping;
it is not CPU clipped. Chart considers the full path bounds, not only its
latest stern, so a visible tail survives when the head leaves the viewport.
Existing wake update/draw/path/sample/primitive profiling counters remain.
Chart's historical WAKE_LIFE_SEC=6 constant is untouched outside the owned
regions; actual recording and drawing clamp to the new 0.7s authority.

BOUNDS AND BEHAVIOR
At most 12 live samples per boat, 64 boats (existing fleet cap).
Renderer reads at most the newest 26 input entries, retains at most 12,
submits at most 3 polygons, each at most 23 vertices: <=69 projections.
Previous drawing used up to 12 polygons and 96 projected vertices while
Chart retained 26 samples and 6s of history per boat. These are source bounds,
not measured timings or FPS. Actual candidate cost remains unmeasured.
Width input clamps to 12 world units; outer half-width <=0.62 of that.
Length <=min(96 world units, newest input half-width *10).
Actual width also shrinks for short travel, age and tight bend radius.
No path extension ahead of the newest stern or behind the oldest retained
point. Teleports, reversal cusps, invalid/expired entries and >135deg of
accumulated turning end the suffix instead of bridging/folding the ribbon.
This deliberately drops older forward wash at a reversal; it is no fluid
simulation and does not attempt propeller jets when stationary.
Chart lowers the velocity cutoff from10 to0.5 units/s, then requires actual
center/stern displacement before emission. Low-speed wash is narrow because
its width is limited by traveled distance. No refresh/drift at rest;
remaining geometry loses width/opacity and all history expires within0.7s.
Numerical crews, stores, saves, simulation and worker scheduling are unchanged.
