CONCRETE BEHAVIOR
- Cannon-primary large/cruiser hulls infer heavy despite secondary fixed tubes.
  A main-tube fit or explicit striker role still selects a strike boat. Explicit
  escort always screens its named ally. Light role uses fitted nominal speed;
  live damage/speed/turn still govern travel and survival, without role jitter.
- Strike distance comes from the usable principal launcher, not a longer gun.
  Its fitted angle/range drive staging; actual per-mount/legacy reload timers
  distinguish staging from launch-window. Time alone never reports a salvo.
  Existing real shot acknowledgements/spent pass identity still cause turn-away.
- Light orbit direction stays tied to existing formation index, not sector parity.
  Wider range-band stations and short bounded bends offer an approach around the
  observed target. Existing actual burst, breakaway, regroup and failed pursuit
  memory remain; no fabricated damage, hits or new readiness meter.
- Heavies stop advancing once inside a useful fitted range band, keep stationary
  pass points and let the existing helm bring working arcs to bear. Heavy/screen
  holds no longer expire merely because an18second pursuit clock has elapsed.
  A loaded/aligned/unspent secondary fixed tube can request launch-window while
  KEEPING heavy station geometry. Each tube retains the existing one-shot pass
  lease and actual acknowledgement. Holding never increments passSequence or
  rearms a tube; a real retreat/regroup+separation or new explicit attack is still
  required. Guns retain their continuous gates. Runtime confirmed this seam.
- Screens score observed closing danger to the real protected ally, lead an
  intercept modestly and keep its leash. Formation remains for clear transit;
  front and lateral alternatives preserve room around the protected hull.
  Unnamed protection can move to a newly wounded ally with160score hysteresis.
  Explicit escort/protectedAllyId cannot silently switch to another hull.
- Support prefers an armed protector (especially its actual assigned escort),
  discounts a failing protector and asks for a rear position outside its hull.
  Friendly HP/flood/guns and observed hostile arcs are the only inputs here.
- Severe live mobility loss under focused fire, flood/fire/HP danger, exhausted
  stores or an autonomous close target envelope cause an explained local retreat.
  Existing conditional-withdrawal and explicit order rules still apply.
