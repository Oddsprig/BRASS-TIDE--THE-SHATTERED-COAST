Source-informed startup order
Campaign.new_game creates ports, then calls Fleets.create_owners. That constructor
allocates actual crews from the home workforce and transfers capped opening
capital from the existing home cash (trader) or governmentPurse (other NPCs).
Campaign subsequently calls Fleets.dock at each NPC's home before clearing atPort
and initializing NavalAI orders. In dock, crew-paid food purchase precedes boat
stores/repairs and the final commercial load. Initial orders/readiness are
therefore downstream of the existing real dock preparation.

Fix and bounds
Within create_owners only, move min(actual opening capital, ceil(three days of
actual crew food use) * current home market food buy price) from owner.coins to
owner.crewPurse. Whole ration lots use the same ceiling as dock's existing food
request; using the actual buy price avoids treating Goods.cost as a market quote.
The transfer is logged. No extra sponsoring transfer or capital request occurs.
Existing dock/_purchase/Economy.transact then buy actual rations from finite
market stock, with normal law, stock/reserve, hold capacity and affordability
checks. Unspent savings stay in crewPurse; there is no recurring top-up or new
financing policy. This allocation is opening crew savings, not a payroll event:
wageArrears, wageRemainder, lastPayroll and all future wages remain unchanged.

Dock now subtracts commercial food for every owner whose tradeGood is food,
matching fleet_reserves and tick's existing own-food accounting. This allows an
ordinary loaded food merchant to buy its separate rations at a real dock. The
existing playerCompany branch already performed the same subtraction and keeps
its prior purchasing, market limits, ledgers and core-required behavior.

Conservation and save boundary
For opening capital C and crew allocation R, home purse delta is -C, owner.coins
delta is +(C-R), crewPurse delta is +R: combined coin delta is exactly zero.
A successful ration purchase of Q at price P subsequently moves P*Q from
crewPurse to port.cash and Q food from port.stocks to owner.cargo; tradeCargo
is not increased for rations. No coin, food, crew, hull or grant is created by
this fix. The original fourteen-wage-day plus three-day food/fuel requested
capital formula and cap are unchanged. The allocation is confined to the new
NPC constructor, not normalization, load/restore, daily ticks or generic dock:
old raw saves receive no opening savings or retroactive purse edits on load.
Their next ordinary paid dock visit uses the corrected food classification.
