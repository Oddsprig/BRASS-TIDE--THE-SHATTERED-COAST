DAY IDENTITY AND MINIMUM TWO COUNTERS

its business_step writes labor.lastProcessedEconomicDay for that same shift.
HomeOps.daily calls Home.tick(c,1), then Campaign.apply_economy calls
RegionalOps.daily, then increments state.econDay. Thus a saved joined receipt's
lastPaidWorkDay is <= published econDay, not a future day. No new worker, timer,
thread, queue, economy cadence, render mode or constructor is introduced.
paidWorkDays is needed because wages include overtime, debt and rest.
lastPaidWorkDay is needed because repeated same-day calls cannot be new days.
working is a dated last-shift result with current staffing/arrears/rest gates,
not an assertion that a new shift or future payroll has already happened.
Only the canonical one-day path earns a receipt; bulk wage calls do not synthesize
several shifts. No saved receipt is sanitized or backfilled by Home.ensure.
