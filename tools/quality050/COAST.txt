ACTUAL INPUT AND CHANGE
Campaign was already a top-down 10400x7200 chart with eight real islands and
ten ports. Previous NauticalArt settlements were a shared 3x4 roof grid;
battle land was expanded polygon shelves, radial bands and mountain fans.
This candidate replaces those active paint paths. The established chart
projection, harbor/fleet click centers, navigation, sea routes and markers stay
authoritative. No world.json, port coordinates, collision or economy edit.

Eight original bounds-relative terrain compositions follow the real island
IDs: Lantern chalk/orchards, Kelp tidal reeds, Sawtooth ironwood/crags,
Verdigris polder terraces, Cinder ash/quarries, Kettle Fen wooded levees,
Oilstone slate benches, Shatter basalt/heath. Actual source polygon points
are the land silhouette. Every beach/cliff/ridge/field/grove fill, roof,
shadow, road and landmark is intersected with that ring during preparation.
There are no decorative islands, opaque offshore rocks or false lagoon holes.
Approach ink is transparent, broken seabed hatching; it is navigable water,
not a new reef obstacle. Thin shore-to-berth ink replaces the old pier symbol
without adding filled blocking land. All coast mesh vertices have height zero;
ridge / cliff shading stays inside the collision silhouette in isometric view.

_ensure_chart_sheet prepares once at Chart.setup; it remains callable by the
existing fixture. _prepare_battle_coast guards on the actual battle islands
array identity in Chart.step before redraw, then prepares the translated rings
once per encounter. Empty ocean stays empty. No preparation happens in _draw,
draw_island or draw_harbor. No triangle clipping, triangulation, noise, random
tree copying, texture generation or material creation occurs in coast drawing.
Transforms derive from actual Chart.project and reset to identity after drawing.
Separate campaign and battle caches never substitute a campaign-index polygon
for encounter geometry. One current / last battle cache is retained, not history.

Three immutable ArrayMeshes per island / harbor, one selected per draw. Mesh
triangulation and storage use local coordinates to retain small roof features.
Per-LOD hard vertex ceilings: 2500 / 9000 / 18000, position Vector3 + RGBA.
At most three native paint calls per visible plan: mesh + outline + reef batch,
or harbor mesh + two pier strokes. Offshore stroke count <=20; roof pieces <=26.
Affine projected bounds cull whole plans; larger meshes are still GPU clipped.
These are source limits, not measured time/VRAM/FPS or proof of visual quality.
The existing CPU presentation/boat/wake/trail/effect profiling is preserved;
coast_draw_calls and coast_us are additive fields. counted_primitives retains
its existing boat/wake/trail/projectile/effect/wreck definition. No RuntimeSpy
or profiling wrapper is edited. battle_minimap_rect remains exactly the input.
