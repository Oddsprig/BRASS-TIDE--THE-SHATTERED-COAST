LOOP AND EXACT RULES
Commit a gun pass or a finite launcher salvo with an exit already chosen.
A large cannon must reload and face its firing lane before another discharge.
A light craft retains its speed/turn advantage for crossing or leaving that
lane. Existing fire, flooding, crew work, bailing/repair and finite supplies
can erase that advantage; regroup grants no health or rounds. Roles, orders,
staggering, physics, acceleration, turn rates and repair rules are unchanged.

combat_balance044.gd contains both shared bounds:
  size >= 3 cannon rate = min(authored rate, 1 / 1.8) shots/second.
  cannon on size >= 3 mount0 arc = min(socket arc, 0.75 * PI).
The second is the large forward socket's cannon traverse, including a small
cannon fitted there. It is not applied to guns, aft/side mounts or launchers.
An already slower cannon or narrower authored arc stays slower/narrower.
No hidden runtime arc multiplier is added. Refit.weapon_for_socket builds the
same detached rate/arc descriptor for effective_spec and BOTH fitting choosers.
Layout.weapon_aim and bare mounts_of share the bound; explicit fitted mount
arcs remain the actual supplied runtime/art data. Socket IDs/positions/angles,
muzzle distances/heights, hit strength, salvos and equipment identity remain.
Fitted BoatArt draws the accepted per-mount localAimAngles on those same socket
coordinates; BoatPortrait's existing sector reads the actual weapon.arc. No
art-only traverse modifier or new rendering behavior is introduced.

ACTUAL FORMULAS AND SCALE
EquipmentCombat conditions an enabled mount once:
  conditionedRate = clamp(baseRate * mountFactor, 0.01, 60).
Battle._fire then sets cooldown = 1 / (conditionedRate * max(0.15,weaponFactor)).
Zero/disabled equipment and inadequate stores still refuse to fire. Thus 1.8 s
is the healthy minimum, not a promise for a damaged, burning or busy crew.
Both choosers display actual shots/s, base reload seconds and the actual arc.
Crew/equipment effects lengthen the live timer; no second cooldown system exists.

Hull damage per hit = .35 * max(.22*D, D-.8*max(armor-penetration,0)).
Shell penetration remains4. Furnace versus Tin armor2 has damage16.1 per hit:
  continuous hull-only TTK = 150 / (.35*46*rate): 8.469791 -> 16.770186 s.
  ten perfect hits from an immediate first shot: 9/rate = 8.181818 -> 16.2 s.
Fitted Long iron versus Tin needs nine perfect hits: 8*1.8=14.4 s from the
first shot; continuous hull-only TTK16.071429 s. These estimates exclude
flooding/fire/crew/module damage, misses, repairs and maneuvering.

Unchanged maximum travel = range / projectileSpeed, before muzzle/inherited
velocity and moving-target interception: Furnace575/620=0.927419 s,
Long iron620/690=0.898551 s, Reed torpedo480/280=1.714286 s. Reed reload remains
1/.65=1.538462 s and costs2 rounds. Projectile origin is the rotated socket
plus the weapon's existing muzzle offset; tests inspect the real projectile.

Tin speed118/turn2.65 versus Furnace72/1.3 remain. Ideal PI turns take
1.185507 s versus2.416610 s. An ideal turn-then-straight escape from340 beyond
575 against a same-direction chase is PI/2.65+(575-340)/(118-72)=6.294202 s.
Real eased acceleration, steering and heavy tracking differ. At25% flooding,
the existing Tin speed factor gives94.99, below dry Ash102: leave early.
A fixed-heading crossing demonstrates geometry only; it does not prove a
turning AI heavy cannot track or hit a light boat.

This source fixture is written to check all stock and fitted descriptors against
chooser/runtime/layout/art, exact unchanged hull/economic inputs, a real paid
canonical heavy installation with finite stores/coin/material conservation,
and stock/fitted live fire kernels. The kernels inspect socket/muzzle/speed,
travel tick and real cooldown, move a light from rest with the real integrator
across a declared fixed heavy heading during reload, reject an out-of-arc shot,
and exhaust two real discharges without a third shot or free rounds.
One duel of up to ten simulation seconds at normal60Hz uses Tin/light_torpedo and
stock Furnace, seed4401, 330-unit opening, four starting rounds each, default
autopilot, and exactly one player move order after the first actual torpedo
launch. Enemy AI remains unchanged. Real events, reload intervals, movements,
finite ammunition, final damage/water/stores and departure time are recorded.
Whether the resulting pass/exit feels deliberate remains a Root play verdict.
