HOW TO LAUNCH
Main menu -> Fleet battle laboratory -> Preset -> choose either practice label
-> Launch fleet engagement. The existing Lab.presets/preset/create and dynamic
LabUI picker consume the JSON; no new API or scenario controller is needed.
Both fleets start on autopilot, use the normal closing formation and obey the
300 DP cap. Space pauses, Tab opens tactical orders, WASD moves the camera.
U toggles manual flagship helm (arrows and mouse). Practice aims are guidance;
these remain ordinary fleet battles with ordinary victory/defeat rules.

pressure_crossing / Practice: torpedo crossing
  Player: Tinwake, light_torpedo in mount0, empty aft mount; stock engine/armor.
  Enemy: Furnace, heavy_cannon in mount0, three empty mounts; stock engine/armor.
  Fitted points: 17 vs 72. Two boats, one launcher against one heavy cannon.
  Watch the cannon shot, cross/line up the fixed tube, launch and exit while
  it reloads. Practice leaving before water/engine damage removes the speed
  advantage. A kill is not the practice aim and is not awarded separately.

pressure_breakaway / Practice: cover a breakaway
  Player: same Tinwake + Ashwake with stock_primary/rapid_gun/empty, stock
  engine/armor. Enemy: same Furnace + stock Bilge skiff. Points: 56 vs 86.
  Four boats. Ashwake's cannon and aft gun cover the faster Bilge pursuer while
  Tinwake breaks away. Select Ashwake and right-click Tinwake to order escort;
  move Tinwake clear with orders or manual helm. Tinwake starts as flagship,
  so its existing Bail water / Patch & repair buttons control its crew.
  If hit, stabilize water, patch with carried kits, check weapons/ammunition,
  and decide whether another pass is affordable. Crew work competes with guns.
  No starting flood, scripted hit, guaranteed recovery or forced win is added.

NORMAL CONSTRUCTION AND INTEGRATION
Refit.normalize validates every loadout; Lab.create uses Damage.create_ship,
Records.initialize and Refit.ensure before Battle.create. Normal stores are
80 rounds per fitted weapon, 120 fuel and 20 repair kits per boat, with actual
optimal crew, full canonical HP and no locker spares. No career is initialized
or billed by Lab.create. No hull/stat buffs, role overrides, cargo, events or
new meters. Boats remain independently owned canonical equipment containers.
Runtime044's WEAPON COMMITMENT.txt owns cannon reload/arc (proposed 1.8 s base,
135-degree heavy forward arc); Render044's INERTIA AND RECOVERY.txt owns hull
response. Presets copy neither timing nor inertia values, and promise no exit
window: actual crew efficiency, damage, course and pursuit still matter.
